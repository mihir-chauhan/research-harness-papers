\textbf{Data.} Inputs are eight i.i.d.\ uniform digits. Pretraining uses 20,000 fixed sequences per seed (seed $1000+s$); adaptation uses a fixed set of $N=2000$ sequences (seed $2000+s$); test sets of 2000 fresh sequences (seed 5000) are shared by all systems; a validation set (seed 7000) is used only for tuning. Train and test sequences are random and may overlap by chance (about $10^{8}$ possible inputs).

\textbf{Protocol.} Seed $s\in\{0,..,4\}$ fixes one pretrained model (4000 steps, batch 128, AdamW, lr $2\times10^{-3}$, cosine; \rhval{main/no-adaptation/sort_desc/pretask_acc/mean:pct2}\% exact match on sort ascending), the adaptation set, and the adapter initialisation. Every adapted system, including from-scratch, uses 1500 steps, batch 64, AdamW with zero weight decay, 50 warm-up steps and cosine decay, and identical data; only the learning rate differs per system. Learning rates were chosen on the validation split of a separate seed (100, with its own pretrained model) from a grid of one run per value and task; after the best value fell at the grid edge for four systems the grid was extended once, but the selected rates for full fine-tuning ($10^{-2}$), LoRA ($3\times10^{-2}$), last block ($10^{-2}$) and head ($3\times10^{-2}$) are still at the upper edge of the extended grid, so they may be below the optimum (only scratch, $3\times10^{-3}$, is interior) (adding $10^{-2}$ for full and scratch and $3\times10^{-2}$ for LoRA, last block and head; first grids: $\{3\times10^{-4},10^{-3},3\times10^{-3}\}$ for full and scratch, $\{10^{-3},3\times10^{-3},10^{-2}\}$ otherwise; see \texttt{experiments/PROTOCOL.md}). Selected rates: full $10^{-2}$, scratch $3\times10^{-3}$, LoRA $3\times10^{-2}$ (tuned at $r=4$ only, reused for all ranks without per-rank tuning), last block $10^{-2}$, head $3\times10^{-2}$. The tuning budget was \rhval{count/tune/runs} runs (about 10 minutes). Main experiments: 9 systems $\times$ 2 tasks $\times$ 5 seeds (\rhval{count/main/runs} runs); ablations \rhval{count/abl_targets/runs}, \rhval{count/sweep_ntrain/runs}, \rhval{count/sweep_lr/runs} and \rhval{count/curves/runs} further runs (target modules, set size, learning rate, curves); a typical run takes 6--27 s (5th--95th percentile; median 16 s), the five registered runs that include pretraining 50--60 s (the seed-0 checkpoint already existed when its first registered run started), and the \rhval{count/runs} registered runs span about 54 minutes of wall clock (68 minutes of summed run time, above the 45 minutes planned in \texttt{research.yaml}) on 2 threads of a laptop CPU with up to two processes in parallel. Mean $\pm$ sample standard deviation over seeds are reported; paired tests are paired $t$-tests over seeds (as in \texttt{rh compare}), which with $n=5$ have little power.

\textbf{Ablations.} (a) LoRA on $\{q,v\}$ only, ranks 1 and 4, five seeds. (b) Adaptation set size $N\in\{100,300\}$ for six systems on descending sort, seeds 0--2, learning rates unchanged from the $N=2000$ tuning. (c) Learning-rate sweep for full fine-tuning and LoRA $r=4$, seeds 0--2. (d) Adaptation curves, evaluated every 50 steps on 500 test sequences, for seeds 0--2. The $N=2000$ and default-learning-rate results are the main-group runs (five seeds, Table~\ref{tab:main}) and are not repeated in the ablation tables.

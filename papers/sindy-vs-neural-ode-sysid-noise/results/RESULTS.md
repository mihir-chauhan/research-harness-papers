# Results (5 seeds; registry results/runs.jsonl; tables in results/tables; v1 results archived in results/archive_v1)
Verdicts use the refutation criteria registered in proposal.md.
- H1 (SINDy lower pred_nrmse than MLP/DMDc at low noise and small data, gap shrinking with noise; refuted if MLP or DMDc is as good or better at noise 0 or N=200):
  - Noise 0 (N=1000): SINDy better than MLP and DMDc in 5/5 seeds on both systems (noise_gap table): not refuted.
  - Noise sweep (N=1000): supported for the pendulum at all noise levels (5/5 seeds, paired p <= 0.015 vs MLP and DMDc) and for Van der Pol up to noise 0.05. At Van der Pol noise 0.1 one SINDy seed diverges (seed 2, 1.748; mean 0.389); the median (0.048) is below the MLP (0.283) and DMDc (0.386) medians, SINDy is better in 4/5 seeds, paired p = 0.819 / 0.998: inconclusive.
  - N=200 (noise 0.05; ntrain_pred and vdp_seeds tables): pendulum supported (5/5 seeds, p = 0.003). Van der Pol REFUTED: two of five SINDy seeds diverge (seed 1 = 4.965, seed 4 = 15.425), SINDy mean 4.190 vs MLP 0.333, SINDy better than the MLP in 3/5 seeds (and than DMDc in 3/5), medians 0.289 vs 0.281, paired p = 0.262. From N=500 upward SINDy is better in 5/5 seeds (p <= 0.014).
  - "Gap shrinks as noise grows": NOT supported in absolute terms. The median MLP-SINDy gap grows from noise 0 to 0.05 on both systems and to 0.1 on Van der Pol; on the pendulum it falls from 0.478 to 0.341 between 0.05 and 0.1. Only the ratio shrinks.
  - The design is two one-dimensional sweeps, not a noise x data grid.
- H2 (LQR cost differences much smaller than prediction-error differences; refuted if cost ranks and gaps track prediction error): supported on Van der Pol (DMDc 1.9-3.5% worse than the oracle in 5/5 seeds; MLP within 0.4% up to noise 0.05, 6.7% at 0.1; SINDy within 0.2%; paired_lqr table). Refuted on the pendulum: cost follows prediction error (SINDy 2.9% above the oracle at noise 0.05 and 23.1% at 0.1; MLP 142.5% and 1010.4%; DMDc fails in most episodes). MPC: same ordering on the pendulum at noise 0.02 and 0.05 only; at noise 0.1 MLP (30.295 +/- 30.687) and DMDc (27.028 +/- 6.085) are not separable. On Van der Pol the MPC differences are within the planner's own suboptimality (DMDc mean below the oracle).
- MLP failure at small data (pendulum, noise 0.05, fixed 2500 steps): LQR cost mean 430.585 (median 121.376) at N=200, 208.285 (1.616) at N=500. The three runs with cost exactly 1000 were diagnosed (group diag_lqr, experiments/diag_lqr.py, diag_lqr table): Riccati solved, 10/10 episodes at the cap, identified input gain has the wrong sign. The 10*Q fallback for an unsolved Riccati equation was not triggered in them.
- H3 (threshold and library matter; removing trig terms hurts on the pendulum, lam sweep U-shaped; refuted if flat): library supported on the pendulum (5/5 seeds worse, p < 0.001); on Van der Pol removing the redundant trig columns is slightly better (5/5 seeds). Threshold weakly supported: mean pred_nrmse is lowest at lam=0.3 and higher on both sides on both systems, but shallow between 0.01 and 0.3; at lam=1 the rise on Van der Pol is in the mean (0.195) and not the median (0.014). LQR cost insensitive. SG smoothing helps on the pendulum, hurts on Van der Pol (5/5 seeds).
- VERDICT.md (regenerated from the current registry) is advisory: its "best baseline" is the oracle, which has zero prediction error by construction, and its ablation rows pool both tasks.

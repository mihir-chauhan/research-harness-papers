\textbf{Systems.} State $x=[x_1,x_2]$, scalar input $u$, $\dot x = f(x,u)$ with $\dot x_1=x_2$, integrated with RK4 at $\Delta t=\rhval{const/dt:2}$. Pendulum (inverted, unstable at the origin): $\dot x_2 = g\sin x_1 - \rhval{const/pendulum_damping:1}\,x_2 + u$ with standard gravity $g$ (4 non-zero terms in the library below). Van der Pol: $\dot x_2 = (1-x_1^2)x_2 - x_1 + u$ (5 terms). Training initial states are uniform in $[-1,1]^2$ (pendulum) and $[-\rhval{const/vdp_train_ic_bound:1},\rhval{const/vdp_train_ic_bound:1}]^2$ (Van der Pol) and training inputs uniform in $[-5,5]$ and $[-3,3]$; closed-loop initial states are uniform in $[-\rhval{const/pendulum_cl_ic_bound:1},\rhval{const/pendulum_cl_ic_bound:1}]^2$ and $[-1,1]^2$. The cost weights are $Q=\mathrm{diag}(10,1)$ and $Q=\mathrm{diag}(1,1)$, with $R=\rhval{const/lqr_input_weight:1}$ for both. These settings are identical for every model.

\textbf{Data.} $N$ transitions are $N/40$ trajectories of 40 steps from random initial conditions with piecewise-constant random inputs (held 5 steps). Measurements are $\tilde x = x + \eta$, with $\eta\sim\mathcal N(0,(\sigma\, s)^2)$, $s$ the per-state standard deviation of the clean training data and $\sigma\in\{0,0.02,0.05,0.1\}$ the noise level. Inputs are noise-free.

\textbf{SINDy-STLSQ.} Derivatives are estimated by central differences, $\dot{\tilde x}_k\approx(\tilde x_{k+1}-\tilde x_{k-1})/2\Delta t$. Because the stencil spans the inputs $u_{k-1}$ and $u_k$ (which differ at hold boundaries), the regression uses the stencil average $\bar u_k=(u_{k-1}+u_k)/2$. With the library $\Theta(x,u)$ (monomials in $x$ up to degree 3, $\sin x_1$, $\cos x_1$, $u$; 13 columns, column-normalised) we solve
\begin{equation}
\min_{\Xi}\ \|\dot X-\Theta(X,U)\,\Xi\|_F^2 ,
\end{equation}
then repeat 10 times: zero all entries with $|\Xi_{ij}|<\lambda$ and refit the remaining entries by least squares. Default $\lambda=\rhval{const/sindy_default_lambda:2}$ (on normalised columns), not tuned per task.

\textbf{Neural ODE (MLP).} $f_\theta(x,u)$ is a $3\!-\!64\!-\!64\!-\!2$ tanh MLP with standardised inputs; the one-step loss $\|\tilde x_{k+1}-\mathrm{RK4}_{f_\theta}(\tilde x_k,u_k)\|^2$ is minimised with Adam (lr \rhval{const/mlp_learning_rate:sci0}, weight decay \rhval{const/mlp_weight_decay:sci0}, cosine decay, batch \rhval{const/mlp_batch_size}) for a fixed budget of \rhval{const/mlp_gradient_steps} gradient steps regardless of the data size. One seventh of the transitions (at least 20) is held out and the parameters with the lowest held-out loss, checked every 25 steps, are kept.

\textbf{DMDc.} The discrete model $x_{k+1}=Ax_k+Bu_k$ is fit by least squares on measured transitions; the fitted $(A,B)$ are used directly for LQR and MPC (no linearisation or discretisation step).

\textbf{Controllers.} For each identified model we linearise at the origin (SINDy, MLP and the oracle: central differences of $f$, then exact zero-order-hold discretisation; the DMDc fit is used as is) and solve the discrete algebraic Riccati equation (DARE) for these $Q,R$, which gives the LQR gain $K$ and value matrix $P$. The MPC controller is a cross-entropy-method planner (horizon 12, 48 samples, 2 iterations, 8 elites, initial sampling standard deviation 5, warm-started from the previous plan) that rolls out the identified model (SINDy, MLP and oracle: RK4 on $f$; DMDc: the linear map) with stage cost $(x^\top Qx+Ru^2)\Delta t$ and terminal cost $x^\top Px\,\Delta t$. Both controllers run on the true system with the input clipped to $[-10,10]$, wider than the training input range, so a controller can query its model outside the inputs seen in training. The closed-loop cost is $J=\sum_k (x_k^\top Qx_k+Ru_k^2)\Delta t$; an episode in which $|x_i|>50$ is stopped and assigned the cap $J=\rhval{const/cost_cap}$, and an episode fails if it is stopped or ends with $\|x\|>\rhval{const/fail_norm:1}$. If the DARE has no solution, the code records the cap cost \rhval{const/cost_cap} and failure for LQR and uses $10\,Q$ as the MPC terminal cost; this fallback was not triggered in the runs with LQR cost at the cap (Table~\ref{tab:diag}).

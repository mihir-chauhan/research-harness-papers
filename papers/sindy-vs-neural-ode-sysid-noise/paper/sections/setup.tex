\textbf{Protocol.} Main experiment: 2 tasks $\times$ 4 noise levels $\times$ 4 systems (SINDy, MLP, DMDc, and the true model as an oracle reference) $\times$ 5 seeds with 1000 training transitions. Sweeps at noise $0.05$: training size $N\in\{200,500,1000,2000,5000\}$ for the same four systems, and threshold $\lambda\in\{0.01,0.03,0.1,0.3,1\}$ for SINDy. Ablation of SINDy at noise 0.05: no trigonometric library terms, Savitzky--Golay smoothing before differentiation (window 9, order 3), and smoothing with $\lambda=0.3$. The seed controls data, noise and network initialisation, and every model sees the same data for a given seed. The $N=1000$ cells of the size sweep and the full-SINDy row of the ablation repeat the noise-0.05 configuration of the main experiment and reproduce its values. Hyperparameters were fixed a priori and not tuned on evaluation data; the ablation row with $\lambda=0.3$ is a post-hoc choice informed by the $\lambda$ sweep.

\textbf{Metrics.} \texttt{pred\_nrmse}: RMS error of 40-step open-loop prediction from the true initial state on 20 clean held-out trajectories, normalised by per-state standard deviation; predicted states are clipped to $[-50,50]$ (so a divergent model contributes a bounded error, which can still be far above 1). \texttt{lqr\_cost}, \texttt{mpc\_cost}: mean closed-loop cost on the true system over 10 (LQR, 80 steps) or 6 (MPC, 60 steps) initial conditions; \texttt{lqr\_fail}, \texttt{mpc\_fail}: fraction of failed episodes; \texttt{support\_f1}, \texttt{n\_terms}: F1 of the recovered library support against the true terms and number of non-zero coefficients (SINDy only; F1 is undefined for the pendulum without trig terms).

\textbf{Hypotheses and registered refutation criteria.} H1: SINDy has lower prediction error than the MLP and DMDc at low noise and small data, and the gap shrinks as noise grows; refuted if the MLP or DMDc is as good or better at noise 0 or at $N=200$. H2: closed-loop cost differences across models are much smaller than prediction-error differences; refuted if cost ranks and relative gaps track prediction error. H3: SINDy depends on the threshold and the library: removing the trig terms hurts on the pendulum and the $\lambda$ sweep is U-shaped; refuted if flat.

\textbf{Scale.} Everything ran on a shared laptop CPU (Apple M1 Pro) with 2 threads. The final registry has 450 runs, about 7 seconds each for the MLP and under one second for the other models, about 21 minutes in total, plus three diagnostic runs (Table~\ref{tab:diag}); an earlier set of runs with two implementation flaws (Sec.~\ref{sec:limitations}) is archived and not used. All baselines are reimplemented here and are not the tuned versions from the cited papers; the ``True model'' rows are an oracle, not a competitor. All $p$-values are paired $t$-tests over the 5 seeds, unadjusted for multiple comparisons.

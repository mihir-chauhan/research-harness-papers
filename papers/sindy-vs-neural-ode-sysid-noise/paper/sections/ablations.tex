\textbf{Ablations (H3).} Table~\ref{tab:abl} compares SINDy variants at noise 0.05, with paired comparisons to the full model. Removing the $\sin x_1$ and $\cos x_1$ columns makes the pendulum model badly wrong in prediction and in closed loop (worse in 5 of 5 seeds). On Van der Pol, whose dynamics are polynomial, the same ablation does not hurt; it lowers the prediction error (\rhval{abl_sindy/sindy-w-o-trig-library/vdp_noise0.05/pred_nrmse/mean:3} versus \rhval{abl_sindy/sindy-stlsq/vdp_noise0.05/pred_nrmse/mean:3}, better in 5 of 5 seeds) and raises support F1 (\rhval{abl_sindy/sindy-w-o-trig-library/vdp_noise0.05/support_f1/mean:3} versus \rhval{abl_sindy/sindy-stlsq/vdp_noise0.05/support_f1/mean:3}) with far fewer retained terms (\rhval{abl_sindy/sindy-w-o-trig-library/vdp_noise0.05/n_terms/mean:1} versus \rhval{abl_sindy/sindy-stlsq/vdp_noise0.05/n_terms/mean:1}), i.e.\ with the redundant trig columns in the library STLSQ keeps more spurious terms under noise. Savitzky--Golay smoothing before differentiation helps on the pendulum (prediction error \rhval{abl_sindy/sindy-+-sg-smoothing/pendulum_noise0.05/pred_nrmse/mean:3} versus \rhval{abl_sindy/sindy-stlsq/pendulum_noise0.05/pred_nrmse/mean:3}, better in 5 of 5 seeds) but hurts on Van der Pol (\rhval{abl_sindy/sindy-+-sg-smoothing/vdp_noise0.05/pred_nrmse/mean:3} versus \rhval{abl_sindy/sindy-stlsq/vdp_noise0.05/pred_nrmse/mean:3}, worse in 5 of 5 seeds), so we cannot recommend smoothing in general; we did not test why. With a larger threshold ($\lambda=0.3$) the pendulum support F1 improves further (\rhval{abl_sindy/sindy-+-sg-smoothing-lam-0.3/pendulum_noise0.05/support_f1/mean:3}); as noted in Sec.~\ref{sec:setup}, this variant was chosen after seeing the sweep, so its advantage is exploratory.

\textbf{Threshold sensitivity.} Table~\ref{tab:lam} shows that the mean prediction error is lowest at $\lambda=0.3$ on both systems and higher on both sides, so the sweep is not flat. The left arm is shallow (from $\lambda=0.01$ to 0.3: pendulum \rhval{sweep_lam_pendulum/sindy-lam-0.01/pendulum_noise0.05/pred_nrmse/mean:3} to \rhval{sweep_lam_pendulum/sindy-lam-0.3/pendulum_noise0.05/pred_nrmse/mean:3}, Van der Pol \rhval{sweep_lam_vdp/sindy-lam-0.01/vdp_noise0.05/pred_nrmse/mean:3} to \rhval{sweep_lam_vdp/sindy-lam-0.3/vdp_noise0.05/pred_nrmse/mean:3}). The right arm at $\lambda=1$ is clear on the pendulum (mean \rhval{sweep_lam_pendulum/sindy-lam-1.0/pendulum_noise0.05/pred_nrmse/mean:3}, median \rhval{sweep_lam_pendulum/sindy-lam-1.0/pendulum_noise0.05/pred_nrmse/median:3}); on Van der Pol it appears in the mean (\rhval{sweep_lam_vdp/sindy-lam-1.0/vdp_noise0.05/pred_nrmse/mean:3}) but not in the median (\rhval{sweep_lam_vdp/sindy-lam-1.0/vdp_noise0.05/pred_nrmse/median:3}), i.e.\ it comes from a minority of seeds. At $\lambda=1$ the smallest model has \rhval{sweep_lam_pendulum/sindy-lam-1.0/pendulum_noise0.05/n_terms/min:0} (pendulum) and \rhval{sweep_lam_vdp/sindy-lam-1.0/vdp_noise0.05/n_terms/min:0} (Van der Pol) terms, fewer than the 4 (pendulum) and 5 (Van der Pol) true terms, so in some seeds a true term is thresholded away. The LQR cost is almost unaffected by $\lambda$ over the whole range (pendulum \rhval{sweep_lam_pendulum/sindy-lam-1.0/pendulum_noise0.05/lqr_cost/mean:3} to \rhval{sweep_lam_pendulum/sindy-lam-0.03/pendulum_noise0.05/lqr_cost/mean:3}, Van der Pol \rhval{sweep_lam_vdp/sindy-lam-0.03/vdp_noise0.05/lqr_cost/mean:3} to \rhval{sweep_lam_vdp/sindy-lam-1.0/vdp_noise0.05/lqr_cost/mean:3}). By the registered criteria H3 is supported for the library on the pendulum and only weakly for the threshold: the sweep is U-shaped in the mean but shallow between 0.01 and 0.3, and the controller is insensitive to it.

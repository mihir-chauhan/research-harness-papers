\textbf{Ablations (H3).} Table~\ref{tab:abl} compares SINDy variants at noise 0.05, with paired comparisons to the full model. Removing the $\sin x_1$ and $\cos x_1$ columns makes the pendulum model badly wrong in prediction and in closed loop (worse in 5 of 5 seeds). On Van der Pol, whose dynamics are polynomial, the same ablation does not hurt; it lowers the prediction error (0.011 versus 0.018, better in 5 of 5 seeds) and raises support F1 (0.982 versus 0.546) with far fewer retained terms (5.2 versus 14.0), i.e.\ with the redundant trig columns in the library STLSQ keeps more spurious terms under noise. Savitzky--Golay smoothing before differentiation helps on the pendulum (prediction error 0.086 versus 0.156, better in 5 of 5 seeds) but hurts on Van der Pol (0.053 versus 0.018, worse in 5 of 5 seeds), so we cannot recommend smoothing in general; we did not test why. With a larger threshold ($\lambda=0.3$) the pendulum support F1 improves further (0.933); as noted in Sec.~\ref{sec:setup}, this variant was chosen after seeing the sweep, so its advantage is exploratory.

\textbf{Threshold sensitivity.} Table~\ref{tab:lam} shows that the mean prediction error is lowest at $\lambda=0.3$ on both systems and higher on both sides, so the sweep is not flat. The left arm is shallow (from $\lambda=0.01$ to 0.3: pendulum 0.177 to 0.140, Van der Pol 0.027 to 0.012). The right arm at $\lambda=1$ is clear on the pendulum (mean 0.334, median 0.207); on Van der Pol it appears in the mean (0.195) but not in the median (0.014), i.e.\ it comes from a minority of seeds. At $\lambda=1$ the smallest model has 3 terms on either system, fewer than the 4 (pendulum) and 5 (Van der Pol) true terms, so in some seeds a true term is thresholded away. The LQR cost is almost unaffected by $\lambda$ over the whole range (pendulum 1.084 to 1.099, Van der Pol 0.687 to 0.695). By the registered criteria H3 is supported for the library on the pendulum and only weakly for the threshold: the sweep is U-shaped in the mean but shallow between 0.01 and 0.3, and the controller is insensitive to it.

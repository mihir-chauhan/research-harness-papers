# Design
`method/run.py` (single entrypoint). Data: sklearn digits (1797 images, 8x8, pixels/16), stratified split per seed: 1078 train / 269 validation / 450 test. Shift is applied to the test images only (rotation by a fixed angle, bilinear, zero fill, no reshape; then additive Gaussian noise N(0, sigma^2) and clipping to [0,1]).
Network: 64-128-128-10 ReLU MLP, Adam lr 1e-3, batch 64, 100 epochs, no weight decay, no early stopping, no tuning. Systems (all reimplemented): `mlp` (T=1), `ts` (T fitted by LBFGS on validation NLL, unshifted validation), `ts_oracle` (ablation: T fitted on validation images shifted like the test condition), `mcd` (dropout p=0.2 after each hidden layer in training, 20 stochastic forward passes averaged at test), `ens` (M=5 independently initialised and shuffled MLPs, probabilities averaged). The single MLP is ensemble member 0 (seed 100*s). Trained nets are cached in /tmp/calib_cache so that rows of one seed share the trained network.
Metrics: accuracy, 15-bin equal-width top-label ECE, NLL, multiclass Brier (sum over classes, mean over samples), mean confidence.

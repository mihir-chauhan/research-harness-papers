# Protocol
Tasks: 11 test conditions (rot0, rot10, rot20, rot30, rot45, rot60, noise0.25, noise0.5, noise0.75, noise1.0, rot30_noise0.5). Seeds 0-4 (split, init and noise all depend on the seed). Systems: MLP, MLP + temperature scaling (method), MC dropout (T=20, p=0.2), Deep ensemble (5). Same data, split, optimiser and epochs for every system. Tuning budget: none (hyperparameters fixed a priori; sensitivity sweeps are reported, not used to pick). Ablations: abl_oracle (ts_oracle, same tasks and seeds), sweep_members (M in 1,2,3,5,10) and sweep_dropout (p in 0.1,0.2,0.3,0.5) on rot0, rot30, noise0.5 with seeds 0-2 (the M=5 and p=0.2 points are deliberate re-runs of the main-group configuration). Hardware: shared CPU, 2 threads, torch CPU. Hypotheses: proposal.md.
Added after seeing the main-group result that MC dropout beat the ensemble (the dropout-trained network differs from the other systems in training regularisation): abl_dropdet runs the dropout-trained network (p=0.2) deterministically with dropout off (same 11 tasks, seeds 0-4), to separate regularisation from MC averaging. This ablation was not in the pre-registered hypothesis list.

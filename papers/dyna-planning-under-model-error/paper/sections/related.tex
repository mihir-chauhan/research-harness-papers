\textbf{Dyna and tabular planning.} Dyna~\cite{sutton1991dyna} integrates learning, planning and reacting by replaying simulated experience from a learned model; prioritized sweeping~\cite{moore1993prioritized} orders planning updates by the size of the expected value change, propagating them to predecessors, to use less data and time. Q-learning~\cite{watkins1992q} is the model-free reference, and Kaelbling et al.~\cite{kaelbling1996reinforcement} survey these methods and the exploration problem they face. Dyna-Q+ and the blocking/shortcut mazes originate in Sutton's integrated Dyna architecture~\cite{sutton1990integrated}; we reimplement them from the published description. Brea~\cite{brea2017is} compares prioritized sweeping with episodic control, a context where a tabular model is again useful.

\textbf{Model error and search control.} van Hasselt et al.~\cite{hasselt2019when} ask when parametric models should be preferred over experience replay and argue that planning from an imperfect model is not obviously better than replay. Pan et al.~\cite{pan2019hill} study how the states from which the model is queried (search control) affect Dyna. In deep model-based RL, MBPO~\cite{janner2019when} analyses how model error bounds the useful rollout length, PETS~\cite{chua2018deep} models uncertainty with ensembles, and Moerland et al.~\cite{moerland2020model} survey the area. Wang et al.~\cite{wang2019benchmarking} benchmark many such algorithms.

\textbf{Evaluation practice.} Agarwal et al.~\cite{agarwal2021deep} argue that few-run RL comparisons should report uncertainty rather than point estimates; we report standard deviations, paired-seed tests and the number of seeds, though not the interval estimators they propose.

\textbf{Gap.} We found no study that sweeps the number of planning steps for Dyna-Q, Dyna-Q+ and prioritized sweeping together across stale, opportunity and stochastic regimes with paired seeds; this paper provides a small one. It is not a claim that none exists, only that our literature search (platform library, arXiv, Semantic Scholar, OpenAlex) did not return one.

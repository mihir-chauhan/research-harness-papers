\textbf{Which part of Dyna-Q+ matters (Table~\ref{tab:abl}).} Removing the bonus lowers post-change reward on the shortcut maze (168.2 versus 229.7, paired $p<0.001$, \texttt{compare\_abl\_dynaq\_plus\_post\_reward.csv}), so the stale-transition bonus accounts for the shortcut gain, and the bonus-free variant equals Dyna-Q there within noise (168.2 versus 165.8). On blocking the drop (36.8 versus 51.9) is only a trend (paired $p=0.069$). Removing only the untried-action initialisation gives the lowest mean on every task: with the bonus but without that initialisation the agent is worse than plain Dyna-Q on the static maze (52.1 versus 128.4) and the stochastic maze (20.1 versus 46.1), both paired $p<0.001$ (\texttt{compare\_abl\_dynaq\_plus\_post\_reward\_refDynaQ.csv}); on blocking, shortcut and stochastic-blocking the gaps to Dyna-Q are trends only ($p=0.30$, $0.16$, $0.13$). The two components are therefore not separable improvements. We have not run the interaction in a finer design. On the stochastic maze the bonus costs post-change reward (36.5 versus 50.8 without it, paired $p=0.002$); a plausible reason, which we did not test, is that a transition looks stale only because it is noisy and the bonus drives pointless re-exploration.

\textbf{Bonus weight (Table~\ref{tab:kappa}).} The default $\kappa=10^{-3}$ was fixed in advance and not tuned. At $\kappa\ge10^{-2}$ Dyna-Q+ collapses on every task to a handful of goals: with $n=10$ the planning bonus $\kappa\sqrt{\tau}$ for rarely tried pairs outweighs the unit goal reward, and the agent keeps chasing it. Smaller $\kappa=10^{-4}$ retains the static and stochastic performance but loses most of the post-change advantage (the shortcut gain disappears; blocking falls between Dyna-Q and the default). The benefit therefore lives in a narrow band of $\kappa$ that depends on the horizon and reward scale, and we only sampled four values. The $n$ sweep (Table~\ref{tab:sweepn}, Fig.~\ref{fig:sweepn}) is the second sensitivity analysis: it is where the planning-step trade-off of the title is read off.

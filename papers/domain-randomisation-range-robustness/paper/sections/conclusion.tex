On CartPole with a CEM-trained MLP, widening the randomisation range raised robustness to shifted dynamics up to a plateau near $w\approx1.5$ while nominal return stayed at the cap, so we did not find a width where nominal performance fell faster than robustness improved. A success-gated curriculum matched wide fixed randomisation and was not better than an ungated linear schedule. These conclusions are specific to this easy, saturated task; a harder nominal objective is the first thing to change in follow-up work.

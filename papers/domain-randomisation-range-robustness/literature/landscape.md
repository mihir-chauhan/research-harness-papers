# Landscape
Domain randomisation of visual and dynamics parameters was introduced for sim-to-real transfer (Tobin et al. 2017; Peng et al. 2018) and scaled in robot-hand work (OpenAI et al. 2019, not cited here as the record could not be fetched). EPOpt (Rajeswaran et al.) trains on model ensembles and optimises a worst-case percentile. Adaptive methods move the randomisation distribution using real data (Chebotar et al. 2019; DROPO, Tiboni et al. 2022). Auto-curricula are surveyed by Portelas et al. 2020. Theory on DR for sim-to-real is given by Chen et al. 2021. Xie et al. 2021 revisit dynamics randomisation for a quadruped, and Yu et al. 2017 pair randomisation with online system identification. Alghonaim and Johns 2020 benchmark visual DR.
Gap: few works isolate the effect of range width on a nominal-vs-robust trade-off in a controlled, cheap setting with out-of-range evaluation, or test a plain success-gated widening rule against fixed ranges. We do not claim novelty of the curriculum rule; the contribution is a small controlled measurement.

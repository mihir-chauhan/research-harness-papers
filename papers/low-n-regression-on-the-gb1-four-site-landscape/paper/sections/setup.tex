\subsection{Data}
We use \texttt{four\_mutations\_full\_data.csv} from the FLIP repository~\cite{dallago2021flip}, the GB1 landscape of Wu et al.~\cite{wu2016adaptation}: \rhval{const/n_variants} variants at four sites (wild type VDGV), of which \rhval{const/n_hd1}, \rhval{const/n_hd2}, \rhval{const/n_hd3} and \rhval{const/n_hd4} lie at Hamming distance 1, 2, 3 and 4 from wild type. The fitness is the enrichment-based value in the file (wild type $=1$, maximum \rhval{const/max_fit:2}); \rhval{const/pct_dead:1}\% of variants are below 0.01. We use only the four-residue string and the fitness column; the raw fitness is the regression target in the main experiments. (The CSV is not committed; \texttt{method/DESIGN.md} gives the URL.)

\subsection{Tasks, splits and seeds}
A task is a training-set composition and size $N\in\{48,96,384,2000\}$. In \emph{rand}$_N$ the $N$ training variants are drawn uniformly without replacement from all variants, and the test set is every other variant. In \emph{dbl}$_N$ they are drawn from the \rhval{const/n_dbl_pool} wild-type, single and double mutants, and the test set is the \rhval{const/n_dbl_test} variants at Hamming distance at least 3, so the task is extrapolation from low-order to high-order variants ($N{=}2000$ uses most of the pool). Seed $s\in\{0,\dots,4\}$ determines the draw, which is identical for all systems at the same task and seed; that makes the per-seed comparisons paired. There is no separate validation split: every hyperparameter is chosen on the training set (ridge by CV, GP by marginal likelihood) or fixed in advance; the CNN configuration was instead chosen manually on a separate draw whose test pool overlaps the evaluation pools (see the tuning budget below).

\subsection{Tuning budget}
Ridge and pairwise ridge: \rhval{const/n_alpha_grid} penalties $\times$ \rhval{const/n_cv_folds} folds on the training set. GP: \rhval{const/n_gp_grid} marginal-likelihood evaluations. CNN: \rhval{const/n_cnn_tune_cfgs} configurations (base, 100 or 400 epochs, dropout 0.2, weight decay 0.1, width 32, dropout and weight decay together) were run on one separate random draw (seed \rhval{const/cnn_tune_seed}) at $N=96$ and $N=384$ and the one with the best mean Spearman (dropout and weight decay together) was fixed for all tasks. That draw is sampled from the same landscape, so test variants of the tuning draw overlap with those of the evaluation draws; the CNN has received a more manual tuning effort than the other systems (Sec.~\ref{sec:limitations}). The tuning runs are logged in group \texttt{tune\_cnn}; some earlier invocations of that script failed on a missing output directory and were rerun (they appear as failed rows in the registry).

\subsection{Hypotheses and decision rules}
Fixed before the main runs (\texttt{proposal.md}): \textbf{H1} pairwise features raise Spearman over additive ridge only at $N\ge384$ with random training; refuted if the pairwise mean is at or above ridge at $N\le96$, or at or below it at $N\ge384$. \textbf{H2} the GP is at least as good as ridge at $N\le96$ (random). \textbf{H3} the CNN is worse than the best linear or kernel model at $N\le384$ and does not clearly exceed it at $N{=}2000$. \textbf{H4} for every system, training on single and double mutants only gives lower Spearman on the Hamming-distance-3 and 4 test variants than random training of the same size. \textbf{H5} no system's mean top-100 recall reaches 0.25 at $N\le384$. The proposal named a Wilcoxon signed-rank test, but with five seeds its smallest attainable two-sided p-value is above 0.05, and the comparison tool in this environment reports Welch and paired $t$-tests; this substitution was made after the main results had been inspected (the mean-based refutation rules were fixed beforehand); we therefore use the mean-based refutation rules above as the verdict, and quote paired $t$-test $p$-values (five pairs, uncorrected for the many comparisons) only descriptively.

\subsection{Compute}
CPU only, on a shared machine with two threads per process; every run finished within the per-run limit, and the whole study took well under the study budget. Wall-clock time is not reported as a result.

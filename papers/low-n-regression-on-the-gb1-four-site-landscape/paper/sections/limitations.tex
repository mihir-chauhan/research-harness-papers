\textbf{One landscape, one protein.} All conclusions concern GB1 at four sites, a landscape in which about \rhval{const/pct_dead:1}\% of variants are non-functional; conclusions about epistasis features, CNNs or GPs need not transfer to other proteins, longer sequences or less skewed fitness distributions.
\textbf{Few draws.} Five draws per cell give standard deviations that, at $N\le96$, are as large as most between-system differences; we report paired $p$-values descriptively and without multiple-comparison correction, and the pre-registered Wilcoxon test cannot reach the 0.05 level with five seeds.
\textbf{Tuning asymmetry.} The CNN configuration was selected by hand on one separate draw at two sizes, evaluated on test variants that overlap with later evaluations' test pools; the linear models and the GP are tuned only on their training data. A fully nested protocol (inner validation for every model) was not run, and the CNN's raw-target advantage may partly reflect this. Ensembling was applied only in the CNN ablation.
\textbf{Reimplemented, simple baselines.} The GP uses a plain Hamming kernel with a small hyperparameter grid, not structure- or language-model-informed kernels such as Kermut~\cite{groth2024kermut} or evolutionary density features~\cite{hsu2022learning}; the CNN is a tiny model on four residues, and no pretrained protein language model~\cite{meier2021language,rao2019evaluating} was used.
\textbf{Mechanisms are suggested, not isolated.} The log-target ablation is consistent with the reading that the CNN's edge is in part an output nonlinearity (the gap narrowed but did not vanish at the largest size), but the log transform is a crude stand-in for a learned monotone function (a global-epistasis model~\cite{otwinowski2018inferring} was not implemented), and the explanations offered for the pairwise-ridge collapse under dbl training and for the small-N advantage of dbl training are hypotheses we did not test.
\textbf{Metrics.} Top-100 recall is computed against the top 100 of the full test set (hundreds of thousands of variants) and is very sensitive to ties and to one or two variants at $N\le384$; Spearman with massive ties at zero fitness is dominated by the alive/dead split.
\textbf{What a full study would add.} More landscapes (e.g.\ the benchmarks in ProteinGym~\cite{notin2023proteingym}), many more draws, nested tuning for every model, a global-epistasis baseline, active learning rather than random draws, and language-model features.

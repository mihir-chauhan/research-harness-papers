# Design
`method/run.py`: plants (pendulum m=l=1, g=9.81, |u|<=3, damping 0.05; cart-pole mc=1, mp=0.3 point mass at l=0.5, |F|<=8, damping 0.02, RK4 dt=0.05), iLQR (Gauss-Newton on residual costs, finite-difference Jacobians, clipped controls, LM regularisation, line search over 5 step sizes, 25 iterations on first call then 4 per step, warm start by shifting), costs, and the energy-shaping+LQR controller.
Costs. quad: 0.5 e'Qe + 0.5 r u^2 (angle wrapped). energy: 0.5 wE (E-E*)^2 + 0.5 r u^2 (no state term). both: sum. Terminal: 0.5 e'Qf e for all MPC variants.
Pendulum Q=diag(1,.1), r=.05, Qf=diag(100,10). Cart-pole Q=diag(1,10,.1,.1), r=.01, Qf=diag(50,500,50,50). Defaults wE=1, qscale=1, horizon 30. Weights and the cart force limit were set by hand during development, before the reported runs. Trial runs made while choosing the cart force limit (seed 100, not a reported seed) were not logged with `rh run`; the only seed-100 rows in the registry are the 3-episode pendulum smoke runs. The a-priori choice therefore cannot be verified from the registry, and the paper says so.
Energy shaping: pendulum u=-k(E-E*)w (k=1); cart-pole acceleration command -k(E*-E)w cos(th) (k=5, saturated at 8; this is the sign in `run.py` and in the paper) plus cart PD, mapped to force; LQR (DARE on linearisation) catch when |th|<0.35 and |E-E*|<25% E*.
Objective: J = dt * sum of running costs + terminal cost (terminal cost not scaled by dt). LQR catch: DARE on the linearised RK4 step with Q and r of the quadratic running cost (unscaled).
`--diag` (off by default, does not change the controller or the other metrics) also reports the closed-loop means of 0.5 (E-E*)^2 and 0.5 e'Qe (`energy_term`, `quad_term`, `term_ratio`).
Analysis scripts: `method/sweep_table.py` (sweep, paired-sweep, tuned and diagnostic tables) and `method/sweep_fig.py` (figures); both read only `results/runs.jsonl` and use the sample std (ddof=1).

# Response to audit (fix round)

1. **Major, H2 effort claim without the default-weights caveat (fixed, with new runs).** The finding is correct. H2 in `results.tex` is now "supported at default weights, smaller after re-weighting" and states that the cart-pole gap is mostly a product of the default Q: iLQR-Quad at q=0.1 keeps success 1.00 and time 1.79 s with effort 50.2 vs 43.3 (gap 31.5 -> 6.9 on seeds 0-2, paired p=0.090; q=0.01: 6.7, p=0.046), that iLQR-Energy at wE=0.01/0.1/100 (51.6/50.9/58.5) is not below iLQR-Quad at q<=0.1, and that the direction persists on the pendulum (11.1 vs 13.9-15.9). Every cell of that comparison is in a new table (`sweep_paired.tex`, Table IV, built from the registry by `method/sweep_table.py`). To give the claim more than n=3, a tuned comparison was added (group `tuned`, 6 new runs on seeds 3-4, code committed first as 3aa1dd3): each cost at the weight with the highest success on seeds 0-2 (ties by lowest effort). Result (Table V): success 0.99 vs 1.00 (pendulum) and 1.00 vs 1.00 (cart-pole); effort 10.9 vs 14.4 and 42.0 vs 48.6 (paired p<0.001 and 0.009, n=5; held-out seeds 3-4 only: 12.0 vs 15.3 and 40.1 vs 46.2). The paper says this is post-hoc and that 3 of the 5 seeds were also used for selection. Abstract, conclusion, limitations, RESULTS.md and BRIEF.md carry the same caveat; "the most consistent effect" was replaced by a sentence that names the direction, its modest size after re-weighting, and that it does not hold for every energy weight on the cart-pole.

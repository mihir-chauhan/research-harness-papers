# Results (5 seeds x 20 starts per task; sweeps 3 seeds). Tables: results/tables/main.md, sweep_weights.tex, sweep_paired.tex, tuned.tex, diag_terms.tex; tests: results/tables/compare_main_*.csv

- **H1 (higher success than iLQR-Quad on both tasks): not supported.** Pendulum 0.95 vs 0.80 (paired p~0.003, n=5), but cart-pole 1.00 vs 1.00 (ceiling). The pendulum gap vanishes at larger quadratic scale (q=100 gives 1.00 in the sweep; tuned comparison 0.99 vs 1.00), so it holds at default weights only.
- **H2 (lower effort): supported at default weights by the registered test, much smaller after re-weighting.** Main group: pendulum 12.24 vs 17.24; cart-pole 42.03 vs 74.77 (both paired p<0.01). The cart-pole gap is mostly a product of the default Q: in the sweep (seeds 0-2) iLQR-Quad at q=0.1 keeps success 1.00 and time 1.79 s with effort 50.2 vs 43.3 for iLQR-Energy at wE=1 (difference 6.9, paired p=0.090, n=3; q=0.01: 50.0, difference 6.7, p=0.046). iLQR-Energy at wE=0.01/0.1/100 (51.6/50.9/58.5) is not below iLQR-Quad at q<=0.1. On the pendulum the direction persists across the sweep (11.1 vs 13.9-15.9). Tuned comparison (weight picked on seeds 0-2, seeds 3-4 added; post-hoc): pendulum 10.9 vs 14.4 (paired p<0.001, n=5), cart-pole 42.0 vs 48.6 (p=0.009, n=5); held-out seeds 3-4 only: 12.0 vs 15.3 and 40.1 vs 46.2 (n=2, no test). iLQR-Quad is nominally faster to upright on the cart-pole (not distinguishable).
- **H3 (less weight-sensitive): refuted.** Success range across weights equal (pendulum 0.17 vs 0.17, cart-pole 0.02 vs 0.02); effort range smaller for energy on cart-pole (15.2 vs 52.4), larger on pendulum (5.3 vs 2.0). Weights not commensurable.
- **H4 (matches/beats energy shaping+LQR): mixed.** Cart-pole: better on effort and time (not distinguishable on success). Pendulum: energy shaping nominally better in success (0.98 vs 0.95, not distinguishable at n=5) and time, and uses less effort (10.78 vs 12.24, paired p=0.0047, lower in all 5 seeds).
- iLQR-Energy vs iLQR-Quad+Energy on the pendulum: equal success, effort 12.24 vs 12.23. Diagnostic (group diag_terms, seeds 0-2): the per-run ratio of the closed-loop mean of 0.5(E-E*)^2 to that of 0.5 e'Qe (metric term_ratio) averages 15.64 on the pendulum and 0.03 on the cart-pole (an earlier version quoted 16.13, the ratio of the two seed-averaged terms, which is not a registry statistic). Consistent with the tie on the pendulum; a magnitude measurement, not an isolated mechanism.
- Bookkeeping: two fix-round cart-pole runs (tuned seed 4, diag_terms seed 2) stalled on the loaded machine and took about 661 s of wall-clock each (above the 6-minute target); outputs are deterministic. The diag_terms runs repeat iLQR-Quad+Energy on seeds 0-2 with the extra `--diag` outputs; their standard metrics equal the main-group rows. Summed run time is about 82 minutes, above the nominal budget.
- Note: the `ablations` entries in research.yaml are the swept systems' names (rh check matches on run name); the groups are sweep_wE, sweep_qscale, tuned and diag_terms.

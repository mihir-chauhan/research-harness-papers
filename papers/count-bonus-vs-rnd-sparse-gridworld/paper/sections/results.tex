Steps to first reward is censored at the budget (30k on chains, 100k on rooms), so a value equal to the budget means the reward was never found. All statements are for five seeds and one hyperparameter setting.

\textbf{H1 (both bonuses beat $\epsilon$-greedy on the larger tasks): supported as stated, but the controls show that neither bonus is the cause.} The count bonus found the reward in all five seeds on every task. $\epsilon$-greedy found it in no seed on chain\_40, room\_4 and room\_6 and in one of five on chain\_20: with all Q-values at zero it is a uniform random walk until the first reward. The RND bonus found the reward in every seed except one on room\_6; its mean is \rhval{main/rnd-bonus/chain_40/steps_to_first_reward/mean} steps on chain\_40 and \rhval{main/rnd-bonus/room_6/steps_to_first_reward/mean} on room\_6, where $\epsilon$-greedy sits at the budget (Welch $p={}$\rhval{cmp/main/epsilon-greedy-q-learning/chain_40/steps_to_first_reward/welch_p} and $p={}$\rhval{cmp/main/epsilon-greedy-q-learning/room_6/steps_to_first_reward/welch_p}, Table~\ref{tab:tests}). On chain\_10 and room\_2, where $\epsilon$-greedy does find the reward, RND does not differ from it at the 0.05 level ($p={}$\rhval{cmp/main/epsilon-greedy-q-learning/chain_10/steps_to_first_reward/welch_p} and \rhval{cmp/main/epsilon-greedy-q-learning/room_2/steps_to_first_reward/welch_p}). The controls change the reading. ``Penalty only'' has the same constant per-step penalty as the bonus systems and no bonus: it finds the reward in all seeds on every task, and its means are close to the count bonus on chain\_40, room\_4 and room\_6 (\rhval{main/step-penalty-only-optimistic-init/chain_40/steps_to_first_reward/mean} against \rhval{main/count-bonus-state/chain_40/steps_to_first_reward/mean}, \rhval{main/step-penalty-only-optimistic-init/room_4/steps_to_first_reward/mean} against \rhval{main/count-bonus-state/room_4/steps_to_first_reward/mean}, \rhval{main/step-penalty-only-optimistic-init/room_6/steps_to_first_reward/mean} against \rhval{main/count-bonus-state/room_6/steps_to_first_reward/mean}). On all six noise-free tasks the gap between the two is below the larger of their standard deviations and Welch $p\geq{}$\rhval{cmp/offset_control/count-bonus-state/chain_20/steps_to_first_reward/welch_p} (Table~\ref{tab:tests}). The count bonus without the offset finds the reward in no seed on chain\_40, room\_4 and room\_6. Hence the gain over $\epsilon$-greedy is explained by the optimistic offset, which the $\epsilon$-greedy baseline does not have; the data give no evidence that counts add speed beyond it on the noise-free tasks. The one exception is room\_4\_tv, where the state count is faster than the penalty-only control (\rhval{main/count-bonus-state/room_4_tv/steps_to_first_reward/mean} against \rhval{main/step-penalty-only-optimistic-init/room_4_tv/steps_to_first_reward/mean} steps, $p={}$\rhval{cmp/offset_control/count-bonus-state/room_4_tv/steps_to_first_reward/welch_p} uncorrected). We read it with caution: the penalty-only control cannot see the token, yet its own mean time differs between room\_4 and room\_4\_tv by more than its standard deviation on either task (\rhval{main/step-penalty-only-optimistic-init/room_4/steps_to_first_reward/mean}\,$\pm$\,\rhval{main/step-penalty-only-optimistic-init/room_4/steps_to_first_reward/std} against \rhval{main/step-penalty-only-optimistic-init/room_4_tv/steps_to_first_reward/mean}\,$\pm$\,\rhval{main/step-penalty-only-optimistic-init/room_4_tv/steps_to_first_reward/std}).

\textbf{H2 (counts at least as fast as RND, noise-free): supported, but it is not evidence for the count signal.} The count bonus is faster than RND on all six noise-free tasks, with paired $p<0.05$ on each (Table~\ref{tab:tests}); four of the six would survive a Bonferroni correction over the six tasks. However, the penalty-only control is faster than RND by similar margins (\rhval{main/rnd-bonus/chain_20/steps_to_first_reward/mean} against \rhval{main/step-penalty-only-optimistic-init/chain_20/steps_to_first_reward/mean} steps on chain\_20, \rhval{main/rnd-bonus/room_4/steps_to_first_reward/mean} against \rhval{main/step-penalty-only-optimistic-init/room_4/steps_to_first_reward/mean} on room\_4, \rhval{main/rnd-bonus/room_6/steps_to_first_reward/mean} against \rhval{main/step-penalty-only-optimistic-init/room_6/steps_to_first_reward/mean} on room\_6; Welch $p<0.05$ on five of the six noise-free tasks, $p={}$\rhval{cmp/main/step-penalty-only-optimistic-init/chain_10/steps_to_first_reward/welch_p} on chain\_10). So H2 holds because the RND-style signal costs time relative to no bonus, not because the count signal gains any.

\textbf{Where RND fails.} Room\_6 is the worst case: one seed never finds the reward, the spread is large (\rhval{main/rnd-bonus/room_6/steps_to_first_reward/mean}\,$\pm$\,\rhval{main/rnd-bonus/room_6/steps_to_first_reward/std} steps), and because the reward is found late the final return is only \rhval{main/rnd-bonus/room_6/final_return/mean}\,$\pm$\,\rhval{main/rnd-bonus/room_6/final_return/std}. Along the chains the absolute gap to the penalty-only control grows (\rhval{main/rnd-bonus/chain_10/steps_to_first_reward/mean} against \rhval{main/step-penalty-only-optimistic-init/chain_10/steps_to_first_reward/mean}, \rhval{main/rnd-bonus/chain_20/steps_to_first_reward/mean} against \rhval{main/step-penalty-only-optimistic-init/chain_20/steps_to_first_reward/mean}, \rhval{main/rnd-bonus/chain_40/steps_to_first_reward/mean} against \rhval{main/step-penalty-only-optimistic-init/chain_40/steps_to_first_reward/mean}) while the relative gap shrinks; the rooms show no monotone pattern.

\textbf{H3 (noisy TV delays RND, not the state count): not supported.} RND's time to first reward is \rhval{main/rnd-bonus/chain_20_tv/steps_to_first_reward/mean} steps on chain\_20\_tv against \rhval{main/rnd-bonus/chain_20/steps_to_first_reward/mean} on chain\_20 and \rhval{main/rnd-bonus/room_4_tv/steps_to_first_reward/mean} on room\_4\_tv against \rhval{main/rnd-bonus/room_4/steps_to_first_reward/mean} on room\_4: above the twin in the mean on one task, below on the other, neither beyond the seed spread (each gap is smaller than the twin's standard deviation, \rhval{main/rnd-bonus/chain_20/steps_to_first_reward/std} and \rhval{main/rnd-bonus/room_4/steps_to_first_reward/std} steps; Tables~\ref{tab:main} and~\ref{tab:tv}). The observation count is not delayed either (\rhval{main/count-bonus-obs/chain_20_tv/steps_to_first_reward/mean} against \rhval{main/count-bonus-obs/chain_20/steps_to_first_reward/mean} and \rhval{main/count-bonus-obs/room_4_tv/steps_to_first_reward/mean} against \rhval{main/count-bonus-obs/room_4/steps_to_first_reward/mean} steps, again within one standard deviation of the twin). For RND this is the expected outcome, not a surprise: its target is a deterministic function of the observation and our token set is finite, so both the RND error and the observation count on the TV decay with visits \cite{burda2018exploration}. The systems that see the token do spend slightly more time on the TV than the state count on room\_4\_tv (\rhval{main/rnd-bonus/room_4_tv/tv_time_frac/mean}\,$\pm$\,\rhval{main/rnd-bonus/room_4_tv/tv_time_frac/std} for RND and \rhval{main/count-bonus-obs/room_4_tv/tv_time_frac/mean}\,$\pm$\,\rhval{main/count-bonus-obs/room_4_tv/tv_time_frac/std} for the observation count against \rhval{main/count-bonus-state/room_4_tv/tv_time_frac/mean}\,$\pm$\,\rhval{main/count-bonus-state/room_4_tv/tv_time_frac/std}), and the $K$ sweep in Section~\ref{sec:ablations} confirms that this share depends on the token. The large TV share of $\epsilon$-greedy on chain\_20\_tv (\rhval{main/epsilon-greedy-q-learning/chain_20_tv/tv_time_frac/mean}) is unrelated to novelty: the TV cell is next to the start, where a random walk stays.

\textbf{What the numbers do not show.} They do not show that count or RND bonuses are useless in general: the tasks are deterministic and small, so optimism alone explores systematically. Nor can they show a noisy-TV failure: forward-model curiosity is not among our systems.

\textbf{Tasks.} \emph{chain\_N} ($N\in\{10,20,40\}$): states $0..N-1$, actions left/right/stay, start at 0, reward 1 at $N-1$ (terminal), episode limit $2N$. \emph{room\_R} ($R\in\{2,4,6\}$): $R$ rooms of $5\times5$ cells in a row, one door in each dividing wall at varying heights, start top-left, goal bottom-right, five actions (four moves and stay), episode limit $12R+16$. \emph{chain\_20\_tv} and \emph{room\_4\_tv} add the noisy-TV cell (cell 1 of the chain; cell $(2,2)$ of the first room). The environments are deterministic apart from the TV token. Budgets: 30k steps (chains), 100k steps (rooms).

\textbf{Systems.} $\epsilon$-greedy Q-learning, Count bonus (state), Count bonus (obs) and the RND bonus, plus the two offset controls and the four normaliser variants of Section~\ref{sec:method}, all reimplemented in numpy. Every system uses the same $\alpha$, $\gamma$, $\epsilon$, $\beta$, $K$ and step budget. Nothing was tuned per system or task or on the evaluation seeds: the offset was introduced after the seed-100 pilot, and the RND guard ($W=64$, $c=5$) was fixed a priori when an audit of the first version found the division by a zero $\sigma_e$ (Section~\ref{sec:ablations}); the clip sweep shows how much that choice matters.

\textbf{Metrics.} Steps to first reward (set to the budget if never found, hence censored; lower is better), reward-found indicator, final return (mean return of the last 20\% of training episodes), greedy success (the greedy policy reaches the goal), TV time fraction (share of steps spent on the TV cell) and coverage. Every run also logs diagnostics of the raw bonus $b$ (maximum, maximum over the first 100 steps, median, share of steps with $b>1$, share of clipped steps) and the largest $|Q|$ written.

\textbf{Protocol.} Five seeds (0--4) per cell; tables show mean $\pm$ sample standard deviation over seeds. There is no train/test split: all metrics are online training metrics plus one greedy rollout. Tests are Welch and paired $t$-tests over seeds (\texttt{rh compare} between systems, scipy for the twin and $K$ comparisons), uncorrected for multiple tasks and computed on budget-censored values, so we treat them as descriptive. All runs come from one commit of the code; sweep cells at the default value reuse the main runs. Hardware: one laptop CPU, numpy, two processes at a time, under 9\,s per run and about 20 CPU-minutes for all 492 runs.

\textbf{Hypotheses} (registered before the runs, with their refutation criteria). H1: without noise both bonuses reach the first reward faster than $\epsilon$-greedy on the larger tasks (chain\_40, room\_6); refuted if $\epsilon$-greedy is not slower. H2: without noise exact counts are at least as fast as RND (paired comparison); refuted if RND is faster. H3: the TV raises the time RND spends on it and delays its first reward relative to the noise-free twin, while the state count is unaffected; refuted if RND's time to first reward on the TV tasks is not above the twin's. H4: TV distraction depends on the token cardinality $K\in\{1,4,16,64\}$; refuted if neither TV time nor time to first reward varies with $K$.

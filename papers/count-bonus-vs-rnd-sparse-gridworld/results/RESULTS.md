# Results (5 seeds per cell; all tables in results/tables/, rebuilt by experiments/build_results.sh)

Sources: `main_compact.md` (main table and normaliser variants), `main.md` (rh table, long format, all ten systems), `tests.md` and `compare_steps_ref_*.csv` (rh compare), `tv_frac.md`, `abl_diag.md`, `abl_diag_per_run.md`, `sweep_*_perconfig.md`.

- **H1 (both bonuses faster than epsilon-greedy on chain_40, room_6): supported as stated, but neither bonus is the cause.** Count bonus: 5/5 seeds on all tasks; epsilon-greedy 0/5 on chain_40, room_4, room_6. RND (guarded): 5242 steps on chain_40 (Welch p < 0.001 against epsilon-greedy), 76276 on room_6 with 4/5 seeds (p = 0.067). Controls: "Step penalty only" (same offset, no bonus) finds the reward in 5/5 on all tasks and is within one std of the count bonus on all six noise-free tasks (Welch p >= 0.288); "Count bonus (state), no offset" finds nothing on chain_40, room_4, room_6. Exception: on room_4_tv count (state) is faster than penalty-only (11643 vs 13231, p < 0.001 uncorrected), read with caution because penalty-only, which cannot see the token, differs from its own twin there (p = 0.011).
- **H2 (counts at least as fast as RND, noise-free; registered test: paired comparison): supported**, paired p < 0.05 on all six noise-free tasks. Not evidence for the count signal: penalty-only is also faster than RND on every task (Welch p < 0.05 on five of six noise-free tasks, 0.062 on chain_10).
- **H3 (TV delays RND, not the state count): not supported.** RND 2387 (chain_20_tv) vs 2146 (chain_20), p = 0.324; 15184 (room_4_tv) vs 18435 (room_4), p = 0.219. Expected for RND (deterministic target, finite token set). TV share on room_4_tv: RND 0.025, count (obs) 0.023, count (state) 0.013.
- **H4 (dependence on K): supported for TV time, not for time to reward.** TV share rises monotonically with K: RND 0.016 -> 0.029, count (obs) 0.013 -> 0.027 (Welch p < 0.001, K=1 vs 64); steps to first reward: p = 0.406 and 0.511.
- **RND normaliser ablation (all eight tasks).** Unguarded normaliser (v1, the first version's method): 20 of 40 runs have a bonus above 1e4 in the first 100 steps (up to 1.44e7, |Q| up to 3.62e6); 1 of those 20 finds the reward, all 20 others do. Clip only: 39/40 found, warm-up only: 38/40, guarded (both): 39/40; no spikes. No normalisation: bonus at most 0.44, behaves like penalty-only, 40/40. All normalised variants are slower than penalty-only on every task.
- **Clip sweep** (chain_20, room_4): c=2: 1730 / 15396 steps; c=5: 2146 / 18435; c=20 and none: 2051 / 19474; 5/5 everywhere; all slower than penalty-only (618 / 11921).
- **Beta sweep** (room_4): steps to first reward identical for all beta for both systems (by construction).
- **Verdict** (`VERDICT.md`): tier 0, the method (RND bonus) is not best on any task; this is the reported, negative outcome.
- **Pilot** (seed 100, group `pilot`): without the offset neither count bonus nor RND (guarded or unguarded) finds the reward on chain_40 or room_6.

History: the 351 runs of the first two rounds are superseded rows in `results/runs.jsonl` (not used in any table). Their RND rows used the unguarded normaliser; the "RND, unguarded normaliser (v1)" rows reproduce their numbers exactly.

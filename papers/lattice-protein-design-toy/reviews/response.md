# Response to audit findings

1. **Major, augmentation ablation at data_frac=1.0.** Accepted. Added a distinct-pair metric (`--report_distinct`, off by default so old runs are unchanged) and re-ran the ablation at data fractions 0.1/0.25/0.5 (group `abl_aug_frac`, 30 runs) plus a step-matched control (no augmentation, 120 epochs, fractions 0.1-1.0; group `abl_aug_steps`, 20 runs); paired analysis in `method/aug_analysis.py` (group `analysis_aug`, new Table V). The paper now says that augmentation is exact duplication at fraction 1.0, that the registered ablation measures training length (confirmed by the step-matched control: indistinguishable from the "augmented" model), reports distinct pair counts, and presents the small-data follow-up as post hoc: augmentation helps K=1 at fractions 0.1 and 0.25 (uncorrected p<0.05), not at 0.5, and does not change K=100 at equal epochs. The H5a verdict was changed from "not supported" to "uninformative at full data, partly supported post hoc at small data" (RESULTS.md, ablations, abstract, conclusion). The registered test result is still reported.

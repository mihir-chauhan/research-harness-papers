# Results (details: paper/main.pdf, tables in results/tables/)
- H1 supported: FNO 0.0049 vs MLP 0.0834, DeepONet 0.2192, CNN 0.2229 (5 seeds; registered test = Welch, p <= 1.4e-5 in every comparison; table h1.tex, `rh compare --metric rel_l2 --group main`).
  DeepONet and CNN are under-fit at 100 epochs; the DeepONet result does not reproduce the near-parity with FNO that Lu et al. (2021) report for simple settings and is a lower bound on DeepONet quality. The CNN's receptive field (49 of 128 points) is an untested second candidate cause of its error.
- H2 supported as stated (FNO ratio 1.00, CNN 2.55 at 256 / 3.27 at 512), but easy test (band-limited data); MLP/DeepONet ratio 1.00 is an artefact of stride-subsampling, so that part of the prediction is refuted.
- H3 supported: 8 -> 16 modes changes the mean by about 1%; 4 modes is 36% worse (Welch p 0.013, 3 seeds); 8/16/32 within noise (ablsum.tex / ablsum.md).
- H4 supported at n=100,200,400 (3 seeds), at a fixed 100-epoch budget (fewer pairs = fewer gradient steps; no matched-step control).
- Training length: DeepONet 0.2173 -> 0.1314 (500 ep) -> 0.1276 (2000 ep) while train error falls to 0.0417, i.e. it overfits; MLP at 500/2000 epochs is within noise of 100 epochs (Welch p 0.314 / 0.145). CNN not run longer (per-run time limit).
- Grid channel: no measurable effect (3 seeds, Welch p 0.279).
- Tuning: 4 learning rates x 4 systems on seed 100 (tune.tex). CNN at 1e-2 diverges (run added in the revision).

# Results (details: paper/main.pdf, tables in results/tables/)
- H1 supported: FNO 0.0049 vs MLP 0.0834, DeepONet 0.2192, CNN 0.2229 (5 seeds; registered test = Welch, p <= 1.4e-5 in every comparison; table h1.tex, `rh compare --metric rel_l2 --group main`).
  DeepONet and CNN are under-fit at 100 epochs; the DeepONet result does not reproduce the near-parity with FNO that Lu et al. (2021) report for simple settings and is a lower bound on DeepONet quality. The CNN's receptive field (49 of 128 points) is an untested second candidate cause of its error.
- H2 supported as stated (FNO error unchanged on the finer grids; CNN mean error 0.2229 at 128, 0.5679 at 256, 0.7270 at 512, and its smallest per-seed 256-point error is above 1.5x its largest per-seed 128-point error), but easy test (band-limited data); MLP/DeepONet ratio 1.00 is an artefact of stride-subsampling, so that part of the prediction is refuted.
- H3 supported: 8 -> 16 modes changes the mean by under 1%; the 4-mode error is 1.36x the 16-mode error (Welch p 0.013, 3 seeds); 8/16/32 within noise (`rh compare --group sweep_modes --metric rel_l2 --ref FNO`, compare_sweep_modes_rel_l2.csv).
- H4 supported at n=100,200,400 (3 seeds), at a fixed 100-epoch budget (fewer pairs = fewer gradient steps; no matched-step control).
- Training length: DeepONet 0.2173 -> 0.1314 (500 ep) -> 0.1276 (2000 ep) while train error falls to 0.0417, i.e. it overfits; MLP at 500/2000 epochs is within noise of 100 epochs (Welch p 0.314 / 0.145). CNN not run longer (per-run time limit).
- Grid channel: no measurable effect (3 seeds, Welch p 0.279).
- Tuning: 4 learning rates x 4 systems on seed 100 (tune.tex). CNN at 1e-2 diverges (run added in the revision).

## Notes on VERDICT.md (generated by `rh verdict`)
- Its p-value for the main comparison is the tool's paired t-test (same seeds); the test registered in proposal.md is the Welch test, reported in h1.tex. Both are far below 0.05.
- The copy of VERDICT.md in this directory predates the conform pass: its ablation rows read "missing" because the reference FNO runs were then only in group `main`. In the conform pass the seed 0-2 default runs of FNO, DeepONet and MLP were copied into the ablation groups with `rh log --from-run` (same command and metrics, `copied_from` set), and the ratios and Welch p-values against the default rows now come from `rh compare` (results/tables/compare_<group>_rel_l2.csv; reference FNO for sweep_modes, abl_grid and sweep_ntrain, MLP for sweep_epochs). results/tables/ablsum.{tex,md} and ratios.tex, which were computed by experiments/make_figs.py, were removed. `rh verdict` was not re-run.

# Design
- `data.py`: ETDRK4 (Kassam-Trefethen contour integrals, M=32) pseudo-spectral Burgers solver, dt=1e-3, 2/3 dealiasing, 512 points. Self-check (`python method/data.py`): dt halving changes the answer by 5e-11 relative; 256- vs 512-point solves agree to 4e-15, 128 vs 512 to 3e-9, i.e. all three grids resolve the solution (no solver error confound).
- ICs: sum_{k=1..63} s_k (a_k cos 2 pi k x + b_k sin 2 pi k x), a,b ~ N(0,1), s_k = 25 sqrt(2)/((2 pi k)^2+25). Band-limited so 128/256/512 grids contain it exactly.
- `run.py`: FNO1d (lift 2->32, 4x[spectral conv 16 modes + 1x1 conv skip], GELU after the first three of these layers and none after the fourth, head 32->128->1; 139,745 real parameters counting complex weights twice), DeepONet (branch 128-128-128-64, trunk 1-128-128-64, bias; 66,305), MLP (128-512-512-128; 394,368), CNN (6 conv layers, 32 ch, k=9, circular, GELU after all but the last, receptive field 49 of 128 points; 37,601).
- Inputs and outputs divided/multiplied by one scalar (training-set std of u0). Loss: per-sample relative L2. Adam (wd 1e-5), one-cycle schedule (10% warm-up), batch 20, 100 epochs; validation every 10 epochs, best-validation checkpoint restored.

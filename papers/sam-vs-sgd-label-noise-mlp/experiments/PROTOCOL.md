Driver: experiments/drive.py; analysis: experiments/analyse.py. Groups: tune, main, sweep_rho, sweep_wd, abl_sam.

Budget is matched in epochs (150), not gradient evaluations: SAM and its ablations use two per step. Tests: Welch and paired t-test on test_acc via `rh compare` (registered); Holm-adjusted paired p over the 8 comparisons is reported in addition.

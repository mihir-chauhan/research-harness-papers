Tasks digits_n{0,0.2,0.4}, spirals_n0.2; splits 600/300/897 (digits), 400/200/400 (spirals) per seed; evaluation seeds 0-4, tuning seeds 100-102 (val accuracy on noisy val labels); grids rho {0.02,0.05,0.1,0.2,0.5,1.0}, wd {1e-4,1e-3,1e-2,5e-2}; lr 0.1 fixed, momentum 0.9, cosine, batch 100, 150 epochs; CPU (Apple M1 Pro, 2 threads). Driver: experiments/drive.py; analysis: experiments/analyse.py. Groups: tune, main, sweep_rho, sweep_wd, abl_sam.

Budget is matched in epochs (150), not gradient evaluations: SAM and its ablations use two per step. Tuning budget: SAM 6 rho values, SGD+WD 4 wd values, 3 tuning seeds each (120 runs); SGD has nothing tuned. On digits, tuning seeds 100-102 re-split the same 1797 images as evaluation seeds 0-4, so tuning train/val images overlap evaluation test images (spirals data are generated fresh per seed). Tests: Welch and paired t-test on test_acc via `rh compare` (registered); Holm-adjusted paired p over the 8 comparisons is reported in addition.

Derived statistics (group `derived`, 22 rows, logged with `$PY experiments/drive.py derived`, one `rh run` of `experiments/derive.py` per row): Spearman correlations per subset (`corr all`, `corr no-collapsed`, `corr SAM` per task; `corr pooled all`, `corr pooled no-collapsed`, `corr pooled SAM digits`) and, per task and baseline, the per-seed differences SAM minus baseline and the Holm-adjusted paired p (`SAM minus SGD`, `SAM minus SGD+WD`). They are computed from the evaluation-seed rows of the registry only and are deterministic. A registry row needs a task: the three pooled correlation rows are filed under the primary task digits_n0.4 (config `pooled_over` and the row note say what they pool). Collapse thresholds of the exploratory "no collapsed" rows: test accuracy below 0.15 (digits) or 0.55 (spirals).

Reproducibility: every result metric is fixed by the seed (split, noise, initialisation, batch order, sharpness directions); `runtime_s` is wall-clock and declared `nondeterministic: true` in research.yaml.

Rebuild: `rh compare --metric test_acc --group main`, then `$PY experiments/analyse.py` (writes the figures and the tables; every result cell of a table is a `\rhval{<key>}` macro filled from the registry at build time, nothing is typed), then `rh paper build`. Setup facts stated in the paper (split sizes, epochs, width, ...) are declared in research.yaml `constants` (`rh const list`).

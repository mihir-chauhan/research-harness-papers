\textbf{H1 (RBM-SR $\alpha{=}2$ beats MF and Jastrow): supported by the registered tests; at small fields the margin over Jastrow is not an ansatz effect.} The mean $\epsilon_E$ of RBM $\alpha{=}2$ SR is below that of MF-SR and Jastrow-SR in all 13 tasks, and it wins 65 of 65 paired seeds against MF-SR and 64 of 65 against Jastrow-SR (Table~\ref{tab:wins}). The paired test gives $p<0.05$ in all 13 tasks against Jastrow-SR and in 12 against MF-SR ($p=$ \rhval{cmp/selected/mean-field-sr/tfim_n10_g0.50/rel_energy_error/paired_p:3} at 10/0.5); with $n=5$ and uncorrected tests these are indicative only. The ratio of means to Jastrow is \rhval{cmp/selected/jastrow-sr/tfim_n10_g0.50/rel_energy_error/inv_ratio:2} to \rhval{cmp/selected/jastrow-sr/tfim_n8_g0.50/rel_energy_error/inv_ratio:2} in the four $\Gamma\le0.5$ tasks, \rhval{cmp/selected/jastrow-sr/tfim_n10_g2.00/rel_energy_error/inv_ratio:2} at 10/2, \rhval{cmp/selected/jastrow-sr/tfim_n10_g0.75/rel_energy_error/inv_ratio:2} at 10/0.75, and \rhval{cmp/selected/jastrow-sr/tfim_n12_g1.50/rel_energy_error/inv_ratio:2} to \rhval{cmp/selected/jastrow-sr/tfim_n8_g1.00/rel_energy_error/inv_ratio:0} in the seven tasks with $\Gamma$ between 1 and 1.5. The L-BFGS rows of Table~\ref{tab:energy} qualify these margins. For $\Gamma\ge0.75$ the Jastrow-SR error is close to the best L-BFGS value of the Jastrow ansatz (at 10/1, \rhval{selected/jastrow-sr/tfim_n10_g1.00/rel_energy_error/median:sci1} against \rhval{exact_opt/jastrow-exact-gradient/tfim_n10_g1.00/rel_energy_error/min:sci1}; the largest gap is at 12/1, \rhval{selected/jastrow-sr/tfim_n12_g1.00/rel_energy_error/median:sci1} against \rhval{exact_opt/jastrow-exact-gradient/tfim_n12_g1.00/rel_energy_error/min:sci1}), and the RBM-SR median is below that value in all nine such tasks: better optimisation of this Jastrow form would not close the gap. For $\Gamma\le0.5$ the Jastrow-SR error is above its L-BFGS value, slightly at 8/0.5 and by more than two orders of magnitude at 10/0.25 (\rhval{selected/jastrow-sr/tfim_n10_g0.25/rel_energy_error/median:sci1} against \rhval{exact_opt/jastrow-exact-gradient/tfim_n10_g0.25/rel_energy_error/min:sci1}), and that L-BFGS value is below the RBM-SR median in three of the four tasks (not at 8/0.5). In those three tasks the ordering of the two VMC results reflects optimisation and sampling under this budget, not what the Jastrow ansatz can represent. The MF median is close to its L-BFGS value in every task (largest gap at 10/0.5, \rhval{selected/mean-field-sr/tfim_n10_g0.50/rel_energy_error/median:sci1} against \rhval{exact_opt/mean-field-exact-gradient/tfim_n10_g0.50/rel_energy_error/min:sci1}): it is limited by the ansatz.

\textbf{H2 (SR beats SGD, RBM $\alpha{=}2$): supported.} The SGD-to-SR ratio of mean errors exceeds one in all 13 tasks (Table~\ref{tab:wins}): \rhval{cmp/selected/rbm-alpha-2-sgd/tfim_n10_g0.25/rel_energy_error/inv_ratio:2} at 10/0.25, \rhval{cmp/selected/rbm-alpha-2-sgd/tfim_n8_g0.50/rel_energy_error/inv_ratio:0} to \rhval{cmp/selected/rbm-alpha-2-sgd/tfim_n12_g0.50/rel_energy_error/inv_ratio:0} in the four tasks at $\Gamma{=}0.5$ and $0.75$, and \rhval{cmp/selected/rbm-alpha-2-sgd/tfim_n12_g1.00/rel_energy_error/inv_ratio:0} to \rhval{cmp/selected/rbm-alpha-2-sgd/tfim_n10_g1.50/rel_energy_error/inv_ratio:0} in the eight tasks with $\Gamma\ge1$; SR wins 65 of 65 paired seeds, with $p$ at most \rhval{cmp/selected/rbm-alpha-2-sgd/tfim_n8_g1.00/rel_energy_error/paired_p:4} (at 8/1) in every task. The cost is robustness from start S, where SR diverged in 12 of 65 runs ($\alpha{=}2$) and 16 of 65 ($\alpha{=}4$); no SGD run and no run from start B diverged (Table~\ref{tab:check}). For MF and Jastrow the optimisers differ far less; the largest gap is for Jastrow at 12/0.5, \rhval{selected/jastrow-sgd/tfim_n12_g0.50/rel_energy_error/median:sci1} with SGD against \rhval{selected/jastrow-sr/tfim_n12_g0.50/rel_energy_error/median:sci1} with SR (Table~\ref{tab:energy}).

\textbf{H3 (error decreases with $\alpha$ at $\Gamma{=}1$ with SR): supported, with overlapping seed ranges.} Mean and median $\epsilon_E$ are ordered $\alpha{=}1>2>4$ for $N=8$, $10$ and $12$ (Table~\ref{tab:alpha}). Not every step exceeds the seed spread: at $N{=}12$ the lowest $\alpha{=}1$ seed (\rhval{selected/rbm-alpha-1-sr/tfim_n12_g1.00/rel_energy_error/min:sci1}) is below the highest $\alpha{=}2$ seed (\rhval{selected/rbm-alpha-2-sr/tfim_n12_g1.00/rel_energy_error/max:sci1}), and at $N{=}10$ the ranges of $\alpha{=}2$ and $4$ overlap. So the method of record is not the best system at the critical field: RBM $\alpha{=}4$ SR is lower on 5 of 5 seeds at $\Gamma{=}1$ for each $N$, and over all tasks $\alpha{=}2$ wins 31 of 65 seeds against it (Table~\ref{tab:wins}). The ordering is not general: at 10/2 $\alpha{=}1$ has the lowest median (Table~\ref{tab:energy}). Nor is it a capacity limit: at 10/1 L-BFGS reaches \rhval{exact_opt/rbm-alpha-2-exact-gradient/tfim_n10_g1.00/rel_energy_error/min:sci1} with $\alpha{=}2$, below every value in Table~\ref{tab:alpha}, so a larger $\alpha$ may ease the stochastic optimisation rather than add needed capacity; we did not test this.

\textbf{H4 (MF errors peak near $\Gamma{=}1$): refuted for energy.} At $N{=}10$ the median MF energy error rises up to $\Gamma{=}1.5$ (\rhval{selected/mean-field-sr/tfim_n10_g1.50/rel_energy_error/median:sci1} with SR) and is slightly lower at $\Gamma{=}2$ (Table~\ref{tab:energy}, Fig.~\ref{fig:gamma}a): the maximum is more than $0.25$ from the critical field. The L-BFGS reference has the same maximum, so this is a property of the product state, not of the optimiser. The MF $|M_z|$ error peaks at $\Gamma{=}1.25$ (Table~\ref{tab:mz}), on the boundary of the registered window.

\textbf{H5 (RBM-SR error does not grow with $N$): refuted.} At $\Gamma{=}1$ the mean $\epsilon_E$ of RBM $\alpha{=}2$ SR is \rhval{selected/rbm-alpha-2-sr/tfim_n8_g1.00/rel_energy_error/mean:sci1}, \rhval{selected/rbm-alpha-2-sr/tfim_n10_g1.00/rel_energy_error/mean:sci1} and \rhval{selected/rbm-alpha-2-sr/tfim_n12_g1.00/rel_energy_error/mean:sci1} for $N=8$, $10$, $12$, and $\alpha{=}1$ and $4$ grow likewise (Table~\ref{tab:alpha}). With three sizes and a fixed budget we cannot separate ansatz, budget and sampler.

\textbf{Magnetisation and fidelity: where low energy misleads.} Magnetisation errors are far larger than energy errors: for RBM $\alpha{=}2$ SR at 10/1, $\epsilon_{M_z}=$ \rhval{selected/rbm-alpha-2-sr/tfim_n10_g1.00/mz_error/median:sci1} against $\epsilon_E=$ \rhval{selected/rbm-alpha-2-sr/tfim_n10_g1.00/rel_energy_error/median:sci1} (Tables~\ref{tab:mz},~\ref{tab:energy}). At 10/1 the MF-SR states have a median infidelity of \rhval{selected/mean-field-sr/tfim_n10_g1.00/infidelity/median:2} and the RBM $\alpha{=}2$ SGD states \rhval{selected/rbm-alpha-2-sgd/tfim_n10_g1.00/infidelity/median:2} (Table~\ref{tab:select}). In the ordered phase the selected states fail in a different way: Jastrow-SR is selected from start B on all five seeds for $\Gamma\le0.75$ with $1-F=$ \rhval{selected/jastrow-sr/tfim_n10_g0.50/infidelity/median:2}, and the RBMs at $\Gamma{=}0.5$ are selected from B on four of five seeds with the same median infidelity. These states have a median $\epsilon_E$ of at most \rhval{selected/rbm-alpha-1-sr/tfim_n10_g0.50/rel_energy_error/median:sci1} at $\Gamma{=}0.5$ but overlap with only half of the symmetric finite-$N$ ground state; they may approximate one of the two ordered configurations, whose energies are nearly degenerate at small $\Gamma$. Energy is a poor selection criterion in this regime, and $\langle|M_z|\rangle$ does not detect the difference.

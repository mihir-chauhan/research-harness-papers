# Results (final protocol: v3 rates, two starts; tables in results/tables/t_*.tex, built by experiments/make_tables.py from results/runs.jsonl)

Primary metric: relative energy error, exact enumeration of the trained state. Every (system, task, seed) is run from a symmetric start S (group `main`) and a symmetry-broken start B (group `main_b`); the reported ("selected") state is the lower-energy start. Per-start rows: `t_start.tex`, `main.md`, `main_b.md`. Sampling-free L-BFGS reference: last three rows of `t_energy.tex` (group `exact_opt`).

- H1 (RBM alpha=2 SR beats MF and Jastrow with SR on every task): supported by the registered tests on selected states (`t_wins.tex`): mean error ratio above 1 in 13/13 tasks against both; paired seeds won 65/65 vs MF-SR and 64/65 vs Jastrow-SR; paired t-test p<0.05 in 13/13 tasks vs Jastrow-SR and 12/13 vs MF-SR (p=0.207 at N10 g0.5), uncorrected, n=5. The margin over Jastrow is 8.55-71x for Gamma in [1, 1.5], 3.80x at N10 g0.75, but only 1.47-2.37x in the four Gamma<=0.5 tasks and 1.59x at N10 g2. For Gamma<=0.5 the Jastrow VMC result is above the Jastrow L-BFGS value (slightly at N8 g0.5: 1.4e-4 vs 1.0e-4; by more than two orders at N10 g0.25: 5.6e-5 vs 2.5e-7), and that L-BFGS value is below the RBM-SR VMC median in 3 of the 4 tasks (not N8 g0.5): in those three tasks the small-field margin is an optimisation effect, not an ansatz effect. For Gamma>=0.75 Jastrow VMC is within about 30% of its L-BFGS value and RBM-SR is below that value in all nine tasks.
  - From start S alone (the registered single-start protocol) Jastrow is stuck far above its start-B result for Gamma<=0.75 and above mean-field for Gamma<=0.5; the first version of this study reported those start-S numbers and overstated the margin.
- H2 (SR beats SGD, RBM alpha=2): supported: SGD/SR mean error ratio 2.07 at N10 g0.25, 15-23 at Gamma 0.5 and 0.75, 68-496 for Gamma>=1; 65/65 paired seeds; p<=0.015 in every task (`t_wins.tex`). SR diverged in 12/65 (alpha=2) and 16/65 (alpha=4) runs from start S, 0 from start B; no SGD run diverged (`t_check.tex`).
- H3 (monotone in alpha at Gamma=1, SR): supported on means and medians for N=8, 10, 12 (`t_alpha.tex`); seed ranges overlap for alpha=1 vs 2 at N=12 and alpha=2 vs 4 at N=10. RBM alpha=4 SR beats the method of record on 5/5 seeds at Gamma=1 for every N (`t_wins.tex`), so `rh verdict` is tier 0. Not monotone off the critical field (N10 g2: alpha=1 lowest).
- H4 (MF errors peak near Gamma=1): refuted for energy (median MF error largest at Gamma=1.5 for N=10, also in the L-BFGS reference); |Mz| error peaks at Gamma=1.25, the boundary of the registered window (`t_n10_mz.tex`).
- H5 (no growth with N): refuted for RBM SR at Gamma=1 (mean error grows 8 -> 10 -> 12 for alpha=1, 2, 4; `t_alpha.tex`).
- Failure modes: RBM-SR divergence from start S at the selected rates; mean-field domain-wall states from start S (N12 g0.5 median 2.3e-1); Jastrow stuck near the symmetric point from start S at small fields; selected states in the ordered phase are symmetry-broken with infidelity 0.5 (`t_select.tex`); RBM VMC errors are 3-400x above the (non-converged) L-BFGS values, i.e. limited by optimiser and sample budget.
- `rh compare` outputs per start: `compare_main_rel_energy_error.csv` (start S; NaN where a seed diverged), `compare_main_b_rel_energy_error.csv` (start B).

# Brief: derivative source for SINDy on noisy Lorenz-63

(Seed text retained in git history; see proposal.md for hypotheses H1-H5.)

**Question.** Holding library (degree-2 polynomials, 10 terms x 3 equations) and solver (STLSQ in numpy) fixed, how does the derivative source (finite differences, Savitzky-Golay, smoothing spline, TV-regularised differentiation, weak form) affect exact support recovery and coefficient error versus noise?

**Decisions taken (no human available).** Spline and TV are both included. Data: Lorenz (10, 28, 8/3), 5 time-unit burn-in, 10 time units recorded at dt=0.01 (1001 samples; dt=0.002 of the registry quick study would make dense TV differentiation too slow), Gaussian noise with std = level x per-component std of the clean signal, levels 0, 0.5, 1, 2, 5, 10 %. 20 seeds (0-19) per system and level; each seed = new initial condition + new noise. Hyperparameters (derivative smoothing parameter and STLSQ threshold) chosen per system and noise level on 5 separate tuning trajectories (seeds 1000-1004) using ground truth (oracle-assisted, equal for all systems). Selection rule: support rate, then median coefficient error, then the largest threshold (revised after the first audit; the first version broke ties by grid order). A tie is equal support rate and equal median error; in 2 of the 19 tie cells (FD at 5 %, spline at 10 %) the tied thresholds give different models on the tuning trajectories (method/tie_check.py). Metrics: support_exact (rate), coef_err (relative L2 over 30 coefficients), coef_err_oracle (least squares on the true support, same regression problem), false_pos, false_neg, runtime_s.
**Method / baselines.** Weak-form STLSQ vs FD, SG, Spline, TV (all reimplemented). **Ablations.** weak window width sweep, test-function exponent, library on smoothed vs raw state, threshold grid on the test seeds (diagnostic), **out of scope**: ensembles, other systems, implicit forms, noise-blind tuning.

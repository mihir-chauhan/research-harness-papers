The ablation compares tuning protocols for the ridge (Table~\ref{tab:h4}, columns Tuned, Global, LOO, val50), and the ridge sweep of Section~\ref{sec:results} is the sensitivity study.

\textbf{H5 (other protocols also remove the hump; exploratory) -- mixed.} GlobalRidge has a mean hump below $0.05$ on all tasks, and below that of TunedRidge on seven of eight tasks (digits\_n0 is the exception, \rhval{main/globalridge/digits_n0/bump_rel/mean:4} against \rhval{main/tunedridge/digits_n0/bump_rel/mean:4}), so on these tasks a single $\lambda$ shared across widths is enough and per-width tuning brings no visible benefit; the final errors of the two are nearly identical (Table~\ref{tab:main}). Inside the threshold window $0.4\le N/n\le4$ the mean selected $\log_{10}\lambda$ moves little (Fig.~\ref{fig:lam}), which may be why per-width selection adds little there; across the full grid it varies considerably more on most tasks (Fig.~\ref{fig:lam}; e.g.\ on synth\_n0 the mean selected $\log_{10}\lambda$ is \rhval{main/tunedridge/synth_n0/log10_lam_thresh/mean:1} at $N/n=1$ and \rhval{main/tunedridge/synth_n0/log10_lam_final/mean:1} at $N/n=25$, and larger still at the smallest widths), and we did not isolate why per-width selection does not help. Tuning on 50 validation points (val50) gives a mean hump above the threshold on synth\_n0.25 (\rhval{abl_tuning/tunedridge-val50/synth_n0.25/bump_rel/mean:4}, essentially at it) and synth\_n1 (\rhval{abl_tuning/tunedridge-val50/synth_n1/bump_rel/mean:4}), and leave-one-out tuning on synth\_n1 (\rhval{abl_tuning/tunedridge-loo/synth_n1/bump_rel/mean:4}); synth\_n1 is the noisiest task, while synth\_n0.25 is only at the threshold with a large seed sd (\rhval{abl_tuning/tunedridge-val50/synth_n0.25/bump_rel/std:3}); that selecting among 15 values from few or noisy labels is less reliable is a possible mechanism we did not test. Their variance across seeds is large (Table~\ref{tab:h4} gives means only; the per-seed values are in \texttt{results/analysis.md}), so with 5 seeds the ranking among TunedRidge, GlobalRidge and LOO is not resolved.

\textbf{Where the method fails.} (a) At zero or small noise on digits, tuned and untuned interpolation reach the same final error (Table~\ref{tab:main}, digits\_n0), so tuning only removes the peak, it does not improve the large-width solution. (b) A large fixed ridge has no peak but a high, flat curve (Table~\ref{tab:sweep}, $\lambda\ge10^{-1}$ gives larger window maxima than $10^{-3}$). (c) On individual seeds the tuned hump reaches \rhval{main/tunedridge/digits_n0.15/bump_rel/max:2} of the best error, which is small in absolute terms but not zero; the hump statistic is relative to the best error and small values on a flat curve are inside the noise of the validation selection.

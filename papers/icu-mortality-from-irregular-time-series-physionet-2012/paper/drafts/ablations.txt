\subsection{Decay ablation}
Table~\ref{tab:abl} (top block) switches off the two decay mechanisms of GRU-D, holding everything else (data, folds, seeds, hyper-parameters) fixed. Removing input decay, hidden decay or both changes the AUROC by <<A d_noin_grud_auroc 4>>, <<A d_nohid_grud_auroc 4>> and <<A d_nodecay_grud_auroc 4>> respectively, all with paired $p>\rhval{const/alpha:2}$ (<<A p_noin_grud_auroc 4>>, <<A p_nohid_grud_auroc 4>>, <<A p_nodecay_grud_auroc 4>>). The variants are thus statistically indistinguishable from the full model on this cohort, and if anything the variants without decay are nominally slightly better. We conclude that at the 48 h horizon, with hourly bins and this tuning budget, the decay mechanism contributes no measurable AUROC; it does not show that decay is useless on larger cohorts or with continuous-time inputs. Note that ``w/o any decay'' differs from the mask+delta GRU in the missing delta input and in the explicit GRU-D input form, and the two are also indistinguishable in Table~\ref{tab:paired}.

\subsection{Do missingness counts matter for the summary models? (H4)}
The bottom blocks of Table~\ref{tab:abl} remove the per-variable observation counts from the summary features. For LR the AUROC drops by <<A d_lrnocnt_lr_auroc 4>> ($p=$<<A p_lrnocnt_lr_auroc 4>>, lower in all five seeds), AUPRC by <<A d_lrnocnt_lr_auprc 4>> and Brier gets worse by <<A d_lrnocnt_lr_brier 4>>. For GBDT the change is <<A d_gbnocnt_gb_auroc 4>> AUROC ($p=$<<A p_gbnocnt_gb_auroc 4>>), not distinguishable from zero. H4 is therefore supported for LR and not for GBDT. A plausible reading is that trees can recover missingness from the native NaN handling of the other summaries (a tree can split on ``is missing''), so the explicit counts are redundant for them; we did not run the experiment that would isolate this (e.g.\ GBDT with imputed values and no counts). The signal in counts is also small in absolute terms: the AUROC of the total number of observations alone is only \rhval{data/cohort-statistics/physionet2012_mortality/auroc_total_count_only/mean:3} (Section~\ref{sec:setup}), which includes the ICU-type effect on measurement frequency.

\subsection{Observation horizon (H5, exploratory)}
To see whether the conclusions depend on how much of the record is available, we truncate the series to the first \rhval{const/h12} or \rhval{const/h24} hours and rerun every system with the same tuned hyper-parameters (no re-tuning, which favours none in particular but may not be optimal for the shorter inputs). Table~\ref{tab:horizon} and Figure~\ref{fig:horizon} show that all systems degrade as the horizon shrinks and that GBDT remains first at every horizon. Two features of the ranking do change. First, the gain from missingness-aware inputs over forward-filling grows when data are scarce: at \rhval{const/h12}\,h GRU-D exceeds the forward-fill GRU by <<A d_h12_grud_ffill_auroc 4>> AUROC ($p=$<<A p_h12_grud_ffill_auroc 4>>, better in <<A w_h12_grud_ffill_auroc 0>> of five seeds), at \rhval{const/h24}\,h by <<A d_h24_grud_ffill_auroc 4>> ($p=$<<A p_h24_grud_ffill_auroc 4>>), and the mask+delta GRU by <<A d_h12_md_ffill_auroc 4>> and <<A d_h24_md_ffill_auroc 4>> (Table~\ref{tab:horizon-paired}); at the full horizon neither difference is detectable (Table~\ref{tab:paired}). H2 therefore holds for short horizons. This is consistent with the idea that mask and delta help most when little has been observed, but we did not test that mechanism directly. Second, the gap to the summary models narrows: GRU-D is within noise of LR at \rhval{const/h12}\,h (<<A d_h12_grud_lr_auroc 4>>, $p=$<<A p_h12_grud_lr_auroc 4>>) and at \rhval{const/h24}\,h (<<A d_h24_grud_lr_auroc 4>>, $p=$<<A p_h24_grud_lr_auroc 4>>), whereas at the full horizon it is below LR; GBDT stays ahead of GRU-D by <<A d_h12_gbdt_grud_auroc 4>> and <<A d_h24_gbdt_grud_auroc 4>> AUROC. On AUPRC at \rhval{const/h12}\,h, GRU-D is nominally above GBDT (<<V sweep_horizon|GRU-D_@12h auprc mean 3>> against <<V sweep_horizon|GBDT_(summary)_@12h auprc mean 3>>; GBDT $-$ GRU-D $=$ <<A d_h12_gbdt_grud_auprc 4>>, $p=$<<A p_h12_gbdt_grud_auprc 4>>), so ``GBDT is best'' is a statement about AUROC, not about every metric.

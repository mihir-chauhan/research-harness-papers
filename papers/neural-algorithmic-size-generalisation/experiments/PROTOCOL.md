# Protocol
Task bellman_ford; train n~U{4..8}, p=0.4, fresh graphs each step; no separate train set. Validation (val_mae_n8, train distribution, 100 graphs) is logged, not used for selection (no tuning: defaults fixed a priori). Test: 200 graphs per (family, n), n in 8,16,32,64, seeds 0-4 (main), 0-2 (ablations). Same metric definition and settings for all systems; message-passing steps T=n-1 at train and test. Hardware: shared Mac CPU, 2 threads. Runtime: ~1 min per run. (4 s to about 2 min).
Test sets: each seed k has its own 200 graphs per cell, drawn from rng(10000+k), independent of the training stream and identical across systems (and across ablation/sweep runs) at that seed; paired tests pair on the seed.
Registered: rh compare on mae_sparse_n64 (H1, H2), mae_sparse_n16 and n64 (H3), per-seed D64/D8 ratio sum+steps vs max+steps (H4, no test), step multipliers {0.5,1,2}. Post hoc: S16 tests for H1/H2, final-only pair for H4, multiplier x4, medians, the MAE>1 failure threshold.

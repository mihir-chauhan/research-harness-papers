# Design
`method/run.py`: data generator (spanning tree + ER, U(0.2,1) weights), numpy Bellman-Ford with per-round hints, FlatMLP (4160->128->128->64), MPNN (encoder, message MLP on [h_i,h_j,w_ij], sum/mean/max over neighbours, update MLP, decoder to (dist, reached logit) at each of T=n-1 steps; hidden 32). Loss: final-only = MSE on final distance; `-hint` = mean over steps of MSE on reached nodes + BCE on reached flag. Adam lr 3e-3 cosine, 1000 steps, batch 32 (single n per batch), grad clip 1. Evaluation on 200 fresh graphs per cell from a separate RNG stream; `--step_mult` rescales test-time steps.

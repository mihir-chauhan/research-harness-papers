On contextual SBM graphs, whether message passing helps depends on both homophily and feature strength. GCN beats the MLP at high homophily and, in this three-class model, also at $h=0$; it falls below the MLP in a low-to-mid homophily band that, at $\mu=1$, is significant at $h=0.2$ and $0.3$ but not at $h=0.4$, and that deepens with stronger features. Separating self and neighbour weights fixes most of the heterophilous failure but not uniformly (6 of 33 cells fall more than 0.02 short of the better of MLP and GCN), and label propagation wins only in the high-homophily corner. These results come from 5 seeds on one small synthetic family with reimplemented baselines and should be treated as a controlled map, not a general rule; extending to more seeds, larger graphs and real benchmarks is the obvious next step.

\textbf{Tasks.} A task is a cell $(h,\mu)$ with $h\in\{0,0.1,\dots,1\}$ and $\mu\in\{0.5,1,2\}$ (33 cells). For each seed in $\{0,\dots,4\}$ we regenerate graph, features, split (random 20\%/20\%/60\% train/validation/test) and initialisation. Systems: MLP, GCN, H2GCN-style, label propagation (LP), all reimplemented in plain torch (no PyG), giving 660 main runs, all with the same metric (test accuracy), data and training budget.

\textbf{Tuning.} Learning rate $\in\{0.01,0.05\}$, dropout $\in\{0,0.5\}$ and weight decay $\in\{5\!\times\!10^{-4},5\!\times\!10^{-3}\}$ (8 configurations) were scored by validation accuracy averaged over cells $h\in\{0.2,0.5,0.8\}$ at $\mu=1$ on separate tuning graphs (seeds 100--102); each trained model gets the selected triple (MLP: 0.01/0.5/$5\!\times\!10^{-3}$; GCN: 0.05/0.5/$5\!\times\!10^{-4}$; H2GCN-style: 0.05/0.5/$5\!\times\!10^{-3}$). Hidden size 32 for all models and LP's $\alpha$ is picked per run on validation accuracy. The grid is small, and differences between configurations on validation were small (log in \texttt{experiments/tuning\_log.json}); we did not tune depth or hidden size.

\textbf{Hypotheses and tests (registered in \texttt{proposal.md} before the main runs).}
H1: at $\mu=1$ and $h\in\{0.3,0.4,0.5\}$ GCN is below the MLP (paired two-sided $t$-test over 5 seeds per cell, $\alpha=0.05$, uncorrected).
H2: H2GCN-style is within 0.02 of or above $\max$(MLP, GCN) in the mean over seeds in every cell.
H3: LP is best only for $h\ge0.8$ with weak features ($\mu=0.5$) and is below the MLP for $h\le0.5$.
H4: the crossover $h$ above which GCN exceeds the MLP is lower for $\mu=0.5$ than for $\mu=2$.
H5: tying self and neighbour weights hurts H2GCN-style at $h\in\{0.1,0.4\}$, $\mu=1$ (paired $t$-test, 5 seeds).
H4 as originally written was ``the lowest $h$ at which GCN exceeds the MLP''; because GCN already exceeds the MLP at $h=0$ in this model, the literal reading is trivially false and we evaluate the crossover on the homophilous branch $h\ge 1/3$ (a post-hoc clarification, flagged again in Section~\ref{sec:results}).

\textbf{Additional studies.} (a) An ablation of H2GCN-style with tied weights and with a 2-hop term, at $\mu=1$ and $h\in\{0.1,0.4,0.9\}$ (30 runs; the full model's rows come from the main grid). (b) A mean-degree sweep $d\in\{2,5,20\}$ for MLP, GCN and H2GCN-style at $h=0.4$, $\mu=1$ (45 runs; $d=10$ rows from the main grid).

\textbf{Compute.} A shared CPU machine, 2 threads, no GPU; one training run takes about one second (LP much less), and the whole main grid took on the order of tens of minutes of wall clock including process start-up. An accidental double launch of the main grid produced duplicate registry entries and one failed entry (a file-name collision between the two launches); runs are deterministic, the duplicates were verified identical in accuracy and were removed, leaving exactly 5 seeds per system and cell.

# Results (5 seeds x 100 episodes per cell; tables in results/tables; final round of runs, commit 745cf32, 660 runs)

A violation is a clearance below -1e-9 m (same tolerance for every system). Safety is reported at barrier level (controlled point vs the radius the barrier uses). For the unicycle the axle-level `violation_rate` has a ~0.15 m buffer and is zero for every filter in every cell; it is shown only for comparison. Each verdict uses the test stated in proposal.md / research.yaml; grid observations are post hoc.

- **H1 (CT-CBF violations grow with dt and alpha, ~0 when small): refuted on its test, partly supported post hoc.** One-factor slices through the default: CT-CBF 0.00 everywhere on both plants. Joint effect in the grid (grid_violation): DI 0.270 (alpha=10, dt=0.1), 0.386 (alpha=10, dt=0.2), 0.020 (alpha=5, dt=0.2) and 0.006 (alpha=0.5, dt=0.2, mean worst penetration 0.073 m); unicycle at barrier level 0.862 (alpha=5, dt=0.2) and 1.000 (alpha=10, dt=0.2). Not monotone.
- **H2 (DT-CBF safer than CT-CBF at large dt, slower): refuted on both plants.** Period sweep at alpha=2, dt=0.1/0.2: DI 0.018/0.070 vs 0.000; unicycle (barrier) 0.734/0.970 vs 0.000. DT faster at the default (7.542 vs 7.800 s, Welch p=0.007). Over the grid DT is lower than CT in one DI cell (alpha=0.5, dt=0.2: 0.000 vs 0.006) and no unicycle cell. Confound: on the DI, CT-CBF is second-order (velocity-aware) and DT-CBF first-order on position; a high-order DT-CBF was not tried.
- **H3 (larger alpha lowers time to goal and clearance): partly supported.** DI monotone for both filters (CT time 10.93 -> 7.05 s, clearance 0.95 -> 0.01 m); on the unicycle the test fails: time falls to alpha=2 (12.84 -> 11.86) then slightly up (11.88, 11.92).
- **H4 (heuristic violates on DI, CBF filters do not): fails its test as written, partly supported.** Braking DI 0.110 (0.184 m), CT 0.000, DT 0.008 (DT vs CT Welch p=0.099). Unicycle: braking 0.000 at both levels.
- DT-CBF depth on the DI (mean over seeds of the per-seed worst penetration): at most 0.016 m where alpha*dt < 1 (alpha=2, dt=0.2); 0.109 m (alpha=10, dt=0.1) and 0.679 m (alpha 5/10, dt=0.2) where alpha*dt >= 1, comparable to the unfiltered 0.759 m. Largest single-seed value 0.760 m.
- Any-time vs at-sample (sample_vs_between): DI DT-CBF at-sample rate 0.000 in every cell except alpha=10, dt=0.2 (0.002, one episode in 500), including the saturated cells; all its other violations are inter-sample, and the rate is 0.000 at dt = 0.005 s = plant step. The at-sample rates of 0.76-0.83 reported in the previous round were round-off (sample_penetration ~1e-14 m) counted by a strict <0 test. CT-CBF DI: at-sample 0.018 of 0.270 and 0.184 of 0.386 at alpha=10; 0.000 of 0.020 at alpha=5, dt=0.2. Unicycle DT-CBF: at-sample = any-time (one cell 0.732 vs 0.734) and violations remain at dt=0.005 (0.034, 0.826, 0.992), consistent with (not proven to be) the Euler-successor mismatch.
- Extra: nearest-obstacle selection: CT 0.81 vs 0.00 (critical); DT 0.01 vs 0.01. Braking dth=2: violation 0.00 but success 0.970.

This is a small CPU study: two plants, one randomised scenario family with three circular obstacles, 5 seeds of 100 episodes, no input limits, no disturbance or model mismatch. All filters are our reimplementations with untuned design choices (critical-obstacle selection on the double integrator, nearest-obstacle selection on the unicycle, heuristic gain $K_b$) that affect the conclusions, as the nearest-obstacle ablation shows. \emph{Design order.} On the double integrator CT-CBF is a second-order condition that uses the velocity and DT-CBF a first-order condition on position, so ``DT-CBF is not safer'' is a statement about these two implementations; a high-order discrete-time condition or an exact unicycle successor might remove the violations we report. \emph{Unexplained effects.} No dedicated run isolates why the inter-sample dips of DT-CBF become deep at $\alpha\Delta t\ge1$, the single at-sample episode at $\alpha=10,\Delta t=0.2$, the CT-CBF violation at $\alpha=0.5,\Delta t=0.2$, or the cause of the unicycle DT-CBF violations at sample instants. \emph{Metrics.} An earlier version tested clearance strictly against zero and reported at-sample violation rates of 0.76--0.83 for DT-CBF in the saturated cells; an audit showed these to be round-off (the projected successor lies about $10^{-14}$\,m inside the boundary), so the $10^{-9}$\,m tolerance was added and every run repeated. The barrier-level unicycle metric was also added after an audit. \emph{Process.} The hypotheses and tests were written in the proposal but first committed together with the first results, so their priority cannot be verified from the history. In the first round of runs the script default was $\Delta t=0.1$\,s while the paper default was 0.05\,s, so the heuristic and nominal rows ran at a different period from the CBF rows; since then $\Delta t$ is passed explicitly to every run. The obstacle-selection rule was changed after a preliminary run and the DT-CBF implementation was corrected after an audit; all reported numbers come from the final round of runs. Verdicts are descriptive, with no multiple-comparison correction; 5 seeds cannot resolve differences of about 0.01 in violation rate. A fuller study would add input limits, a multi-constraint QP, noise, more plants and a high-order discrete-time CBF.

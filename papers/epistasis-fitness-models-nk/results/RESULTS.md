# Results (simulated NK landscapes; 440 runs, 0 failures; numbers from results/hypotheses.txt and results/tables/)
Only (L,A) = (15,4) and (20,20) were run, so L and A are confounded. n = 5 seeds; p-values are nominal and uncorrected.
- **H1 supported.** Additive ridge Spearman at K=0, N=1000: 1.000 (L15_A4) and 1.000 (L20_A20) (threshold 0.95). Nets: CNN 0.965 at L20_A20_K0.
- **H2 supported.** Additive mean Spearman falls 1.000/0.596/0.310/0.079 (K=0,1,2,4; L15_A4) and 1.000/0.110/0.021/0.014 (L20_A20). Pairwise > Additive at K=1 in all 5 seeds on both tasks (one-sided Wilcoxon p=0.031 = minimum attainable with n=5; analysis/hyp.py). Effect: +0.402 on L15_A4_K1, only +0.029 on L20_A20_K1 (paired t p=0.054, rh compare).
- **H3 supported at N=1000 (0 of 6 cells above Pairwise ridge for either net).** In the N sweep the nets stay below Pairwise at every N on both tasks; at N=4000 on L15_A4_K2 the nets (0.616, 0.628) are 0.053 and 0.042 below Pairwise (0.669). On L20_A20_K1 nets are at or below Additive at every N (MLP 0.070 vs 0.068 at N=500 is the one exception, inside seed spread) and 0.140/0.110 below Pairwise at N=4000.
- **H4: registered criterion met (rho > 0), evidence weak.** Spearman corr of dSpearman vs dhit (Pairwise-Additive) over 40 (task,seed) units = 0.516 (nominal p=0.0006, units not independent). It is carried by L15_A4_K1 (hit 0.90 vs 0.38, paired t p=0.003): without that cell rho=0.244 (p=0.157); over the 8 task means rho=0.59 (p=0.12). On L15_A4_K2 a Spearman gain of 0.198 gives a hit gain of 0.08 (p=0.34). Second part: all learned models have mean design_hit above Random and above the exact chance rate (frac_mutants_better) at K<=1, but at L20_A20_K1 all four (0.30-0.38 vs Random 0.24, chance 0.233) are within seed SD (0.15-0.28), and at L15_A4_K1 Additive/CNN/MLP vs Random have paired p=0.47/0.18/0.11 (vs exact chance: 0.08/0.018/0.006).
- **Unexplained pattern, not a finding:** at L20_A20 K=2,4 Additive's mean hit rate (0.34, 0.18) is above Random (0.14, 0.08) and exact chance (0.185, 0.115) while its Spearman is low (0.021, 0.014). Paired t: p=0.15, 0.30 vs Random; p=0.09, 0.10 vs chance. Not distinguishable from noise at n=5; no conclusion drawn. At K>=2 no learned model differs from Random in design_hit in paired t-tests (all p>=0.14; Welch p=0.049/0.047 for Additive/Pairwise at L20_A20_K2).
- **H5 supported.** Registered test (K=2 task): Pair-Add gap 0.067 at N=250 -> 0.311 at N=4000 (L15_A4_K2); also 0.005 -> 0.091 on L20_A20_K1. 5 seeds were run (proposal planned 3).
- Ablations (per-task deltas vs tuned Pairwise with paired t in results/hypotheses.txt, lines `ablation delta`): fixing r=1 costs 0.098 at L20_A20_K0 (0.995 vs 0.897) and at most 0.012 elsewhere; oracle interaction graph gives +0.113 (L15_A4_K2, p=0.0007) and +0.112 (L20_A20_K1, p=0.0002) over Pairwise, at most +0.005 in the other six cells.
- Failure regime: K=4 (both settings) and (20,20) with K>=2: every model's mean Spearman <= 0.080 at N=1000; larger N not tested there.
- `rh verdict` (results/VERDICT.md) is an advisory tier computed for Spearman on the main group. Its ablation rows read "missing" because the harness looks for the method's own rows inside the ablation group, and the tuned Pairwise runs exist only in `main` (re-logging them under `abl_pair` would duplicate runs); the ablation deltas are the lines above.

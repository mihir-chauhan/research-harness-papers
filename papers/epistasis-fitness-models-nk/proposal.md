# Proposal

## Direction
How much epistasis can simple sequence-to-fitness models absorb, on simulated NK landscapes (L 15-20, alphabet 4-20, K 0-4)?
**All landscapes are simulated; nothing here is a statement about real proteins.**

## Setup
Landscape: random-neighbourhood NK, fitness = mean over L sites of a table lookup on (own residue, K random other sites), table entries iid U(0,1)
(generated by a hash, so alphabet 20 / K=4 needs no memory). Training: N uniformly random sequences with noiseless fitness; test: 5000 other uniformly random sequences.
Systems: one-hot ridge (Additive), ridge on one-hot + all pairwise one-hot products (Pairwise ridge, the focal method), small CNN, small MLP, plus a Random scorer.
Design step: parent = best training sequence; score all L(A-1) single mutants; propose top 10.
Metrics: test Spearman; design_hit = fraction of the 10 proposals with true fitness above the parent; design_gain = (best proposed - parent) in test-fitness SDs.
Primary grid (main): tasks L15_A4_K{0,1,2,4} and L20_A20_K{0,1,2,4}, N=1000, seeds 0-4 (each seed = new landscape, new data). Sweep over N in {250,500,2000,4000} on two tasks, 3 seeds.

## Hypotheses (tests fixed before the main runs)
- **H1 (K=0 sanity/additive absorbs additive):** at K=0, Additive reaches Spearman >= 0.95 on both (L,A) tasks at N=1000 (mean over 5 seeds). Refuted if either mean < 0.95.
- **H2 (epistasis costs the additive model; pairwise recovers pairs):** Additive Spearman at N=1000 decreases from K=0 to K=4 on both (L,A); and Pairwise ridge > Additive at K=1 (paired over 5 seeds, one-sided Wilcoxon / sign of the mean difference, per task). Refuted if Pairwise <= Additive at K=1 on a task, or the Additive mean does not fall.
- **H3 (small nets do not beat pairwise ridge at this data scale):** at N=1000, for K in {1,2,4}, the CNN and the MLP each have mean Spearman below Pairwise ridge in at least half of the six (task,K) cells (K in {1,2,4} x 2 tasks); refuted if either net is above Pairwise ridge in more than half of those 6 cells.
- **H4 (design tracks prediction):** across all (task, K, seed) units in main, the Spearman rank correlation between (Spearman_Pairwise - Spearman_Additive) and (hit_Pairwise - hit_Additive) is positive (> 0); additionally every learned model has mean design_hit above Random in every cell with K<=1. Refuted if the correlation is <= 0.
- **H5 (data scaling):** in the N sweep, the Pairwise-minus-Additive Spearman gap at K=2 grows with N (gap at N=4000 > gap at N=250). Refuted otherwise.

## Baselines (all reimplemented here)
Additive one-hot ridge (low-N baseline of [hsu2022learning]); Pairwise ridge; CNN; MLP; Random.

## Ablations
abl_pair: Pairwise ridge with (a) a fixed pair/additive penalty ratio of 1 (no tuning of the ratio); (b) pairs restricted to the true interaction graph (oracle; upper bound that uses knowledge a practitioner lacks). N sweep (H5).

## Risks
Surprising negative results (nets fail to beat ridge) are fine. Pairwise ridge at A=20 has ~76k features for N<=4000, so it may overfit.

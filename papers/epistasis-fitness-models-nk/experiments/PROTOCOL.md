# Protocol
All data are simulated (random-neighbourhood NK, see method/DESIGN.md). Hardware: shared Apple-silicon laptop CPU, 2 threads, no GPU; at most two runs in parallel.
- **main**: tasks L{15,20}_A{4,20}_K{0,1,2,4} (pairs (15,4) and (20,20)), N=1000 train, 5000 test, seeds 0-4 (seed = new landscape + new data; identical for all systems). Systems: Pairwise ridge (method), Additive ridge, CNN, MLP, Random.
- **sweep_n** (ablation kind): N in {250,500,2000,4000} on L15_A4_K2 and L20_A20_K1, four learned systems, seeds 0-4. (N=1000 comes from main.) Deviation from proposal.md/BRIEF.md: the proposal planned 3 seeds for this sweep; 5 were run (all five are reported, none dropped) because the runs were cheap.
- **abl_pair**: Pairwise ridge (r fixed) and Pairwise ridge (oracle graph) on all 8 main tasks, N=1000, seeds 0-4.
- Tuning: inside each run, on training data only (ridge: 24 or 6 configs on a 20% hold-out; nets: 2 weight decays on a 15% validation split). Test set never used for selection. No hyperparameters were tuned across tasks.
- Metrics: test Spearman (5000 sequences), design_hit, design_gain, fit_seconds (see DESIGN.md). Run time from the registry (results/hypotheses.txt, line `runtime`): longest run 22.3 s, 17 of 440 runs over 15 s, 2 over 20 s; the registry spans 14.1 minutes of wall-clock (two runs in parallel), sum of run durations 26.5 minutes. `frac_mutants_better` (fraction of all single mutants above the parent) is the exact chance level of design_hit; the Random system's design_hit is a 10-proposal sample of it.
- Statistics: paired over seeds (same landscape and data). `rh compare` gives Welch and paired t-tests: `results/tables/compare_main_spearman.csv` (reference Pairwise ridge), `compare_main_design_hit_ref_pairwise.csv` and `compare_main_design_hit_ref_random.csv` (`compare_main_design_hit.csv` is the last of these as written by rh). The one-sided Wilcoxon tests (H2), the H4 rank correlations with leave-one-cell-out and task-mean variants, the tests of design_hit against the exact chance rate, and the per-task ablation deltas are computed by `analysis/hyp.py` from `results/runs.jsonl` and written to `results/hypotheses.txt`. n=5 seeds, so the smallest attainable one-sided Wilcoxon p is 1/32; no correction for multiple comparisons; all p-values are indicative.
- Tuning asymmetry (disclosed in the paper's limitations): ridge models are refit on all training data after selection (24 configs for Pairwise, 6 for Additive); the CNN/MLP use the early-stopped model trained on 85% of the training data (2 configs), no refit.
- L and A are confounded: only (15,4) and (20,20) were run.

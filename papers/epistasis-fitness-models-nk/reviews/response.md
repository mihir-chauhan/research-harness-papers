# Response to the audit (single fix round)

No new experiment runs were made: every finding concerned wording, reported statistics or disclosure, and the 440 registered runs are unchanged. New statistics are computed from `results/runs.jsonl` by `analysis/hyp.py` (output `results/hypotheses.txt`), new tables by `analysis/tables_figs.py`, and paired t-tests on design_hit by `rh compare`.

## Major

1. **Additive-beats-random design claim (abstract, results, conclusion).** Accepted. Ran `rh compare --metric design_hit` against Random and against Pairwise ridge (`results/tables/compare_main_design_hit_ref_random.csv`, `..._ref_pairwise.csv`). Table I now has a "Chance" column (mean `frac_mutants_better`, the exact chance rate) next to Random; Table II now also gives the seed SD of design_hit; new Table III lists the paired differences and p-values (Additive vs Random at L20_A20_K2/K4: p=0.15, 0.30; vs exact chance: 0.09, 0.10). The abstract no longer says additive ridge beats random proposals; it says design hit rates at K>=2 are not reliably distinguishable from random proposals. The results paragraph calls the pattern unexplained and within noise, and draws no conclusion; the "therefore" sentence and the conclusion's "decoupled ... so the two endpoints should be reported together" recommendation are deleted. No seeds were added.

2. **H4 correlation and p<0.001.** Accepted. The H4 paragraph is retitled "registered criterion met, evidence weak". It states that the 40 units are not independent, that the correlation is carried by L15_A4_K1, and reports the leave-that-cell-out value (0.24, p=0.16) and the task-mean value (0.59, p=0.12) in Table III, together with the L15_A4_K2 case (Spearman 0.310 -> 0.508, design_hit gain 0.08, p=0.34). All eight leave-one-cell-out values are in `results/hypotheses.txt`. The abstract, conclusion and `results/RESULTS.md` say the same. The verdict still uses the registered test (rho > 0).

3. **"no model learned much from N<=4000" in the conclusion.** Accepted. Changed to "from N=1000 (mean Spearman at most 0.080); larger N was not tested in those regimes". The N sweep was not extended; the limitations now say the sweep covers none of the failure regimes.

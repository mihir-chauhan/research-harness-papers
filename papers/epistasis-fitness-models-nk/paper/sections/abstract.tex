How much non-additive structure can simple regressors learn from a few hundred to a few thousand variants? We study this on \emph{simulated} NK landscapes only, at two settings of length and alphabet, $(L,A)=(15,4)$ and $(20,20)$ (so $L$ and $A$ are confounded), with $K\in\{0,1,2,4\}$, comparing reimplemented one-hot ridge, pairwise-epistasis ridge, a small CNN and a small MLP, with held-out Spearman and one greedy design step as endpoints (5 seeds, \rhval{count/runs} CPU runs). At $K=0$ additive ridge is near-perfect (Spearman $\rhval{main/additive-ridge/l15_a4_k0/spearman/mean:3}$). Additive Spearman at $N=1000$ falls to $\rhval{main/additive-ridge/l15_a4_k1/spearman/mean:3}$ at $K=1$ and $\rhval{main/additive-ridge/l15_a4_k2/spearman/mean:3}$ at $K=2$ for $(15,4)$, and to $\rhval{main/additive-ridge/l20_a20_k1/spearman/mean:3}$ at $K=1$ for $(20,20)$. Pairwise ridge recovers $K=1$ at $(15,4)$ ($\rhval{main/pairwise-ridge/l15_a4_k1/spearman/mean:3}$) but gains little at $(20,20)$ ($\rhval{main/pairwise-ridge/l20_a20_k1/spearman/mean:3}$ versus $\rhval{main/additive-ridge/l20_a20_k1/spearman/mean:3}$); the networks never exceed it at $N=1000$, and on the $(15,4)$, $K=2$ task they remain below it at $N=4000$ ($\rhval{sweep_n/cnn@n_train=4000/l15_a4_k2/spearman/mean:3}$ and $\rhval{sweep_n/mlp@n_train=4000/l15_a4_k2/spearman/mean:3}$ versus $\rhval{sweep_n/pairwise-ridge@n_train=4000/l15_a4_k2/spearman/mean:3}$). At $K=4$, and at $(20,20)$ for $K\ge2$, every model's mean Spearman at $N=1000$ is at most $\rhval{main/pairwise-ridge/l15_a4_k4/spearman/mean:3}$. The pre-registered design criterion, a positive rank correlation between the Spearman gain and the design-hit gain of pairwise over additive ridge, is met over the 40 task--seed units, but the evidence is weak: one task cell carries it, and with 10 proposals and 5 seeds the design hit rates at $K\ge2$ are not reliably distinguishable from random proposals. Results concern this simulator only.

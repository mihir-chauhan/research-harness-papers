\paragraph{Training-set size (H5): supported.} Table~\ref{tab:sweep} and Fig.~\ref{fig:sweep} vary $N$ from 250 to 4000 on L15\_A4\_K2 and L20\_A20\_K1 ($N=1000$ comes from the main group). The registered test concerns the $K=2$ task: the Pairwise$-$Additive Spearman gap there is larger at $N=4000$ ($\rhval{sweep_n/pairwise-ridge@n_train=4000/l15_a4_k2/spearman/mean:3}$ versus $\rhval{sweep_n/additive-ridge@n_train=4000/l15_a4_k2/spearman/mean:3}$) than at $N=250$ ($\rhval{sweep_n/pairwise-ridge@n_train=250/l15_a4_k2/spearman/mean:3}$ versus $\rhval{sweep_n/additive-ridge@n_train=250/l15_a4_k2/spearman/mean:3}$), and grows at every step in between; on L20\_A20\_K1 it also grows (from $\rhval{sweep_n/pairwise-ridge@n_train=250/l20_a20_k1/spearman/mean:3}$ versus $\rhval{sweep_n/additive-ridge@n_train=250/l20_a20_k1/spearman/mean:3}$ to $\rhval{sweep_n/pairwise-ridge@n_train=4000/l20_a20_k1/spearman/mean:3}$ versus $\rhval{sweep_n/additive-ridge@n_train=4000/l20_a20_k1/spearman/mean:3}$). Regarding H3, the networks stay below pairwise ridge at every $N$ on both tasks. On L15\_A4\_K2 they are indistinguishable from additive ridge at $N=500$ ($\rhval{sweep_n/cnn@n_train=500/l15_a4_k2/spearman/mean:3}$, $\rhval{sweep_n/mlp@n_train=500/l15_a4_k2/spearman/mean:3}$ versus $\rhval{sweep_n/additive-ridge@n_train=500/l15_a4_k2/spearman/mean:3}$) and close the distance to pairwise ridge at $N=4000$ ($\rhval{sweep_n/cnn@n_train=4000/l15_a4_k2/spearman/mean:3}$ and $\rhval{sweep_n/mlp@n_train=4000/l15_a4_k2/spearman/mean:3}$ versus $\rhval{sweep_n/pairwise-ridge@n_train=4000/l15_a4_k2/spearman/mean:3}$). On L20\_A20\_K1, however, they are at or below additive ridge at every $N$ (the one exception, MLP $\rhval{sweep_n/mlp@n_train=500/l20_a20_k1/spearman/mean:3}$ versus $\rhval{sweep_n/additive-ridge@n_train=500/l20_a20_k1/spearman/mean:3}$ at $N=500$, is far inside the seed spread of Fig.~\ref{fig:sweep}), the best score at $N=4000$ is only $\rhval{sweep_n/pairwise-ridge@n_train=4000/l20_a20_k1/spearman/mean:3}$, and their distance to pairwise ridge does not shrink ($\rhval{sweep_n/cnn@n_train=4000/l20_a20_k1/spearman/mean:3}$ and $\rhval{sweep_n/mlp@n_train=4000/l20_a20_k1/spearman/mean:3}$ versus $\rhval{sweep_n/pairwise-ridge@n_train=4000/l20_a20_k1/spearman/mean:3}$). So H3 held at every $N$ we ran; the sweep suggests the gap may close with more data on L15\_A4\_K2, but $N>4000$ was not run and only two tasks were swept.

\paragraph{Pair-feature ablations.} Table~\ref{tab:abl} compares tuned Pairwise ridge with (i) the penalty ratio fixed at $r=1$ and (ii) pair features restricted to the true interaction graph (an oracle). Fixing $r=1$ matters only where pair features carry no signal: at L20\_A20\_K0 it costs $\rhval{main/pairwise-ridge/l20_a20_k0/spearman/mean:3}\to\rhval{abl_pair/pairwise-ridge-r-fixed/l20_a20_k0/spearman/mean:3}$; elsewhere the differences are within one seed standard deviation. The oracle graph helps in two cells: $\rhval{main/pairwise-ridge/l15_a4_k2/spearman/mean:3}\to\rhval{abl_pair/pairwise-ridge-oracle-graph/l15_a4_k2/spearman/mean:3}$ on L15\_A4\_K2 and $\rhval{main/pairwise-ridge/l20_a20_k1/spearman/mean:3}\to\rhval{abl_pair/pairwise-ridge-oracle-graph/l20_a20_k1/spearman/mean:3}$ on L20\_A20\_K1; it is at ceiling ($\rhval{abl_pair/pairwise-ridge-oracle-graph/l15_a4_k1/spearman/mean:3}$) at $K\le1$ for (15,4). Even then L15\_A4\_K2 stays at $\rhval{abl_pair/pairwise-ridge-oracle-graph/l15_a4_k2/spearman/mean:3}$, below the level that $K=1$ reaches, which is consistent with (but not proof of) order-3 terms that no pairwise model can capture; and at $K=4$ nothing helps ($\rhval{abl_pair/pairwise-ridge-oracle-graph/l15_a4_k4/spearman/mean:3}$ at best). We did not run a three-way-interaction model, so the order-3 explanation is untested. Knowing which pairs interact thus raised mean Spearman clearly in those two cells and changed it little in the other six (Table~\ref{tab:abl}); this is an upper reference for what pair selection could add in this simulator, obtained with information a practitioner lacks.

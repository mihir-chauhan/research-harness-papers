# Landscape
Neural-guided proof search: DeepMath (alemi2016deepmath) and Loos et al. (loos2017deep) learn premise/clause selection for first-order provers; Kaliszyk et al. (kaliszyk2018reinforcement) use RL-guided connection tableaux; GPT-f (polu2020generative), HTPS (lample2022hypertree), BFS-Prover (xin2025bfs), LeanDojo (yang2023leandojo) and HER-based provers (aygn2021proving) use language models as tactic policies with best-first/MCTS search on large libraries. Synthetic reasoning benchmarks (saxton2019analysing, clark2020transformers) and length-generalisation studies (anil2022exploring) show transformers struggle out of distribution.
Gap: those systems are evaluated at scale with learned and hand components confounded; few report a fair hand-written baseline in a calculus where optimal proofs are computable. We provide a small controlled study.

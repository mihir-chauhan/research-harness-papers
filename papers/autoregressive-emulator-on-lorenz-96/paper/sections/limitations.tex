\textbf{Scale and statistics.} A single small architecture ($\rhval{main/cnn-k-1/l96/n_params/mean:0}$ parameters), one learning rate shared across $K$ (tuned for $K=1,4$ only), 1500 optimiser steps and 5 seeds. The $K{=}8$ vs $K{=}1$ effect is about five percent in RMSE at one lead with a paired $p$ that is not corrected for the many metrics and leads we inspected; the $K{=}4$ effect is not robust to the unpaired test. Rollout lengths above $8$ were not run, nor equal-compute comparisons (a $K{=}8$ step costs roughly eight times a $K{=}1$ step; a $K{=}1$ model trained eight times longer might close the gap).

\textbf{Setting.} A single system with one parameter set ($F{=}10$, $c{=}b{=}10$) and one sampling step. Instabilities of one-step emulators are reported for harder, higher-dimensional problems; failing to see them here does not show they are absent elsewhere. Our stability criterion (divergence beyond $|x|>50$ within 200 time units from 16 states) cannot detect slow drift of the climate that stays bounded; we tracked variance, roughness and a marginal KS distance, but not spectra, extremes or temporal correlations.

\textbf{Baselines.} Persistence, climatology and a linear stencil are reimplemented and simple; there is no comparison with a published emulator, a data-assimilation-style ensemble, or with the true one-scale system. The metrics are deterministic and say nothing about calibration. ACC is relative to a scalar climatology (the system is statistically homogeneous), and climatology's ACC is defined as $0$.

\textbf{Evaluation.} Test windows from the same trajectory overlap in the sense of being correlated; they are averaged before the seed-level tests, which treat seeds as the replicates. Seeds change data, initialisation and minibatches together, so we cannot attribute variance to any one of them. All results are for a CPU-scale model; a full study would add larger networks, longer training, other noise strategies (pushforward, refinement), equal-compute budgets, ensembles with CRPS, and multiple Lorenz-96 regimes.

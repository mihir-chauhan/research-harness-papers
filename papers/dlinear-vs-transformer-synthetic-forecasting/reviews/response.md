# Response to the audit (fix round)

New evidence added in this round: 108 runs through `rh run` (groups `sweep_steps`, `sweep_dwell_2000`; runner `experiments/run_budget.sh`,
committed before the runs; same code and settings as the main grid except `--steps`). All runs finished with status ok, the longest took 247 s. (Checking durations for this round also showed that two main-grid PatchTST runs
from the original batch took 450 s and 544 s under machine load, above the 6-minute limit; the setup section now says so.)
The sweep is described everywhere as post hoc (added after the audit); the hypotheses and the registered win rule are unchanged.

## Major 1. Nonlinear baselines under-trained at 400 steps; conclusions flip with budget
Accepted. The auditor's numbers reproduce exactly (regime, H=96, 2000 steps: DLinear 0.4611/0.4324/0.4029, GRU 0.4049/0.3990/0.3549).
- Added a training-budget sweep: regime and etth1 at 1000 and 2000 steps, all three horizons, 3 seeds, three learned systems; seas1, trend,
  multiseas and noisy at 2000 steps, H=96; and the dwell sweep (the registered test of H2's second clause) repeated at 2000 steps, H=96.
- New Table IV (`results/tables/budget.tex`) reports every cell with MSE, change vs DLinear, seeds better and the win mark; Table VI now has
  the dwell sweep at both budgets; Fig. 1 and Fig. 2 show both budgets.
- What the sweep shows: DLinear is converged at 400 steps (MSE moves by at most 0.004 on regime/etth1), the GRU is not. At 1000 and 2000 steps
  on regime, PatchTST and the GRU win at H=24, the GRU wins at H=96 (-7.4%, -10.6%), nobody wins at H=192. No win on the other four synthetic
  tasks (H=96, 2000 steps) or on ETTh1 (any horizon, any budget).
- Verdicts rewritten per budget in abstract, introduction, results, conclusion, `results/RESULTS.md`: H2 is "not supported as registered at any
  budget (no win at H=192, margin not monotone in switching rate); partly supported from 1000 steps (wins at H=24 and H=96)". The statements
  "the GRU is worse at every horizon" and "no nonlinear win on any task" are now explicitly restricted to 400 steps. The earlier untested
  explanation of the GRU's multiseas gap (difficulty with the period-168 component) was removed, since the gap disappears at 2000 steps.
- Not repeated at longer budgets (stated in setup, results H3, ablations and limitations): the noise sweep, the design ablation, and H=24/192
  for the four tasks without regime changes.

## Major 2. "Simple linear models were therefore sufficient" / "no support for regime switching"
Accepted. Both sentences were removed. The conclusion and contribution (ii) now say that at 400 steps no nonlinear model passed the rule,
that at 1000 and 2000 steps the GRU (H=24, 96) and the patch transformer (H=24) pass it on regime-switching data, and that regime switching
was the only property we varied under which a nonlinear model passed the rule, with the limits of what was tested at 2000 steps stated.

means are driven by seed 1 and "essential" was removed.

## Minor 4. "GRU is the worst learned model on etth1"
Fixed. The text now says worst on etth1 at H=24 and tied with PatchTST at H=96 and 192 (0.399 vs 0.400, 0.438 vs 0.438), all at 400 steps.

## Minor 5. H1 "within the seed spread"; H3 "no consistent gap" for the GRU
Fixed. Table III now carries the number of seeds in which each nonlinear model is better; H1 describes the paired, sign-consistent differences
(and that their sign changes at 2000 steps). H3 is now "mixed": PatchTST keeps a small 3/3-seed advantage at every noise level, and the GRU's
gap runs against the hypothesis (+8.4% at 0.1 to -2.1% at 3.0). One correction to the finding: the GRU trend is not strictly monotone
(-0.6% at 0.6, -0.1% at 1.5, -2.1% at 3.0), so the paper gives the values instead of the word "monotonically".

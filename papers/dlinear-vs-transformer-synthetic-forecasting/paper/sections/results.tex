\textbf{Effect of the budget.} DLinear is converged at 400 steps: on regime and etth1 its seed-mean MSE moves by at most 0.004 between 400 and 2000 steps (Table~\ref{tab:budget}). The GRU is not: on regime its MSE falls from 0.322 to 0.253 at $H=24$ and from 0.446 to 0.386 at $H=96$. The patch transformer improves at regime $H=24$ (0.291 to 0.277) but is slightly worse at 2000 than at 400 steps in most other cells (e.g.\ etth1 $H=192$, 0.438 to 0.447), which may be over-fitting (untested). The 400-step grid thus compared a converged linear model with an under-trained GRU; verdicts are given per budget.

\textbf{H1 (no regime change $\Rightarrow$ DLinear not beaten): supported where tested.} At 400 steps, on seas1, trend, multiseas and noisy, neither nonlinear model is more than about one percent better than DLinear at any horizon (best: GRU on noisy, $-1.11\%$ at $H=24$) and the GRU is up to $+11.11\%$ worse (multiseas). The small differences are not seed noise: a seed is a dataset shared by all systems, and the paired differences have the same sign in all three seeds in every one of these cells (Table~\ref{tab:rel}: both nonlinear models worse on seas1, trend and multiseas, both better on noisy). They are consistent but far below 5\%. At 2000 steps ($H=96$, Table~\ref{tab:budget}) there is again no win, but the signs change: the GRU's multiseas gap goes from $+9.6\%$ to $-1.2\%$ (better in 3/3 seeds), it is $-1.5\%$ on seas1 (3/3), and both nonlinear models lose their small edge on noisy ($+1.4\%$ and $+0.6\%$); PatchTST is worse than DLinear in all seeds on all four tasks ($+1.4\%$ to $+6.9\%$). H1 was not tested at 2000 steps for $H=24$ and 192.

\textbf{H2 (regime switching $\Rightarrow$ a nonlinear model wins at $H=96$ and 192, and the margin grows with switching rate): not supported as registered at any budget; partly supported from 1000 steps.} At 400 steps PatchTST is better than DLinear in all three seeds at $H=24$ ($-4.97\%$) and $H=96$ ($-4.82\%$), both short of 5\%, and is $+1.41\%$ worse at $H=192$; the GRU is worse on the mean at every horizon. At 1000 and 2000 steps the picture changes (Table~\ref{tab:budget}): at $H=24$ both models win (PatchTST $-9.8\%$ and $-9.2\%$, GRU $-13.2\%$ and $-17.1\%$) and at $H=96$ the GRU wins ($-7.4\%$ and $-10.6\%$), while PatchTST at $H=96$ stays below the threshold ($-3.7\%$, $-2.5\%$, 3/3 seeds). At $H=192$ nobody wins at any budget: the GRU is $-2.3\%$ and $-1.5\%$ (2/3 seeds) and PatchTST is worse than DLinear. The first clause is thus met at $H=96$ but not at $H=192$. The second clause is tested by the dwell sweeps (Table~\ref{tab:dwell}). At 400 steps the PatchTST margin is not monotone ($-0.2\%$ at dwell 100, $-5.1\%$ at 1000, $-1.7\%$ at 4000); the GRU margin does move in the predicted direction ($-4.1\%$ at dwell 100 to $+91.7\%$ at 4000) but never reaches a win. At 2000 steps the GRU wins at dwell 100 ($-5.7\%$) and 500 ($-10.6\%$), is $-8.9\%$ at 250 but better in only 2/3 seeds, and has no win at 1000 ($-3.6\%$) or 4000 ($+12.0\%$): its wins are confined to mean regime lengths up to 500 steps, but the margin peaks at 500 and does not grow monotonically with the switching rate. PatchTST has no win in the 2000-step dwell sweep.

\textbf{H3 (more noise erases the nonlinear advantage): mixed; tested at 400 steps only.} Table~\ref{tab:noise}: the PatchTST gap to DLinear is $-9.3\%$ at noise sd 0.1 (a win under the rule), $-4.8\%$ at 0.3, $-1.3\%$ at 0.6, $-0.7\%$ at 1.5 and $-1.1\%$ at 3.0, so its above-threshold advantage disappears, but a small advantage in 3/3 seeds remains at every noise level. The GRU runs against the hypothesis: its gap moves from $+8.4\%$ at 0.1 through $-0.6\%$ and $-0.1\%$ to $-2.1\%$ (3/3 seeds) at 3.0. A shrinking \emph{relative} gap is expected in any case, since all MSEs approach the noise variance.

\textbf{H4 (ETTh1: DLinear not beaten at $H=96,192$; all learned models beat seasonal naive): supported at all three budgets.} At 400 steps DLinear has MSE 0.376 and 0.410 at $H=96$ and 192; PatchTST is $+6.32\%$ and $+6.82\%$ worse and the GRU $+6.16\%$ and $+6.79\%$ (Tables~\ref{tab:main}, \ref{tab:rel}). At 2000 steps PatchTST is $+7.6\%$ and $+9.3\%$ worse and the GRU $+2.9\%$ and $+6.8\%$, in all seeds (Table~\ref{tab:budget}). At $H=24$ both are slightly better in all seeds at 2000 steps ($-2.4\%$, $-3.8\%$), below the threshold. Every learned model beats seasonal naive (0.424, 0.512, 0.581) at all horizons and budgets.

\textbf{Where the nonlinear models fail.} (i) Short budget: at 400 steps the GRU is the worst learned model on multiseas at every horizon and on etth1 at $H=24$ (at $H=96$ and 192 it is tied with PatchTST, 0.399 vs 0.400 and 0.438 vs 0.438); the multiseas gap vanishes at 2000 steps, so it reflects under-training. (ii) Long horizon: no nonlinear model wins at $H=192$ on regime data at any budget. (iii) Long regimes: at dwell 4000 the GRU is $+12.0\%$ worse than DLinear at 2000 steps and $+91.7\%$ at 400. (iv) ETTh1: both nonlinear models are worse at $H=96$ and 192 at every budget. Seasonal naive is far worse than every learned model on regime (1.587 vs 0.434 for DLinear at $H=96$).

\textbf{Effect of the budget.} DLinear is converged at 400 steps: on regime and etth1 its seed-mean MSE is almost the same at 400, 1000 and 2000 steps (Table~\ref{tab:budget}; e.g.\ regime $H=96$: \rhval{main/dlinear/regime/mse_96/mean:3}, \rhval{sweep_steps/dlinear@steps=1000/regime/mse_96/mean:3} and \rhval{sweep_steps/dlinear@steps=2000/regime/mse_96/mean:3}). The GRU is not: on regime its MSE falls from \rhval{main/gru/regime/mse_24/mean:3} to \rhval{sweep_steps/gru@steps=2000/regime/mse_24/mean:3} at $H=24$ and from \rhval{main/gru/regime/mse_96/mean:3} to \rhval{sweep_steps/gru@steps=2000/regime/mse_96/mean:3} at $H=96$. The patch transformer improves at regime $H=24$ (\rhval{main/patchtst/regime/mse_24/mean:3} to \rhval{sweep_steps/patchtst@steps=2000/regime/mse_24/mean:3}) but is slightly worse at 2000 than at 400 steps in most other cells (e.g.\ etth1 $H=192$, \rhval{main/patchtst/etth1/mse_192/mean:3} to \rhval{sweep_steps/patchtst@steps=2000/etth1/mse_192/mean:3}), which may be over-fitting (untested). The 400-step grid thus compared a converged linear model with an under-trained GRU; verdicts are given per budget.

\textbf{H1 (no regime change $\Rightarrow$ DLinear not beaten): supported where tested.} At 400 steps, on seas1, trend, multiseas and noisy, no nonlinear model comes near a win at any horizon: the lowest MSE ratio to DLinear in these rows of Table~\ref{tab:rel} is \rhval{cmp/main/gru/noisy/mse_24/inv_ratio:4} (GRU on noisy, $H=24$) and the highest is \rhval{cmp/main/gru/multiseas/mse_24/inv_ratio:4} (GRU on multiseas, $H=24$). The small differences are not seed noise: a seed is a dataset shared by all systems, and the paired differences have the same sign in all three seeds in every one of these cells (Table~\ref{tab:rel}: both nonlinear models worse on seas1, trend and multiseas, both better on noisy). They are consistent but far from the 5\% threshold. At 2000 steps ($H=96$, Table~\ref{tab:budget}; ratios to DLinear from \texttt{rh compare} on the 2000-step runs of these four tasks) there is again no win, but the signs change: the GRU's multiseas ratio goes from \rhval{cmp/main/gru/multiseas/mse_96/inv_ratio:3} to \rhval{cmp/sweep_steps/gru/multiseas/mse_96/inv_ratio:3} (better in 3/3 seeds), it is \rhval{cmp/sweep_steps/gru/seas1/mse_96/inv_ratio:3} on seas1 (3/3), and both nonlinear models lose their small edge on noisy (\rhval{cmp/sweep_steps/patchtst/noisy/mse_96/inv_ratio:3} and \rhval{cmp/sweep_steps/gru/noisy/mse_96/inv_ratio:3}); PatchTST is worse than DLinear in all seeds on all four tasks (ratios from \rhval{cmp/sweep_steps/patchtst/noisy/mse_96/inv_ratio:3} to \rhval{cmp/sweep_steps/patchtst/trend/mse_96/inv_ratio:3}). H1 was not tested at 2000 steps for $H=24$ and 192.

\textbf{H2 (regime switching $\Rightarrow$ a nonlinear model wins at $H=96$ and 192, and the margin grows with switching rate): not supported as registered at any budget; partly supported from 1000 steps.} At 400 steps PatchTST is better than DLinear in all three seeds at $H=24$ (MSE ratio to DLinear \rhval{cmp/main/patchtst/regime/mse_24/inv_ratio:4}) and $H=96$ (\rhval{cmp/main/patchtst/regime/mse_96/inv_ratio:4}), both short of the 0.95 that the 5\% rule requires, and is worse at $H=192$ (\rhval{cmp/main/patchtst/regime/mse_192/inv_ratio:4}); the GRU is worse on the mean at every horizon. At 1000 and 2000 steps the picture changes (Table~\ref{tab:budget}): at $H=24$ both models win (PatchTST \rhval{sweep_steps/patchtst@steps=1000/regime/mse_24/mean:3} and \rhval{sweep_steps/patchtst@steps=2000/regime/mse_24/mean:3}, GRU \rhval{sweep_steps/gru@steps=1000/regime/mse_24/mean:3} and \rhval{sweep_steps/gru@steps=2000/regime/mse_24/mean:3}, against \rhval{sweep_steps/dlinear@steps=1000/regime/mse_24/mean:3} and \rhval{sweep_steps/dlinear@steps=2000/regime/mse_24/mean:3} for DLinear) and at $H=96$ the GRU wins (\rhval{sweep_steps/gru@steps=1000/regime/mse_96/mean:3} and \rhval{sweep_steps/gru@steps=2000/regime/mse_96/mean:3} against \rhval{sweep_steps/dlinear@steps=1000/regime/mse_96/mean:3} and \rhval{sweep_steps/dlinear@steps=2000/regime/mse_96/mean:3}), while PatchTST at $H=96$ does not reach the threshold (\rhval{sweep_steps/patchtst@steps=1000/regime/mse_96/mean:3} and \rhval{sweep_steps/patchtst@steps=2000/regime/mse_96/mean:3}, better in 3/3 seeds). At $H=192$ nobody wins at any budget: the GRU is slightly lower on the mean but better in only 2/3 seeds (\rhval{sweep_steps/gru@steps=1000/regime/mse_192/mean:3} and \rhval{sweep_steps/gru@steps=2000/regime/mse_192/mean:3} against \rhval{sweep_steps/dlinear@steps=1000/regime/mse_192/mean:3} and \rhval{sweep_steps/dlinear@steps=2000/regime/mse_192/mean:3}) and PatchTST is worse than DLinear. The first clause is thus met at $H=96$ but not at $H=192$. The second clause is tested by the dwell sweeps (Table~\ref{tab:dwell}). At 400 steps the PatchTST margin is not monotone in the dwell (PatchTST against DLinear: \rhval{sweep_dwell/patchtst@dwell=100/regime/mse_96/mean:3} vs \rhval{sweep_dwell/dlinear@dwell=100/regime/mse_96/mean:3} at dwell 100, \rhval{sweep_dwell/patchtst@dwell=1000/regime/mse_96/mean:3} vs \rhval{sweep_dwell/dlinear@dwell=1000/regime/mse_96/mean:3} at 1000, \rhval{sweep_dwell/patchtst@dwell=4000/regime/mse_96/mean:3} vs \rhval{sweep_dwell/dlinear@dwell=4000/regime/mse_96/mean:3} at 4000); the GRU margin does move in the predicted direction (from \rhval{sweep_dwell/gru@dwell=100/regime/mse_96/mean:3}, better than DLinear in 3/3 seeds, at dwell 100 to \rhval{sweep_dwell/gru@dwell=4000/regime/mse_96/mean:3}, far worse, at 4000) but never reaches a win. At 2000 steps the GRU wins at dwell 100 (\rhval{sweep_dwell_2000/gru@dwell=100/regime/mse_96/mean:3} vs \rhval{sweep_dwell_2000/dlinear@dwell=100/regime/mse_96/mean:3}) and 500 (\rhval{sweep_steps/gru@steps=2000/regime/mse_96/mean:3} vs \rhval{sweep_steps/dlinear@steps=2000/regime/mse_96/mean:3}), is lower at 250 (\rhval{sweep_dwell_2000/gru@dwell=250/regime/mse_96/mean:3} vs \rhval{sweep_dwell_2000/dlinear@dwell=250/regime/mse_96/mean:3}) but better in only 2/3 seeds, and has no win at 1000 (\rhval{sweep_dwell_2000/gru@dwell=1000/regime/mse_96/mean:3} vs \rhval{sweep_dwell_2000/dlinear@dwell=1000/regime/mse_96/mean:3}) or 4000 (\rhval{sweep_dwell_2000/gru@dwell=4000/regime/mse_96/mean:3} vs \rhval{sweep_dwell_2000/dlinear@dwell=4000/regime/mse_96/mean:3}): its wins are confined to mean regime lengths up to 500 steps, but the margin peaks at 500 and does not grow monotonically with the switching rate. PatchTST has no win in the 2000-step dwell sweep.

\textbf{H3 (more noise erases the nonlinear advantage): mixed; tested at 400 steps only.} Table~\ref{tab:noise}: PatchTST wins under the rule at noise sd 0.1 (\rhval{sweep_noise/patchtst@noise=0.1/regime/mse_96/mean:3} against \rhval{sweep_noise/dlinear@noise=0.1/regime/mse_96/mean:3} for DLinear) and at no higher noise level (\rhval{main/patchtst/regime/mse_96/mean:3} vs \rhval{main/dlinear/regime/mse_96/mean:3} at 0.3, \rhval{sweep_noise/patchtst@noise=0.6/regime/mse_96/mean:3} vs \rhval{sweep_noise/dlinear@noise=0.6/regime/mse_96/mean:3} at 0.6, \rhval{sweep_noise/patchtst@noise=1.5/regime/mse_96/mean:3} vs \rhval{sweep_noise/dlinear@noise=1.5/regime/mse_96/mean:3} at 1.5 and \rhval{sweep_noise/patchtst@noise=3.0/regime/mse_96/mean:3} vs \rhval{sweep_noise/dlinear@noise=3.0/regime/mse_96/mean:3} at 3.0), so its above-threshold advantage disappears, but a small advantage in 3/3 seeds remains at every noise level. The GRU runs against the hypothesis: it is worse than DLinear at 0.1 (\rhval{sweep_noise/gru@noise=0.1/regime/mse_96/mean:3}) and 0.3 (\rhval{main/gru/regime/mse_96/mean:3}), close to it at 0.6 (\rhval{sweep_noise/gru@noise=0.6/regime/mse_96/mean:3}) and 1.5 (\rhval{sweep_noise/gru@noise=1.5/regime/mse_96/mean:3}), and better in 3/3 seeds at 3.0 (\rhval{sweep_noise/gru@noise=3.0/regime/mse_96/mean:3}). A shrinking \emph{relative} gap is expected in any case, since all MSEs approach the noise variance.

\textbf{H4 (ETTh1: DLinear not beaten at $H=96,192$; all learned models beat seasonal naive): supported at all three budgets.} At 400 steps DLinear has MSE \rhval{main/dlinear/etth1/mse_96/mean:3} and \rhval{main/dlinear/etth1/mse_192/mean:3} at $H=96$ and 192; the MSE ratios to DLinear are \rhval{cmp/main/patchtst/etth1/mse_96/inv_ratio:4} and \rhval{cmp/main/patchtst/etth1/mse_192/inv_ratio:4} for PatchTST and \rhval{cmp/main/gru/etth1/mse_96/inv_ratio:4} and \rhval{cmp/main/gru/etth1/mse_192/inv_ratio:4} for the GRU (Tables~\ref{tab:main}, \ref{tab:rel}). At 2000 steps both are again worse in all seeds (PatchTST \rhval{sweep_steps/patchtst@steps=2000/etth1/mse_96/mean:3} and \rhval{sweep_steps/patchtst@steps=2000/etth1/mse_192/mean:3}, GRU \rhval{sweep_steps/gru@steps=2000/etth1/mse_96/mean:3} and \rhval{sweep_steps/gru@steps=2000/etth1/mse_192/mean:3}, against \rhval{sweep_steps/dlinear@steps=2000/etth1/mse_96/mean:3} and \rhval{sweep_steps/dlinear@steps=2000/etth1/mse_192/mean:3} for DLinear; Table~\ref{tab:budget}). At $H=24$ both are slightly better in all seeds at 2000 steps (\rhval{sweep_steps/patchtst@steps=2000/etth1/mse_24/mean:3} and \rhval{sweep_steps/gru@steps=2000/etth1/mse_24/mean:3} against \rhval{sweep_steps/dlinear@steps=2000/etth1/mse_24/mean:3}), below the threshold. Every learned model beats seasonal naive (\rhval{main/seasonal-naive/etth1/mse_24/mean:3}, \rhval{main/seasonal-naive/etth1/mse_96/mean:3}, \rhval{main/seasonal-naive/etth1/mse_192/mean:3}) at all horizons and budgets.

\textbf{Where the nonlinear models fail.} (i) Short budget: at 400 steps the GRU is the worst learned model on multiseas at every horizon and on etth1 at $H=24$ (at $H=96$ and 192 it is tied with PatchTST, \rhval{main/gru/etth1/mse_96/mean:3} vs \rhval{main/patchtst/etth1/mse_96/mean:3} and \rhval{main/gru/etth1/mse_192/mean:3} vs \rhval{main/patchtst/etth1/mse_192/mean:3}); the multiseas gap vanishes at 2000 steps, so it reflects under-training. (ii) Long horizon: no nonlinear model wins at $H=192$ on regime data at any budget. (iii) Long regimes: at dwell 4000 the GRU is worse than DLinear at both budgets (\rhval{sweep_dwell_2000/gru@dwell=4000/regime/mse_96/mean:3} vs \rhval{sweep_dwell_2000/dlinear@dwell=4000/regime/mse_96/mean:3} at 2000 steps, \rhval{sweep_dwell/gru@dwell=4000/regime/mse_96/mean:3} vs \rhval{sweep_dwell/dlinear@dwell=4000/regime/mse_96/mean:3} at 400). (iv) ETTh1: both nonlinear models are worse at $H=96$ and 192 at every budget. Seasonal naive is far worse than every learned model on regime (\rhval{main/seasonal-naive/regime/mse_96/mean:3} vs \rhval{main/dlinear/regime/mse_96/mean:3} for DLinear at $H=96$).

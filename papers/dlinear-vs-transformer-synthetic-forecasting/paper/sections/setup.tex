\textbf{Tasks.} Each synthetic dataset has 4 channels of 8000 steps (60/20/20 chronological train/validation/test); its generator seed is $1000+$seed, so a seed is a new dataset (shared by all systems) as well as a new initialisation. A channel is $y_t=\sum_k A_k s_t\sin\phi_{k,t}+\text{trend}_t+e_t$ with integrated phase $\phi_{k,t}=\phi_{k,0}+\sum_{u\le t}2\pi/P_{k,u}$ and AR(1) noise $e_t$ ($\rho=0.5$, stationary sd = ``noise''). \texttt{seas1}: one period 24, noise 0.3. \texttt{trend}: seas1 plus a piecewise-linear trend (slope redrawn every 600--1400 steps). \texttt{multiseas}: periods 24/168/12 with amplitudes 1/0.7/0.4. \texttt{noisy}: multiseas with noise 1.5. \texttt{regime}: multiseas where a Markov chain over three regimes (geometric dwell, mean 500 steps) rescales periods by $1/1.6/0.6$ and amplitudes. \texttt{etth1}: the public ETTh1 CSV, first 14400 rows, 7 channels, split 8640/2880/2880; seeds change only initialisation and batch sampling. All data are z-scored per channel with training statistics.

\textbf{Metrics.} Test MSE and MAE in z-scored units over all windows with stride 1 (no dropped batch), pooled over channels. Mean $\pm$ sd over 3 seeds (0, 1, 2).

\textbf{Experiments.} Main grid: 4 systems $\times$ 6 tasks $\times$ 3 seeds (72 runs, each covering the 3 horizons with separately trained models). Design ablation: no instance norm for each learned model and no decomposition for DLinear, $H=96$, tasks trend/regime/etth1 (36 runs). Sweeps on the regime task at $H=96$: mean dwell $\in\{100,250,1000,4000\}$ and noise sd $\in\{0.1,0.6,1.5,3.0\}$ (72 runs); the default dwell 500 and noise 0.3 rows are the main-grid regime rows at $H=96$ (models are trained per horizon with the same seed, so these equal a separate $H=96$ run). No hyper-parameter was tuned on any split.

\textbf{Training-budget sweep (post hoc).} After an independent audit found that the 400-step verdicts changed at 2000 steps, we added 108 runs with the same code and settings except the number of steps: (a) regime and etth1 at 1000 and 2000 steps, all three horizons; (b) seas1, trend, multiseas and noisy at 2000 steps, $H=96$ only; (c) the dwell sweep repeated at 2000 steps, $H=96$. Each cell has the three learned systems and three seeds; the 400-step cells are the main-grid rows. The noise sweep and the design ablation were not repeated. This sweep is not in the registered plan; the win rule applied to it is.

\textbf{Hardware and runtime.} A shared CPU machine, 2 threads per process, at most two processes at a time. Parameter counts and median per-run wall-clock (under load from other jobs, so only indicative) are in Table~\ref{tab:size}; two main-grid PatchTST runs exceeded six minutes under heavy machine load (durations are in the registry), all others stayed below. Code, runners, per-run logs and the registry (\texttt{results/runs.jsonl}) are in the repository. Two discard-and-rerun events preceded the reported runs (both recorded with \texttt{rh decide}): the first launch labelled every run with one system name (a shell bug in the runner), and stray processes from that launch then wrote further mislabelled rows. Both times the affected registry rows and raw files were removed and all runs redone; logs of the discarded runs were not kept, so the registry lists the runs that were kept, not every process started.

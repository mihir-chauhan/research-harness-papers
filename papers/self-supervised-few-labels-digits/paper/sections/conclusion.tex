On $8\times8$ digits, a SimCLR-style pretrained encoder with a linear probe was the best system at all three label budgets, with a large margin at 10 labels, a smaller one at 50, and no resolvable margin over supervised scratch at 200. Rotation prediction did not help and was below a random encoder. Both augmentation families contribute, and the temperature and epoch settings matter at the scale of a standard deviation. These findings hold for this dataset, architecture and five-seed budget only.

\textbf{H1: CFG raises class accuracy (supported).} On \texttt{mix\_overlap}, CFG $w=3$ gives 1.000 against 0.926 for unguided sampling (Table~\ref{tab:main}, mean difference \rhval{cmp/main/unguided-conditional/mix_overlap/class_acc/delta:4}, paired $p=$\,\rhval{cmp/main/unguided-conditional/mix_overlap/class_acc/paired_p:sci1}, Table~\ref{tab:hyp}). Real samples score 0.915 there, so the guided samples are \emph{more} class-typical than the data themselves: the metric measures separation from other classes, not fidelity to $p(x|c)$. On the separated task there is little to gain (unguided 0.993, Table~\ref{tab:sep}).

\textbf{H2: CFG distorts mode weights and reduces spread (supported, with a caveat).} At $w=3$ mode\_tv rises from 0.017 to 0.129 and std\_ratio drops from 0.917 to 0.800 (paired $p=$\,\rhval{cmp/main/unguided-conditional/mix_overlap/mode_tv/paired_p:sci1} and \rhval{cmp/main/unguided-conditional/mix_overlap/std_ratio/paired_p:sci1}, Table~\ref{tab:hyp}). Mode coverage falls from 1.000 to 0.850, and the cost increases with $w$ (coverage 0.633 and mode\_tv 0.229 at $w=8$, Table~\ref{tab:sweep}). Table~\ref{tab:permode} shows where the mass goes: the minor mode (weight 0.2) keeps 0.63 of its mass at $w=3$ and 0.34 at $w=8$, while the major mode is inflated to 1.18 and 1.36. The sliced Wasserstein distance to the true class distribution grows monotonically with $w$ (0.120 to 1.473). However, the variance is \emph{not} monotonically collapsed: std\_ratio is lowest around $w=1$--2 (0.748, 0.752; not distinguishable) and rises to 1.083 at $w=8$ (Fig.~\ref{fig:w}). std\_ratio is computed on in-support samples only, so this rise coincides with samples being pushed to the rim of the $3\sigma$ ball and off the support (off\_support 0.055 at $w=8$); we did not isolate the cause.

\textbf{H3: CFG's gain is not reproduced by temperature (supported for the registered comparison, qualified).} CFG $w=3$ has higher class accuracy than $\tau=0.5$ (1.000 vs 0.993, paired $p=$\,\rhval{cmp/main/low-temperature-tau-0.5/mix_overlap/class_acc/paired_p:sci1}). But the two retain very different variance (std\_ratio 0.800 vs 0.527), so this test alone is not a matched comparison. In the exploratory matched-spread view (Fig.~\ref{fig:frontier}), CFG $w=0.5$ (std 0.789, acc 0.990, tv 0.030) beats $\tau=0.8$ (std 0.757, acc 0.966, tv 0.050), and CFG $w=1$ (std 0.748, acc 0.999, tv 0.052) beats $\tau=0.8$ in accuracy at similar mode\_tv. Temperature is not free either: it also removes the minor mode (0.44 of its mass at $\tau=0.5$, coverage 0.700) and its swd rises to 0.899. Temperature keeps full mode coverage down to $\tau=0.7$ (std 0.676, lower than any CFG point with full coverage) and has coverage 0.700 at $\tau=0.5$; with only the grid points shown we cannot say where the transition lies. At $w\ge2$ guidance gives up coverage (0.950 at $w=2$) while temperature at $\tau=0.7$ (std 0.676) has not yet; i.e.\ if one wants accuracy $\ge0.98$ with full coverage, $\tau=0.7$ (0.982, coverage 1.000) is a competitive point on this grid, while CFG $w\le1$ reaches 0.999 with coverage 1.000. We make no claim beyond this grid.

\textbf{H4: the cost depends on class overlap (refuted).} The increase in mode\_tv from $w=0$ to $w=3$ is practically identical on the two tasks (0.017 to 0.129 on overlap; 0.018 to 0.129 on separation, Tables~\ref{tab:sweep},\ref{tab:sep}; mean difference \rhval{cmp/main/unguided-conditional/mix_overlap/mode_tv/delta:4} on overlap and \rhval{cmp/main/unguided-conditional/mix_sep/mode_tv/delta:4} on separation). The registered test for H4, a Welch test on the per-seed difference of differences across the two tasks, is not a statistic that \texttt{rh compare} recomputes from the registry, so its value is not reported; the verdict rests on these two mean differences. Mode loss is therefore not an artefact of class overlap in our setting; what the overlap changes is the benefit (H1) and the variance: the std\_ratio drop from $w=0$ to $w=3$ is larger on the separated task (\rhval{cmp/main/unguided-conditional/mix_sep/std_ratio/delta:3} vs \rhval{cmp/main/unguided-conditional/mix_overlap/std_ratio/delta:3}) and the swd rise is smaller (\rhval{cmp/main/unguided-conditional/mix_sep/swd/delta:3} vs \rhval{cmp/main/unguided-conditional/mix_overlap/swd/delta:3}); these are post hoc descriptive comparisons of mean differences, with no test across the two tasks reported.

\textbf{H5: very large $w$ leaves the support (supported descriptively, small sample).} Off-support mass at $w=8$ is 0.055 vs 0.007 unguided. The registered paired test compares runs of two registry groups (the $w$ sweep and the main runs), which \texttt{rh compare} does not recompute, so no test statistic is reported; in our analysis script this was the only borderline test (Section~\ref{sec:limitations}). On \texttt{mix\_sep} it is 0.058. The $w=8$ run also has the lowest in-support precision (0.945 and 0.942). At $w=3$ off-support mass (0.006) is close to the unguided value (0.007); we did not test this difference.

\textbf{Failure case.} The study's clearest failure of CFG: at $w=8$ (1) the minor modes are largely lost, (2) 5.5\% of samples fall off the support, and (3) swd to the true class distribution is 1.473 against 0.120 unguided, while class accuracy is saturated at 1.000, so accuracy alone would call this the best setting.

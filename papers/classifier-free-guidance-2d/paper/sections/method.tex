\textbf{Data.} A ring of $12$ Gaussian modes, radius $R=4$, centres $\mu_j=R(\cos\frac{2\pi j}{12},\sin\frac{2\pi j}{12})$ with isotropic std $\sigma$. Mode $j$ belongs to class $c_j=j \bmod 4$ and has within-class weight $\pi_j\in\{0.5,0.3,0.2\}$ according to $\lfloor j/4\rfloor$. Hence $p(x|c)=\sum_{j:c_j=c}\pi_j\mathcal N(x;\mu_j,\sigma^2I)$ and neighbouring modes on the ring always belong to different classes.

\textbf{Model.} An $\epsilon$-prediction DDPM \cite{ho2020denoising}, $x_t=\sqrt{\bar\alpha_t}x_0+\sqrt{1-\bar\alpha_t}\,\epsilon$, $T=100$, cosine $\bar\alpha_t$ \cite{nichol2021improved}, loss $\mathbb E\|\epsilon_\theta(x_t,t,\tilde c)-\epsilon\|^2$ where the label is replaced by a null token with probability $p$ (label dropout, $p=0.1$ by default).

\textbf{Classifier-free guidance} \cite{ho2022classifier} (reimplemented), with the convention that $w=0$ is unguided:
\begin{equation}
\tilde\epsilon_\theta(x_t,t,c)=(1+w)\,\epsilon_\theta(x_t,t,c)-w\,\epsilon_\theta(x_t,t,\varnothing).
\end{equation}
Optionally guidance is applied only when $t/T\in[\ell,h]$ (the interval ablation, in the spirit of \cite{kynknniemi2024applying}); otherwise $\tilde\epsilon=\epsilon_\theta(x_t,t,c)$.

\textbf{Sampler.} Ancestral DDPM: $\hat x_0=\mathrm{clip}\big((x_t-\sqrt{1-\bar\alpha_t}\tilde\epsilon)/\sqrt{\bar\alpha_t},\pm3\big)$ (normalised units, identical for all systems), 
$x_{t-1}=\tfrac{\sqrt{\bar\alpha_{t-1}}\beta_t}{1-\bar\alpha_t}\hat x_0+\tfrac{\sqrt{\alpha_t}(1-\bar\alpha_{t-1})}{1-\bar\alpha_t}x_t+\tau\sqrt{\tilde\beta_t}\,z$, $z\sim\mathcal N(0,I)$, $\tilde\beta_t=\beta_t\frac{1-\bar\alpha_{t-1}}{1-\bar\alpha_t}$, with $x_T\sim\mathcal N(0,\tau^2I)$.

\textbf{Low-temperature baseline} (reimplemented): $w=0$ and $\tau<1$, i.e.\ the initial noise and every injected noise are scaled by $\tau$. This is one of several possible definitions of temperature for a diffusion sampler; it is not the same as sampling a tempered density. \textbf{Unguided conditional}: $w=0,\tau=1$. All systems share one trained network per (task, seed).

\textbf{Metrics} (per class, averaged over the 4 classes; $d_k(x)=\|x-\mu_k\|/\sigma$). With $\pi_c$ the true mode weights of class $c$ and $\mathcal M_c$ its modes:
\begin{itemize}
\item \texttt{class\_acc}: fraction of samples whose Bayes-posterior class $\arg\max_{c'}\sum_{j:c_j=c'}\pi_j\mathcal N(x;\mu_j,\sigma^2I)$ (equal class priors) equals the conditioning class. Real samples do not reach 1 on the overlapping task (Table~\ref{tab:sweep}).
\item \texttt{support\_prec}: fraction within $3\sigma$ of some mode in $\mathcal M_c$ (``in the right class region'').
\item \texttt{mode\_cov}: fraction of the three modes in $\mathcal M_c$ that receive at least half of their true share of all samples, counting a sample for the nearest right-class mode if within $3\sigma$.
\item \texttt{mode\_tv}: $\frac12\sum_{k\in\mathcal M_c}|\hat\pi_k-\pi_k|$, with $\hat\pi$ the empirical shares among in-support samples.
\item \texttt{std\_ratio}: $\sqrt{\mathrm{mean}(d_k^2)/2}$ over in-support samples; 1 would be the true within-mode std, less than 1 is variance collapse. Because of the $3\sigma$ truncation, real samples give 0.975.
\item \texttt{off\_support}: fraction farther than $3\sigma$ from every one of the 12 modes. \texttt{swd}: sliced Wasserstein-1 (64 projections) against 1500 fresh true samples of the class.
\end{itemize}

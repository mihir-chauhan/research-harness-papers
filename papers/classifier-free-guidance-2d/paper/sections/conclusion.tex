On a 2D class-conditional mixture, classifier-free guidance buys class separation (class accuracy 0.926 to 0.999 at $w=1$, overlapping task) and pays with minor-mode mass, mode coverage (1.000 to 0.850 at $w=3$) and distance to the true class distribution (swd 0.120 at $w=0$ to 1.473 at $w=8$); beyond $w=5$ samples start leaving the support. Variance is not monotonically collapsed, and the mode-weight distortion did not depend measurably on class overlap (variance loss and sliced-Wasserstein growth did differ between the tasks, post hoc and descriptive). Low-temperature sampling also raises class accuracy and also removes minor modes, but on our grid CFG at small $w$ reaches higher accuracy than temperature at similar retained spread. In this setup, guidance restricted to the low-noise half of the chain retained all modes at essentially the same accuracy, a result worth testing on larger models. Everything here is a five-seed toy-scale result.

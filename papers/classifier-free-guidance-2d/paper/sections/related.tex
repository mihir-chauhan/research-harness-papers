\textbf{Guidance.} Classifier guidance \cite{dhariwal2021diffusion} trades diversity for fidelity using gradients of a noisy classifier, and GLIDE \cite{nichol2021glide} showed that CFG is competitive for text conditioning. CFG \cite{ho2022classifier} removes the classifier and is the baseline we reimplement. Kynk\"a\"anniemi et al.~\cite{kynknniemi2024applying} report that guidance is harmful at high noise levels, largely unnecessary at low ones and beneficial in the middle, and restrict it to an interval; our interval ablation is a toy-scale probe of the same idea, not a replication of their ImageNet results. Moufad et al.~\cite{moufad2025conditional} argue that CFG does not correspond to a well-defined diffusion model and that it reduces diversity; Patel et al.~\cite{patel2023bridging} point to a training/sampling discrepancy and report out-of-distribution samples and mode collapse at high $w$. Our experiments measure these symptoms, but do not test their proposed corrections.

\textbf{Diffusion backbones.} We use the DDPM objective and ancestral sampler \cite{ho2020denoising} with a cosine schedule \cite{nichol2021improved}; \cite{song2020denoising,karras2022elucidating} give alternative samplers that we do not evaluate.

\textbf{Measuring diversity.} Precision and recall for generative models \cite{sajjadi2018assessing,kynknniemi2019improved} separate fidelity from coverage using feature-space neighbourhoods. On a mixture with a known density we can replace them by exact quantities (posterior class, distance to the true mode centres), at the cost of realism. Reduced-temperature (noise-scaled) sampling was already tested for diffusion models by Dhariwal and Nichol \cite{dhariwal2021diffusion} (Appendix G), who report low precision and recall, and Ho and Salimans \cite{ho2022classifier} note that decreasing the sampling noise is ineffective for image quality. We do not add a new idea here: we repeat such a comparison on a toy mixture where mode weights are known, and there temperature does raise class accuracy (Section~\ref{sec:results}); whether this differs from the image findings because of the toy setting or the metrics is not tested.

Classifier-free guidance \cite{ho2022classifier} is the default way to make conditional diffusion models follow their condition: a conditional and an unconditional score are learned by one network with label dropout, and sampling extrapolates from the unconditional towards the conditional prediction by a guidance scale $w$. The original paper already reports a fidelity/diversity trade-off in FID and Inception score as $w$ grows, and classifier guidance \cite{dhariwal2021diffusion} behaved the same way; GLIDE \cite{nichol2021glide} compared CLIP guidance with CFG. Follow-up work reports that CFG reduces diversity and does not sample the distribution suggested by its limiting score \cite{moufad2025conditional}, that large $w$ gives out-of-distribution samples and mode collapse \cite{patel2023bridging}, and that guidance is harmful at high noise levels \cite{kynknniemi2024applying}.

On images these costs are measured indirectly, through FID or feature-space precision and recall \cite{kynknniemi2019improved,sajjadi2018assessing}. Which modes are lost, whether within-mode variance collapses, and whether a similar effect could be had by merely sampling at a lower temperature are not directly observable. We therefore study the question on a 2D class-conditional mixture where modes, weights and the class posterior are known, so that every cost can be computed exactly.

\textbf{Contributions.} (i) A controlled benchmark with seven metrics (class accuracy, in-support precision, mode coverage, mode-weight distortion, variance ratio, off-support mass, sliced Wasserstein distance). (ii) A guidance-scale sweep and a matched temperature sweep on the same trained network, with five seeds. (iii) Two small ablations (label dropout, guidance interval) and a per-mode mass analysis. We state five hypotheses with tests registered in \texttt{proposal.md} (committed one second before the first main run, after a seed-0 smoke run of CFG $w=3$ and an unregistered timing trial that trained the seed-0 \texttt{mix\_overlap} network); one of the five (H4) is refuted and one (H3) is supported only in a qualified sense (Section~\ref{sec:results}). This is a small replication-style study, not a new method.

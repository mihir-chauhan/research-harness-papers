\textbf{Emergent communication.} Referential games with neural agents were popularised by Lazaridou et al.~\cite{lazaridou2016multi} and extended to variable-length symbol sequences trained with Gumbel-softmax and REINFORCE by Havrylov and Titov~\cite{havrylov2017emergence}, who report straight-through Gumbel-softmax converging much faster than REINFORCE, which motivated our H4 and which we did not reproduce; Lazaridou and Baroni survey the field~\cite{lazaridou2020emergent}. Kottur et al.\ show that natural-language-like structure does not emerge naturally~\cite{kottur2017natural}. The EGG toolkit~\cite{kharitonov2019egg} provides standard game implementations; we reimplement a minimal game here rather than use it.

\textbf{Capacity and generalisation.} Resnick et al.~\cite{resnick2019capacity} study how model capacity and channel bandwidth interact with compositionality (model capacity is their foremost factor; we vary only the channel), and Chaabouni et al.~\cite{chaabouni2020compositionality} study generalisation to held-out attribute combinations, the setting adopted in this paper, and the relation of generalisation to compositionality. Kharitonov et al.~\cite{kharitonov2019entropy} link emergent protocols to entropy minimisation.

\textbf{Measuring and encouraging compositionality.} Topographic similarity originates in work on linguistic evolution~\cite{brighton2006understanding}; Korbak et al.~\cite{korbak2020measuring} propose measures of non-trivial compositionality, and van der Wal et al.~\cite{wal2020grammar} analyse the grammar of emergent languages. Pressures that encourage structure include iterated learning~\cite{ren2020compositional} and noise~\cite{kuciski2021catalytic}; we use none, so our learned protocols are the ``default'' case.

\textbf{Discrete-channel estimators.} The Gumbel-softmax / Concrete relaxation~\cite{jang2016categorical,maddison2016concrete} gives biased low-variance gradients through a discrete sample, while REINFORCE~\cite{williams1992simple} gives unbiased high-variance ones. We compare both under the same budget.

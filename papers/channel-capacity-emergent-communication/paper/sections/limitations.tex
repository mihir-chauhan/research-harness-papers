This is a small CPU study: three seeds per cell, 6000 training steps, one architecture and one hidden size, 26 held-out objects per seed (so held-out accuracy moves in steps of 1/26 per seed), and one object space ($4\times4$). The data are synthetic enumerated attribute-value vectors, not natural or perceptual inputs. Held-out accuracy at tight channels is close to zero because Gumbel-softmax training there is unstable (train accuracy peaks near 0.25 within 1400 steps and then collapses, also at 20000 steps) while REINFORCE memorises the training objects without generalising; we ran this longer-training control at V4\_L4 only, and did not tune learning rate, temperature or entropy weight, so differences between estimators and between channel sizes are confounded with optimisation behaviour. The step-budget probe was not recorded. Baselines are reimplemented minimal versions (a moving-average REINFORCE baseline without the variance-reduction tricks of larger systems), not the code of the original papers or of EGG. The oracle and random codes are fixed senders, not competing methods. Topographic similarity with Hamming distances is only one compositionality measure and is known to be insensitive to some structure; we did not compute others~\cite{korbak2020measuring}. $p$-values come from Welch tests with $n=3$ and are not corrected for multiple comparisons. A full study would add many more seeds, tuned temperature and learning rate (to separate instability from capacity), population or iterated-learning pressures~\cite{ren2020compositional}, noise~\cite{kuciski2021catalytic} and larger object spaces.

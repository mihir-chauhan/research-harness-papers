Neural agents that must cooperate through a discrete channel can develop their own protocol, and a long line of work asks whether such emergent languages resemble natural language, in particular whether they are compositional~\cite{lazaridou2016multi,havrylov2017emergence,lazaridou2020emergent}. Early results showed that compositional, human-interpretable protocols do not emerge by default~\cite{kottur2017natural}, and that the channel itself is a key lever: Resnick et al.\ study model capacity alongside channel bandwidth in relation to compositionality (their ``capacity'' is mainly model capacity, whereas we use it for channel bits)~\cite{resnick2019capacity}, and Chaabouni et al.\ show that generalisation to novel attribute combinations emerges when the input space is large enough and does not require compositionality~\cite{chaabouni2020compositionality}.

We ask a deliberately narrow, controlled version of this question on a small CPU budget. In a Lewis reconstruction game, a speaker sees an object with four attributes of four values each (256 objects, 8 bits) and sends $L$ symbols from a vocabulary of size $V$; a listener must reconstruct every attribute. We train both agents on 90\% of the combinations and test on the held-out 10\%. We vary $V$ and $L$ (hence the capacity $L\log_2 V$ bits), compare Gumbel-softmax~\cite{jang2016categorical,maddison2016concrete} with REINFORCE~\cite{williams1992simple} senders, and compare with two fixed-sender references: a random code and an oracle compositional code. Our hypotheses (Sec.~\ref{sec:setup}) are that (H1) capacity below 8 bits caps accuracy, (H2) a channel far larger than needed generalises worse and is less compositional than a tight one, (H3) learned protocols are far less compositional than the oracle code, and (H4) Gumbel-softmax beats REINFORCE.

\textbf{Contributions.} (i) A small replication-style grid over $V$ and $L$ with held-out combinations and topographic similarity~\cite{brighton2006understanding}; (ii) two fixed-sender references that separate what a listener can learn from a code from what the speaker invents; (iii) an honest account of where the study fails: the tight channels are not learned (Gumbel-softmax collapses after an early peak, REINFORCE is still improving at the end of the budget), so H2 cannot be tested as stated and the observed direction is the reverse. The short step-budget probe that fixed the 6000-step budget was not recorded in the registry (Sec.~\ref{sec:setup}). No claim is made beyond this scale.

# Landscape
Cosine decay with restarts (Loshchilov & Hutter 2016) and AdamW (2017) are the default recipe; warmup is motivated by Adam's early variance (Liu et al. 2019, RAdam), and studied for GPT training by Kalra & Barkeshli 2024 and Kosson et al. 2024. Schedule-Free (Defazio et al. 2024) removes the horizon-dependent schedule via iterate averaging; benchmarks (Schmidt et al. 2020; Dahl et al. 2023) show tuning budget and schedule dominate optimiser rankings. Gap: the interaction of schedule choice and peak-LR sensitivity in a very short, tiny setting, with a defined sensitivity metric.

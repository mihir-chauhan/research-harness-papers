# Landscape
Diffusion models (Ho et al. 2020; Song et al. 2020 score SDE) sample by reversing a noising process; DDIM (Song et al.) gives a deterministic few-step sampler, Improved DDPM and EDM study schedules and samplers. Flow matching (Lipman et al. 2022), rectified flow (Liu et al. 2022) and stochastic interpolants (Albergo & Vanden-Eijnden 2022) regress a velocity field along (near-)straight paths; rectified flow adds reflow, re-training on the model's own (noise, sample) couplings. Minibatch-OT flow matching (Tong et al.) straightens paths at training time. Convergence of reflow is analysed by Bansal et al. 2024, and Wang et al. 2024 (Rectified Diffusion) argue that first-order ODE-ness, not straightness, is what matters. Consistency models distil to one step. The gap: the published comparisons are mostly image benchmarks on a shared architecture scored by FID/likelihood (Lipman et al. state that all compared models use the same architecture and hyper-parameters; they report NLL, FID and adaptive-solver NFE); a controlled 2D same-MLP replication adds held-out SW/MMD over the step count, a DDIM sampler baseline and seed variance, and shows what the straightness story does and does not buy at this scale.

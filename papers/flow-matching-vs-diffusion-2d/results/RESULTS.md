# Results (5 seeds main, 3 seeds ablations; tables in results/tables, tests in results/tests.json)
- H1 (FM < DDIM SW at K=4, per task): PARTLY SUPPORTED. Eight Gaussians and checkerboard FM better (paired p 5e-4, 7e-3); two moons DDIM better in SW only (0.203 vs 0.263, p 2e-3); MMD at K=4 favours FM on all three tasks incl. two moons (0.0145 vs 0.0252), so the reversal is metric-dependent; checkerboard gap (0.013 SW) is ~1 DDIM std. Registered refutation rule (DDIM not worse on >=2 tasks) not triggered.
- H2 (ratio closer to 1 at K=100): NOMINALLY REFUTED by the registered rule / mixed. Ratios K=4 -> K=100: 3.34->2.15 (8G, closer), 0.77->1.25 (moons, |r-1| .23->.25, log-ratio slightly closer), 1.06->1.73 (checker, farther).
- H3 (reflow lowers K=1 SW, no gain at K=100): SUPPORTED. K=1 SW 0.042/0.028/0.032 vs 1.234/1.145/0.931; K=100 difference <=0.0012 and slightly worse (p<0.05).
- K=1: FM has lower SW but 3-4x higher MMD than DDIM on all tasks; neither usable.
- H4 (FM straighter than DDIM; reflow straighter still): FIRST PART REFUTED (FM 0.657/0.676/0.450 < DDIM 0.752/0.814/0.777), second part supported (reflow >=0.998).
- Ablations: cosine DDIM fails without a truncated start (SW>1 at K=100), truncated start recovers K=100 but is worse than linear at K=16; reflow with a 4-step teacher plateaus at the teacher's quality; second reflow round changes SW by <=0.009.
- Failure cases: FM on two moons at K=2..16; one-step FM MMD worse than DDIM's despite lower SW.
- H2 detail (exploratory, results/tables/expl.tex): SW@100 FM-DDIM = -0.030 (p 0.086, eight Gaussians; DDIM std 5x FM's), -0.006 (p 0.048, two moons), -0.022 (p<0.001, checkerboard).
- Ancestral vs DDIM at K=4: worse on two moons and checkerboard (SW and MMD, p<0.001); on eight Gaussians within seed noise (SW p 0.068, MMD gap 0.0087, p 0.050).
- Heun at equal NFE (exploratory): at 8 NFE Heun (K=4) beats FM-Euler (K=8): -0.008 (p 0.034), -0.083, -0.074 (p<0.001); at 4 NFE Heun is worse on eight Gaussians (+0.051) and better on the other two; at 2 NFE worse on all three tasks.
- Where the tests come from: all registered and exploratory paired tests are computed by experiments/analyze.py from results/runs.jsonl (results/tests.json; tables tests.tex, expl.tex, reflow_paired.tex, and tests_all.tex, which is the compact form of the same numbers used as Table III of the paper). `rh compare` outputs for sw_k1, sw_k4, sw_k100, mmd_k1, mmd_k4 and straight on each task are in results/tables/compare_main_<metric>_<task>.csv and give the same paired p values for the comparisons against Flow Matching (Euler).

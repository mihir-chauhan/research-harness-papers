\textbf{Diffusion models and fast samplers.} DDPM~\cite{ho2020denoising} trains a network to predict the noise added by a fixed forward process; Song et al.~\cite{song2020score} unify this with score-based models through SDEs and a probability-flow ODE. DDIM~\cite{song2020denoising} observes that the same training objective supports a family of non-Markovian samplers with a tunable noise level $\eta$ on a sub-sampled timestep grid, which is the few-step diffusion baseline we use ($\eta{=}0$ and $\eta{=}1$). Improved DDPM~\cite{nichol2021improved} introduced the cosine schedule that we use in an ablation, and EDM~\cite{karras2022elucidating} studies how preconditioning, noise levels and solvers interact in sampling cost; we do not reimplement EDM.

\textbf{Flow matching and rectified flow.} Flow matching~\cite{lipman2022flow} regresses a conditional vector field and obtains simulation-free training of continuous normalising flows. Rectified flow~\cite{liu2022flow} proposes the straight interpolation and the reflow procedure, and stochastic interpolants~\cite{albergo2022building} give a unifying view; the flow matching guide~\cite{lipman2024flow} collects the formulations and practice. Minibatch optimal-transport couplings~\cite{tong2023improving} straighten paths at training time instead of through reflow. Theory of reflow is developed in~\cite{bansal2024convergence}, and Rectified Diffusion~\cite{wang2024rectified} argues that what matters for few-step quality is that the training target is a first-order ODE with a pre-trained pairing, not straightness itself; our straightness measurements and the diffusion-schedule ablation bear on this question only in 2D. Consistency models~\cite{song2023consistency} distil a pretrained model, or are trained directly, for one-step generation, which we do not study.

\textbf{Metrics.} Sliced Wasserstein distances~\cite{bonneel2015sliced,kolouri2019generalized} average one-dimensional Wasserstein distances over random projections and are cheap in 2D; we use the sliced $W_1$ with a fixed set of directions together with a kernel MMD, and report the distance between fresh true samples and the reference as a noise floor.

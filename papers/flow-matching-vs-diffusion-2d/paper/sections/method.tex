All systems share one velocity/noise network $f_\theta(x,t)$: an MLP with four hidden layers of width 128, SiLU activations, and as input $x\in\mathbb R^2$, the scalar $t\in[0,1]$ and the sin/cos Fourier features of $t$ at eight frequencies $2^j\pi$, $j=0,\dots,7$. Let $p$ be the data density and $z\sim\mathcal N(0,I)$.

\textbf{DDPM (reimplemented).} With $T{=}1000$ steps, $\beta_i$ linear from $10^{-4}$ to $0.02$ and $\bar\alpha_i=\prod_{j\le i}(1-\beta_j)$, we draw $x_0\sim p$, $i\sim\mathcal U\{0,\dots,T{-}1\}$, $\epsilon\sim\mathcal N(0,I)$, set $x_i=\sqrt{\bar\alpha_i}x_0+\sqrt{1-\bar\alpha_i}\,\epsilon$ and minimise
\begin{equation}\mathcal L_{\rm DDPM}=\mathbb E\,\|f_\theta(x_i,i/T)-\epsilon\|^2 .\end{equation}
\textbf{DDIM sampler (reimplemented).} On the uniform sub-grid $\tau_k=\lfloor(T{-}1)(1-(k{-}1)/K)\rfloor$, $k=1,\dots,K$, of timestep indices (the last step goes to $\bar\alpha{:=}1$), $\hat x_0=(x-\sqrt{1-\bar\alpha_\tau}\hat\epsilon)/\sqrt{\bar\alpha_\tau}$ and
\begin{equation}x'=\sqrt{\bar\alpha_{\tau'}}\hat x_0+\sqrt{1-\bar\alpha_{\tau'}-\sigma^2}\,\hat\epsilon+\sigma\xi,\quad \sigma=\eta\sqrt{\tfrac{1-\bar\alpha_{\tau'}}{1-\bar\alpha_\tau}}\sqrt{1-\tfrac{\bar\alpha_\tau}{\bar\alpha_{\tau'}}},\end{equation}
with $\hat\epsilon=f_\theta(x,\tau/T)$ and $\xi\sim\mathcal N(0,I)$. $\eta{=}0$ is ``DDIM'' and $\eta{=}1$ is the strided ancestral DDPM sampler (``DDPM ancestral''). No clipping of $\hat x_0$ is applied. In the cosine ablation $\bar\alpha$ follows~\cite{nichol2021improved} (offset $0.008$, $\beta\le0.999$), and the diagnostic variant replaces $T{-}1$ in the grid by the last index with $\bar\alpha\ge4\times10^{-5}$.

\textbf{Conditional flow matching (reimplemented).} With noise at $t{=}0$ and data at $t{=}1$, $x_t=(1-t)z+tx_1$, $x_1\sim p$, $t\sim\mathcal U[0,1]$:
\begin{equation}\mathcal L_{\rm FM}=\mathbb E\,\|f_\theta(x_t,t)-(x_1-z)\|^2 .\end{equation}
Sampling integrates $\dot x=f_\theta(x,t)$ from $t{=}0$ to $1$ with $K$ uniform steps of Euler, $x\leftarrow x+\Delta f_\theta(x,t)$, $\Delta=1/K$, or Heun, $x\leftarrow x+\tfrac{\Delta}{2}\big(f_\theta(x,t)+f_\theta(\tilde x,t{+}\Delta)\big)$, $\tilde x=x+\Delta f_\theta(x,t)$, which costs two network evaluations per step.

\textbf{Reflow (reimplemented).} Given a trained flow model $f^{(r-1)}$, draw $N{=}20000$ noise samples $z_n$ and compute $x_n=\mathrm{ODE}_{100}(z_n;f^{(r-1)})$ with 100 Euler steps (4 in one ablation). The student $f^{(r)}$ is initialised from $f^{(r-1)}$ and trained with $\mathcal L_{\rm FM}$ on the fixed pairs $(z_n,x_n)$, $x_t=(1-t)z_n+tx_n$, with the same optimiser and number of updates. ``Reflow-1'' uses the FM model as teacher; ``Reflow-2'' uses Reflow-1.

\textbf{Metrics.} For $n{=}10000$ generated points $x_i$ and the $10000$ held-out points $y_i$, the sliced $W_1$ is $\mathrm{SW}=\frac1L\sum_{l=1}^L\frac1n\sum_{i}|x_{(i)}^\top u_l-y_{(i)}^\top u_l|$ with $L{=}256$ fixed unit directions $u_l$ and sorted projections. MMD is the biased estimate $\mathrm{MMD}^2=\overline{k(x,x')}+\overline{k(y,y')}-2\,\overline{k(x,y)}$ with $k$ the mean of four RBF kernels (bandwidths $0.1,0.3,1,3$) on the first 3000 points of each set. Straightness is the mean over 2000 samples of $\|x_{\rm end}-x_{\rm start}\|/\sum_k\|x_{k+1}-x_k\|$ along the 100-step deterministic path of the system's own sampler (Euler or Heun for flows, DDIM for diffusion); it equals 1 for a straight line. It is a scale-free chord-to-arc ratio and not the straightness functional $\int_0^1\mathbb E\|(Z_1-Z_0)-\dot Z_t\|^2dt$ of~\cite{liu2022flow}.

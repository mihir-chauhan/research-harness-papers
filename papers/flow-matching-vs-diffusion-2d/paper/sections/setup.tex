\textbf{Tasks and data.} Eight Gaussians: eight components with std $0.1$ on a circle of radius 2. Two moons: two half circles with Gaussian noise $0.05$, centred and scaled by 2. Checkerboard: uniform on the black cells of a $4\times4$ board on $[-2,2]^2$. Training batches are sampled on-line; the held-out reference is 10000 points from a fixed generator seed that is never used for training.

\textbf{Training.} All networks: same MLP, Adam with learning rate $2\times10^{-3}$ cosine-annealed to 0, 3000 updates, batch 512, EMA of the weights (decay 0.999 with warm-up), and the EMA weights are used for sampling. Reflow students use the same settings on a pool of 20000 pairs. These values were chosen once, before any run, and not tuned for any system; the reduction from 5000 to 3000 updates was made before the main runs to fit the CPU budget (recorded with \texttt{rh decide}). The tuning budget of every system is therefore zero.

\textbf{Evaluation.} Step counts $K\in\{1,2,4,8,16,32,64,100\}$ on uniform grids, the same for every system; one network evaluation per step for Euler, DDIM and ancestral sampling and two for Heun. Seeds 0--4 (main) and 0--2 (ablations); the seed fixes initialisation, training stream and sampling noise, and all systems of a seed share the sampling noise generator seed. Paired tests use the seed as pairing key; all tests are two-sided paired $t$-tests without multiplicity correction, computed from the run registry by \texttt{experiments/analyze.py} (the harness's \texttt{rh compare} gives the same paired $p$ for the comparisons against FM-Euler). ``Real data'' is a fresh set of 10000 true samples scored against the reference with the same metrics and gives the floor reachable with a perfect sampler. All runs are on a shared laptop CPU with two threads; one run (training plus the 8 step counts) takes about 3 to 54~s, and the registered runs, launched two at a time, were completed within about 35 minutes of wall-clock. Code and logs: \texttt{method/run.py}, \texttt{results/runs.jsonl}.

\textbf{H1: flow matching beats DDIM at four steps} --- \emph{partly supported; reversed on two moons}. At $K{=}4$ FM-Euler has lower SW than DDIM on eight Gaussians (Table~\ref{tab:sw}; $p=\rhval{cmp/main/flow-matching-euler/eight_gaussians/sw_k4/paired_p:4}$ in Table~\ref{tab:tests}) and on the checkerboard ($p=\rhval{cmp/main/flow-matching-euler/checkerboard/sw_k4/paired_p:3}$), but the checkerboard gap (\rhval{cmp/main/flow-matching-euler/checkerboard/sw_k4/delta:3}) is about one DDIM seed standard deviation, and on two moons DDIM has the lower \emph{SW} (\rhval{main/ddim/two_moons/sw_k4/mean:3} against \rhval{main/flow-matching-euler/two_moons/sw_k4/mean:3} for FM, $p=\rhval{cmp/main/flow-matching-euler/two_moons/sw_k4/paired_p:3}$; the same direction nominally at $K{=}2$, 8 and 16, where the $K{=}16$ gap is small). This reversal is specific to SW: the MMD at $K{=}4$ on the same runs favours FM on every task, including two moons (\rhval{main/flow-matching-euler/two_moons/mmd_k4/mean:4} against \rhval{main/ddim/two_moons/mmd_k4/mean:4}, Table~\ref{tab:mmd}; paired $p$ of \rhval{cmp/main/flow-matching-euler/eight_gaussians/mmd_k4/paired_p:3}, \rhval{cmp/main/flow-matching-euler/two_moons/mmd_k4/paired_p:sci1} and \rhval{cmp/main/flow-matching-euler/checkerboard/mmd_k4/paired_p:4} on the three tasks), so by MMD H1 holds on all three tasks and the two-moons failure is metric-dependent. The registered refutation rule (DDIM not worse on at least two tasks) is not triggered, but ``flow matching gives better few-step samples'' is not a general statement in this study. At $K{=}1$ FM has the lower SW on all tasks, yet its MMD is higher than that of DDIM on all three, so the two metrics disagree there; we have not isolated why. DDPM ancestral and DDIM coincide at $K{=}1$ (the last step has no noise). At $K{=}4$ ancestral sampling is worse than DDIM in SW and MMD on two moons and the checkerboard (paired $p$ at most \rhval{cmp/main/ddpm-ancestral/checkerboard/sw_k4/paired_p:sci1}, Table~\ref{tab:tests}); on eight Gaussians both differences are within seed noise (SW \rhval{main/ddpm-ancestral/eight_gaussians/sw_k4/mean:3} $\pm$ \rhval{main/ddpm-ancestral/eight_gaussians/sw_k4/std:3} against \rhval{main/ddim/eight_gaussians/sw_k4/mean:3} $\pm$ \rhval{main/ddim/eight_gaussians/sw_k4/std:3}; the MMD difference DDIM$-$ancestral of \rhval{cmp/main/ddpm-ancestral/eight_gaussians/mmd_k4/delta:4} is about one DDIM seed standard deviation in size).

\textbf{H2: the gap closes with more steps} --- \emph{nominally refuted; mixed}. The SW ratio DDIM/FM (Table~\ref{tab:tests}) moves towards 1 between $K{=}4$ and $K{=}100$ only on eight Gaussians; on the checkerboard it moves away from 1, and on two moons it changes from DDIM-better to FM-better, with $|r-1|$ (the rule as registered) nominally larger at $K{=}100$ (\rhval{cmp/main/flow-matching-euler/two_moons/sw_k4/ratio:2} to \rhval{cmp/main/ddim/two_moons/sw_k100/inv_ratio:2}), although a log-ratio would call it slightly closer. At $K{=}100$ FM has the lower mean SW on all tasks, but the evidence differs by task. On eight Gaussians DDIM's seed-to-seed spread is about five times that of FM (\rhval{main/ddim/eight_gaussians/sw_k100/mean:3} $\pm$ \rhval{main/ddim/eight_gaussians/sw_k100/std:3} against \rhval{main/flow-matching-euler/eight_gaussians/sw_k100/mean:3} $\pm$ \rhval{main/flow-matching-euler/eight_gaussians/sw_k100/std:3}) and the gap is not significant ($p=\rhval{cmp/main/ddim/eight_gaussians/sw_k100/paired_p:3}$); on two moons the spreads are similar and the FM$-$DDIM difference of \rhval{cmp/main/ddim/two_moons/sw_k100/delta:3} is marginal ($p=\rhval{cmp/main/ddim/two_moons/sw_k100/paired_p:3}$); on the checkerboard the gap is consistent across seeds (\rhval{main/ddim/checkerboard/sw_k100/mean:3} $\pm$ \rhval{main/ddim/checkerboard/sw_k100/std:3} against \rhval{main/flow-matching-euler/checkerboard/sw_k100/mean:3} $\pm$ \rhval{main/flow-matching-euler/checkerboard/sw_k100/std:3}, $p=\rhval{cmp/main/ddim/checkerboard/sw_k100/paired_p:sci1}$), so there DDIM has not reached the FM error by 100 steps.

\textbf{H3: one reflow round} --- \emph{supported}. Reflow-1 lowers one-step SW on every task by a large margin (for example \rhval{main/reflow-1-euler/eight_gaussians/sw_k1/mean:3} against \rhval{main/flow-matching-euler/eight_gaussians/sw_k1/mean:3} on eight Gaussians, $p=\rhval{cmp/main/reflow-1-euler/eight_gaussians/sw_k1/paired_p:sci1}$) and stays close to the real-data floor at every $K$ of Table~\ref{tab:sw} on every task, while its MMD stays above the floor (\rhval{main/reflow-1-euler/checkerboard/mmd_k4/mean:4} against \rhval{main/real-data-floor/checkerboard/mmd_k4/mean:4} on the checkerboard at $K{=}4$). At $K{=}100$ it does not improve: the FM$-$Reflow difference of SW is \rhval{cmp/main/reflow-1-euler/eight_gaussians/sw_k100/delta:4}, \rhval{cmp/main/reflow-1-euler/two_moons/sw_k100/delta:4} and \rhval{cmp/main/reflow-1-euler/checkerboard/sw_k100/delta:4} on the three tasks, that is, Reflow-1 is slightly \emph{worse}, with $p=\rhval{cmp/main/reflow-1-euler/eight_gaussians/sw_k100/paired_p:3}$, \rhval{cmp/main/reflow-1-euler/two_moons/sw_k100/paired_p:3} and \rhval{cmp/main/reflow-1-euler/checkerboard/sw_k100/paired_p:4}. Reflow-1 received twice the updates of FM-Euler plus 20000 teacher trajectories (no FM control).

\textbf{H4: straightness} --- \emph{first part refuted, second part supported}. By chord/arc length FM paths (\rhval{main/flow-matching-euler/eight_gaussians/straight/mean:4}, \rhval{main/flow-matching-euler/two_moons/straight/mean:4}, \rhval{main/flow-matching-euler/checkerboard/straight/mean:4} for the three tasks) are \emph{less} straight than DDIM paths (\rhval{main/ddim/eight_gaussians/straight/mean:4}, \rhval{main/ddim/two_moons/straight/mean:4}, \rhval{main/ddim/checkerboard/straight/mean:4}; paired $p$ at most \rhval{cmp/main/ddim/eight_gaussians/straight/paired_p:sci1}), while Reflow-1 paths are at \rhval{main/reflow-1-euler/eight_gaussians/straight/mean:4}, \rhval{main/reflow-1-euler/two_moons/straight/mean:4} and \rhval{main/reflow-1-euler/checkerboard/straight/mean:4}. FM is less straight than DDIM on all three tasks, yet has lower SW at $K{=}4$ on two of them and lower MMD on all three, so straightness by this measure does not order the two families by few-step quality. Among the Euler-sampled flow models of Table~\ref{tab:sw}, the straighter one (Reflow-1) is the better model at $K\le4$; the 4-step-teacher student of Sec.~\ref{sec:ablations} shows that the converse does not hold. The measure compares trajectories of different parametrisations (a DDIM path in $x$-space includes its $\sqrt{\bar\alpha}$ rescaling), so this refutation is specific to the measure we registered.

\textbf{Heun at equal evaluations (exploratory).} FM-Heun has the lowest SW in Table~\ref{tab:sw} among the systems without a second training stage at every $K$ from 1 to 16, but it uses $2K$ network evaluations, so it must be compared with the $2K$ columns of the other samplers. These comparisons are between seed means of Table~\ref{tab:sw} at different step counts; no paired test is reported for them. At 8 evaluations Heun ($K{=}4$) has lower SW than FM-Euler ($K{=}8$) on two moons (\rhval{main/flow-matching-heun/two_moons/sw_k4/mean:3} against \rhval{main/flow-matching-euler/two_moons/sw_k8/mean:3}) and the checkerboard (\rhval{main/flow-matching-heun/checkerboard/sw_k4/mean:3} against \rhval{main/flow-matching-euler/checkerboard/sw_k8/mean:3}), and by a small margin on eight Gaussians (\rhval{main/flow-matching-heun/eight_gaussians/sw_k4/mean:3} against \rhval{main/flow-matching-euler/eight_gaussians/sw_k8/mean:3}); its mean SW is also below that of DDIM at 8 evaluations on all three tasks, but on eight Gaussians the DDIM seed spread is large (\rhval{main/ddim/eight_gaussians/sw_k8/mean:3} $\pm$ \rhval{main/ddim/eight_gaussians/sw_k8/std:3}) and we do not claim a reliable difference there. The advantage disappears at low budgets: at 4 evaluations Heun is worse than Euler on eight Gaussians (\rhval{main/flow-matching-heun/eight_gaussians/sw_k2/mean:3} against \rhval{main/flow-matching-euler/eight_gaussians/sw_k4/mean:3}), and at 2 evaluations it is worse on all three tasks. From 8 evaluations on, a second-order solver on the unchanged flow model thus closes most of the gap to Reflow-1 (\rhval{main/flow-matching-heun/two_moons/sw_k4/mean:3} against \rhval{main/reflow-1-euler/two_moons/sw_k8/mean:3} for Reflow-1 at $K{=}8$ on two moons) without reflow's extra training. We ran no second-order solver for the diffusion model, so this is not a statement about flows against diffusion.

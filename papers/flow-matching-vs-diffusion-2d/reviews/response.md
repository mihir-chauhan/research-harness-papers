# Response to audit (fix round)

No experiment was re-run and no registry row was added or changed in this round (127 rows, as audited). All changes are to
text, table/figure generation from `results/runs.jsonl`, and documentation. Everything is regenerated by
`sh experiments/make_tables.sh` (new; documents the previously undocumented `rh table` -> postprocess -> analyze chain).

1. (major) Introduction gap statement / research.yaml gap. Fixed. The claim that the compared methods' "networks, schedules
   and samplers differ" is removed. I re-read both papers: Lipman et al. state that all compared models use the same
   architecture and hyper-parameters and report NLL, FID and adaptive-solver NFE on CIFAR-10 and ImageNet; Liu et al. report
   FID and recall of one-step rectified flows on CIFAR-10. The introduction now says exactly that, says "we do not question
   that evidence", and motivates the study as a small controlled 2D replication that adds held-out SW/MMD, a 1-to-100 step
   sweep, a DDIM sampler baseline and seed variance. `research.yaml` (gap) and `literature/landscape.md` were changed the same way.
2. (minor) H2, DDIM spread "five times that of FM". Fixed. The statement is restricted to eight Gaussians; the K=100 gap is
   now reported per task with a paired test (new exploratory row "SW@100 FM-DDIM" in Table III: p = 0.086, 0.048, <0.001):
   not significant on eight Gaussians, marginal on two moons, consistent on the checkerboard.
3. (minor) Conclusion on H2. Fixed. Conclusion and abstract now say the outcome is mixed: the ratio shrinks on eight
   Gaussians, grows on the checkerboard and changes side on two moons.
4. (minor) Ancestral vs DDIM, MMD at K=4 on eight Gaussians. Fixed. Now described as within seed noise (gap 0.0087, about
   one DDIM seed std), together with the SW comparison; the supporting paired tests are rows "SW@4 Anc.-DDIM" and
   "MMD@4 Anc.-DDIM" of Table III.
5. (minor) "at most 0.001". Fixed: "at most 0.0012".
6. (minor) FM-Heun never discussed. Fixed. New results paragraph "Heun at equal evaluations (exploratory)" with paired tests
   at 8, 4 and 2 network evaluations (Table III rows "SW Heun@K - Euler@2K", "SW Heun@4 - DDIM@8"), computed from the
   existing runs. It is stated to be exploratory (not registered), that Heun is worse at 2 evaluations and on eight Gaussians
   at 4, and that no second-order solver was run for the diffusion model (also added to the limitations). Abstract,
   conclusion, contributions and BRIEF.md mention it.
7. (minor) `rh compare` only for two_moons sw_k4. Fixed both ways. The setup section and RESULTS.md state that all tests are
   computed by `experiments/analyze.py`; `rh compare` CSVs now exist for every task for sw_k1, sw_k4, sw_k100, mmd_k1, mmd_k4
   and straight (`results/tables/compare_main_<metric>_<task>.csv`), and their paired p values equal those of the tests table.
8. (minor) Table labels and marks. Fixed in `method/postprocess_tables.py`: the label is typeset with alpha-bar, the task
   name is "Checkerboard", and the best/second marks are removed from the two-row ablation blocks. Values are unchanged
   (diffed against the previous tables).

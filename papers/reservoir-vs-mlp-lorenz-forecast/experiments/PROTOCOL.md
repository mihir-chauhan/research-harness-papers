# Protocol
Tasks: lorenz63 (D=3, dt=0.02, Lyapunov 0.905), lorenz96 (D=10, F=8, dt=0.05, Lyapunov 1.158). Data: three independent trajectories per seed (train n_train+1 samples, default 5000; test 20 segments; reference 15000), standardised by training statistics. Seeds: 0-4 for group main (systems, true-system reference, component ablations), 0-2 for sweeps; validation seed 100 used only for tuning and for fixing the climate-run length.
Metrics: vpt (first RMS standardised error > 0.4, capped at 15 Lyapunov times, mean over 20 test ICs), vpt_min, clim_w1 / clim_ok / blowup / clim_w1_max (20 free runs of 10000 steps vs reference), fit_seconds.
Tuning: staged grids in method/tune.py on seed 100, mean VPT over 10 ICs, n_train=5000 (ESN 27 cells; MLP 9 + 9; GRU 5 + 6). The tuned optimiser setting of MLP/GRU is used at every n_train.
Groups: main (ESN, MLP delay, GRU, True system, ESN without squared features, GRU without input noise; 2 tasks x 5 seeds); sweep_rho (ESN rho in {0.1,0.7,1.0,1.4,2.0}); sweep_size (ESN N in {100,250,1000,2000}); sweep_ntrain (all systems, n_train in {500,2000,10000}); sweep_steps (MLP steps in {4000,8000,16000,32000}, GRU steps in {3000,6000,12000}, without the tuned value). Sweeps use seeds 0-2 and take their tuned default point from the group-main runs of those seeds.
Job list: experiments/make_jobs.py -> experiments/jobs_main.json, run by experiments/launch.py (two lanes); tuning: experiments/jobs_tuning.json. Tables and figures: analysis/build.sh.
Hardware: shared Apple-silicon CPU machine, 2 threads per process, two processes at a time, no GPU; each run < 6 min.

Registry note: results/runs.jsonl is append-only. The rows of the first version of this study (groups main, abl_components, sweep_*, tuning) were produced by code with a non-deterministic ESN eigensolver, an untuned optimiser budget and a 5-rollout climate metric; after the audit they were retired with `rh supersede` and every group was rerun. Ten failed rows of that first version (a launch-script quoting bug) also remain. The old tuning files are in experiments/old/.
Second fix round: the two Lyapunov runs had been logged from an uncommitted working tree, so they were superseded and rerun unchanged at a commit that contains `method/lyap.py` (identical values). Five main runs were repeated as a determinism check (group `repro`, kind sanity: ESN seed 3 on both tasks, MLP seed 0 on both tasks, GRU seed 0 on Lorenz-63); all metrics are identical to the registered main rows. The first-version tuning rows (group `tuning`) are superseded; group `tuning2` holds the tuning used.
Compute actually used by the live rows: about 100 minutes of process time (tuning2 20, main 29, sweeps 47, lyapunov 3, repro 3), i.e. about 50 minutes of wall-clock at two processes; the superseded first version cost about 55 more. This exceeds the nominal 30-minute budget (decision recorded with `rh decide`).
Paper tables: main_systems, tests, sweep_ntrain, sweep_steps, main_ablation, sweep_rho, sweep_size; figures: sweep_ntrain_fig, sweep_steps_fig. `sweep_ntrain_clim.tex`, `main_vpt_fig.pdf`, `sweep_rho_fig.pdf` and `sweep_size_fig.pdf` are generated but not included in the paper (page limit).

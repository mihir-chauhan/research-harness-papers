\subsection{Task and demonstrations}
A point mass has position $p_t\in\mathbb R^2$ and moves by $p_{t+1}=p_t+\mathrm{clip}(a_t)$, where $\mathrm{clip}$ limits $\|a_t\|$ to 0.05. It starts at $p_0\sim U([-j,j]^2)$ and must reach the goal $(1,0)$ within 0.05 in at most 40 steps, without entering the disc of radius 0.15 centred at $(0.5,0)$ (collision is tested at four points along each step). The scripted expert picks the upper ($y>0$) mode with probability $q$ (else lower), samples a waypoint $w=(0.5,\pm0.35)+\mathcal N(0,0.02^2I)$, drives to $w$ and then to the goal with $a_t=\mathrm{clip}(\text{target}-p_t)+\mathcal N(0,0.003^2 I)$. The policy observes only $p_t$ (the mode is latent). We use three tasks: \texttt{bimodal50} ($q=0.5$), \texttt{bimodal80} ($q=0.8$) and \texttt{unimodal} ($q=1$, a control). Because the observation is the position only, near the start state the demonstrated action distribution is a mixture of two incompatible directions.

\subsection{Heads}
All heads map the (standardised) observation $o$ to an action chunk $x\in\mathbb R^{2K}$ ($K=1$ unless stated); all $K$ actions of the chunk are executed open loop before replanning.

\textbf{MSE-MLP (reimplemented BC).} $\hat x=f_\theta(o)$, $\mathcal L=\|f_\theta(o)-x\|^2$; execution is deterministic.

\textbf{Gaussian-mixture head (reimplemented MDN-style head).} $f_\theta(o)$ outputs weights $\pi_m$, means $\mu_m$ and diagonal log-scales $\log\sigma_m$ for $M$ components (clamped to $[-6,2]$): $p_\theta(x|o)=\sum_{m=1}^M\pi_m\,\mathcal N(x;\mu_m,\mathrm{diag}\,\sigma_m^2)$, trained with $\mathcal L=-\log p_\theta(x|o)$. At test time we sample a component, then sample from it. Main runs use $M=2$.

\textbf{DDPM head (reimplemented Diffusion-Policy-style head).} A network $\epsilon_\theta(o,x^k,k)$ predicts the noise added to the chunk. With a cosine schedule~\cite{nichol2021improved} $\bar\alpha_k$, $\beta_k$, $\alpha_k=1-\beta_k$: $x^k=\sqrt{\bar\alpha_k}x+\sqrt{1-\bar\alpha_k}\,\epsilon$, $\mathcal L=\mathbb E_{k,\epsilon}\|\epsilon-\epsilon_\theta(o,x^k,k)\|^2$ with $k$ uniform on $\{0,\dots,N-1\}$, $N=50$. Sampling follows the ancestral DDPM update~\cite{ho2020denoising}: $x^{k-1}=\frac{1}{\sqrt{\alpha_k}}\big(x^k-\frac{\beta_k}{\sqrt{1-\bar\alpha_k}}\epsilon_\theta\big)+\tilde\sigma_k z$, $\tilde\sigma_k^2=\beta_k\frac{1-\bar\alpha_{k-1}}{1-\bar\alpha_k}$, starting from $x^N\sim\mathcal N(0,I)$ (no noise at the last step). The network is an MLP on $[o,x^k,\text{sinusoidal embedding of }k]$.

\subsection{Metrics}
Over 200 closed-loop rollouts: \emph{success} (goal reached without collision), \emph{collision}, \emph{upper/lower share} (fraction of all rollouts that succeeded having crossed $x=0.5$ with $y>0$ / $y<0$), \emph{upper\_frac} $=U/(U+L)$ among successes, and \emph{mode\_balance} $=1-|U-L|/(U+L)$ (0 if no successes). Mode balance is only the right target on \texttt{bimodal50}; on \texttt{bimodal80} the target of upper\_frac is the demonstration share 0.8. Rollouts that neither succeed nor collide time out.
